# Germany / EU and US recipient adaptation

Verification date: 2026-10-02. Scope: founder-led individual first touch and follow-up through email and social DMs. This is general risk research and a writing framework; it does not authorize contact or determine legality of a particular message.

## Findings that change drafting

1. **Purpose comes before channel conclusions.** [UWG section 2(1)(2)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/uwg_2004/__2.html) defines business conduct through its objective connection to goods, services, and contracts. [FTC guidance](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/can-spam-act-compliance-guide-business) classifies email by primary purpose. A civic exchange, editorial tip, or genuine research request needs its own assessment. Calling a product free, nonprofit, or a feedback experiment is insufficient evidence of an exemption. Do not automatically forbid every genuine noncommercial message either.
2. **German and US commercial email have different starting rules.** [UWG section 7](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/uwg_2004/__7.html) requires prior express consent for promotional electronic mail, including B2B, subject to a narrow customer exception with cumulative conditions. [CAN-SPAM](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/can-spam-act-compliance-guide-business) generally regulates US commercial email through truthful identification and an opt-out framework. A public address, relevant role, or LinkedIn connection alone does not establish German marketing-email consent.
3. **Recipient country cannot erase sender duties.** [GDPR Article 3(1)](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2016/679/oj) links scope to EU establishment activities. [Articles 14 and 21](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2016/679/oj) address third-party data transparency and objection rights. Check the German sender as well as the US recipient. This research does not determine all cross-border conflict-of-law questions.

## What the research does not establish

- No verified universal German-versus-American wording, follow-up interval, persuasion formula, or reply-rate uplift. Imported NotebookLM assertions about national personality, fixed day 0/2/5/9 cadence, and percentage increases are not promoted.
- No case-specific ruling that every civic/editorial/research message falls inside or outside advertising law. Analyze purpose and commercial connection honestly; retain platform and data duties where applicable.
- No conclusion that email legislation automatically applies identically to every social DM, or that a DM is exempt. Platform rules and exact channel classification remain separate.
- No complete state/sector-law survey, cross-border conflict-of-law analysis, or national implementation survey outside Germany. English-language outreach is not synonymous with US outreach.

## Operational recommendations

Keep one recipient brief that distinguishes legal facts from writing hypotheses. Reuse known context, resolve only material missing facts, and continue independent research while the affected channel is unresolved. Do not turn this into a permission questionnaire for every message.

For DE recipients with little style evidence, test a factual reason and concrete question. For US recipients with little style evidence, test early relevance and one clear next step in plain English. Both are deliberately unproven defaults. Individual language, role, relationship, and channel evidence override them; follow-up depends on the recipient's actual reply or an honest new reason, never nationality.

Reference these market files from first-touch, recipient research, and follow-up skills when sender/recipient jurisdiction or language adaptation matters. Compare real outcomes only after recording comparable project, purpose, role, relationship, channel, and ask; preserve raw counts and sample limits. The objective is useful replies and agreed progress, not polite response inflation.
