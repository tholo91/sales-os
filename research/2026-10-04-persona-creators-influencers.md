# Creators and influencers: what gets an unpaid civic yes

Builds on `knowledge/strategies/recipient-context.md` (creator row), `knowledge/channels/instagram.md` (comment boundary), `warm-contact-comment` skill and `workspace/projects/brief-nach-berlin/instagram-start.md`. Those cover the comment etiquette and the "voluntary vs paid" distinction. This note adds the delivery mechanics, the creator's own cost/benefit, a concrete kit, the disclosure answer for an unpaid civic share, and the route from "yes" to a post date.

## 1. Findings that change drafting (ranked)

1. **On Instagram the first DM to a non-follower is one text-only shot, and you cannot follow up in DM.** Since August 2023, a sender can send only one message request until the recipient accepts, and it may contain text only: no screen recording, image or voice note. *(verified-source: [TechCrunch 2023-08-03](https://techcrunch.com/2023/08/03/instagrams-new-feature-protects-users-from-unwanted-images-and-videos-in-dms/) reporting Instagram's change.)* Drafting rule: the first IG DM must stand alone (who, why them, the ask, a reason to reply) in under ~80 words. The kit only goes after they accept. A "nudge" in DM is impossible until then, so the follow-up must be planned for a different place (see section 4).
2. **Creators sort requests by sender type and follower count, so a small, empty sender account gets sorted to the bottom.** Since November 2024 creator accounts can filter requests by verified accounts, businesses, creators and subscribers, and sort by follower count; story replies have their own filter. *(verified-source: [Engadget 2024-11-01](https://www.engadget.com/social-media/instagram-reorganizes-message-requests-for-creators-191802121.html).)* Practitioners agree that the strongest way into the main inbox is a thread the creator started or answered (comment reply, story reply). Identical openers, bursts of messages and link-first messages get filtered or ignored. *(practitioner-consensus: [Inro 2026-07-22](https://www.inro.social/blog/instagram-dm-deliverability), a vendor that sells DM automation. It cites Meta only for the basic rule that non-followers land in Requests; the four ranking factors are its own observation, not Meta documentation.)* Drafting rule: warm up publicly first (1–2 substantive comments or one story reply over 1–2 weeks). Send from a profile that already explains BnB: the pinned posts from `instagram-start.md` should be live before the first creator DM. Never put a link in the first DM.
3. **An unpaid civic share needs no "Werbung" label, but only if it is genuinely unpaid and free of strings attached. Thomas must keep it that way and say so in writing.** The Medienanstalten matrix (June 2024) says posts made "aus eigener Motivation ohne kommerziellen Anreiz Dritter" need no label (row C). It names invitations to events, travel costs and gifted products as possible consideration. It explicitly warns against "sicherheitshalber" labels such as "unbezahlte Werbung". *(verified-source: [Leitfaden Werbekennzeichnung, Stand Juni 2024](https://www.lmsaar.de/sites/default/files/2024-06/Leitfaden%20zur%20Werbekennzeichnung%20in%20den%20Online_Medien_Matrix%202024.pdf).)* Under § 5a Abs. 4 UWG an act benefiting another enterprise has no commercial purpose without payment or similar consideration. However, receiving consideration is presumed unless the creator can credibly show otherwise. *(verified-source: [§ 5a UWG](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/uwg_2004/__5a.html).)* Drafting rule: every creator ask states "kein Geld, keine Gegenleistung, keine Textvorgaben". The written thread is the creator's proof. No merch, tickets, dinners or "I'll promote you back" deals.
4. **Paying a creator would turn a safe civic share into a regulated, partly prohibited political ad. Keep the creator lane unpaid and organic.** The Leitfaden row L marks paid "Politische Werbung" as "Nicht erlaubt" in video and audio, and for image/text it requires a note on whose behalf it was made ("finanziert durch"). § 8 Abs. 9 MStV bans political/ideological advertising but excludes "unentgeltliche Beiträge im Dienst der Öffentlichkeit". The EU political-advertising regulation 2024/900 has applied since 10 October 2025 to paid messages designed to influence a legislative process or voting behaviour. It excludes political opinions expressed in a personal capacity. Meta stopped political, electoral and social-issue ads in the EU from October 2025, so boosting a creator's BnB Reel is likely blocked anyway. *(verified-source: Leitfaden; [MStV text via bayern.landtag](https://www.bayern.landtag.de/www/ElanTextAblage_WP18/Drucksachen/Basisdrucksachen/0000005000/0000005098.pdf); [EUR-Lex 2024/900](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX:32024R0900); [MVFP on 10.10.2025](https://www.mvfp.de/nachricht/artikel/neue-eu-verordnung-ueber-transparenz-politischer-werbung-tritt-am-10-oktober-2025-in-kraft); [Al Jazeera 2025-07-25](https://www.aljazeera.com/economy/2025/7/25/meta-to-suspend-political-advertising-in-the-eu-as-transparency-law-looms). Whether a non-partisan letter tool counts as "political" advertising is an open legal question, so treat it as risk guidance and not a legal conclusion.)*
5. **The creator's payment is content value. Fit and creative freedom matter; over-scripting is a common deal-killer.** CreatorIQ (2025-07-09, State of Creator Marketing 2024–2025, sample size not stated) reports that 84 % of creators name product quality as the No. 1 reason to work with a brand and 99 % care about creative control. It does **not** compare these factors with reach. *[Review: the earlier wording "values alignment over reach" overstated the source.]* NeoReach and Viral Nation list misalignment, restrictions and scripting as reasons to decline. *(vendor surveys: [CreatorIQ](https://www.creatoriq.com/blog/what-creators-want-from-brand-partnerships-creatoriq-connect), [NeoReach](https://neoreach.com/creators-keep-walking-away-from-your-brand-deals/), [Viral Nation](https://www.viralnation.com/blog/what-creators-want-from-brands); paid commercial deals, not unpaid civic asks. NeoReach and Viral Nation were not re-checked in review.)* For BnB, "product quality" means: the tool must work smoothly on a phone in front of the creator's eyes. German NGO practitioners report that cause-aligned creators do support without budget when the ask is honest and the mutual benefit is spelled out. *(practitioner-consensus: [Fundraiser-Magazin](https://fundraiser-magazin.de/ngos-und-influencer-so-gelingt-die-zusammenarbeit), [Kolsquare](https://www.kolsquare.com/de/blog/influencer-marketingkampagnen-fur-ngos-verantwortungsbewusst-fur-ein-besseres-morgen).)* Drafting rule: offer a ready angle that fits their format, then let them say it their way. Do not send a caption to copy.
6. **For young audiences, address the viewer directly and drop the self-praise.** Bertelsmann's September 2025 study combined ~31,000 analysed political short videos, a representative survey and a selection experiment. In the experiment, direct address of the viewer raised the chance of watching on by 17 %, self-praise lowered it by 24 % and dancing by 45 %. Respondents most wanted honesty and simple, clear language. 60 % of young people follow political influencers. *(verified-source: [Bertelsmann Stiftung, "How to Sell Democracy Online (Fast)", 2025-09](https://www.bertelsmann-stiftung.de/fileadmin/files/user_upload/dpz_how_to_sell_democracy_online__fast__NEU.pdf). The experiment used politicians' and influencers' clips, so transferring it to a BnB creator kit is inference.)* *(Review re-check: figures confirmed in the PDF, chapter "Jugendbezug und direkte Ansprache statt Eigenlob". Self-praise there means a sender stressing their own skills, values or successes. Across all ~31,000 videos, direct address had no effect on views; the +17 % applies only in the experiment with young people.)* Kit rule: the angle is "Du ärgerst dich über X? So landet das bei der zuständigen Stelle", not "tolles Tool". Do not promise "in 5 Minuten" until Thomas has timed it [PRUEFEN].
7. **The biggest German politics explainers are public-broadcaster journalism. Pitch them editorially, never as a "Kooperation".** MrWissen2go (ZDF) and DIE DA OBEN! (ARD) are funk formats producing hosted "Infopinion" journalism with labelled host opinions and no dominant party line. *(verified-source: [OBS-Arbeitspapier 82, Oktober 2025](https://www.otto-brenner-stiftung.de/fileadmin/user_upload/was_wir_tun/publikationen/die_politik_erklaerer/AP82_Die_Politik_Erklaerer_innen_Kurzfassung_WEB.pdf).)* Route these through `knowledge/strategies/editorial-pitch.md`: a topic or case for their audience, not "please share my tool". *(The OBS paper confirms only the format type. That they "cannot plug a third-party offer" is an inference from Pressekodex Ziffer 7 and public-broadcast advertising rules; the OBS paper says nothing about cooperations.)*
8. **Political influencer content is often sharp and enemy-framed. Vet the creator for reputational fit before asking.** A study by the NRW media authority (LfM NRW) analysed 1,000 posts by 98 high-reach accounts around the 2025 federal election. In 59 % of posts, opinion and information could not be clearly separated, and 28 % built a clear enemy image. *(verified-source: [LfM NRW, 2025-10-20](https://www.medienanstalt-nrw.de/presse/pressemitteilungen/pressemitteilungen-2025/september/political-influencer-zwischen-journalismus-und-algorithmus.html).)* Rule: check their last ~10 posts. If the post would present BnB as a weapon against a party or group, skip the creator or ask for the neutral frame ("egal, wo du stehst").
9. **Making the share easy is established practice for civic tools.** The bpb offered ready Wahl-O-Mat sharepics in exact Instagram feed, story, Facebook and X sizes (published 2025-01-06). *(verified-source: [bpb Sharepics](https://bpb.de/themen/wahl-o-mat/558102/social-media-sharepics).)* Instagram link stickers are open to all accounts except new or repeatedly violating ones, with one link per story. *(verified-source: [Instagram 2021-10-27](https://about.instagram.com/blog/announcements/expanding-sharing-links-in-stories-to-everyone).)* Collab posts list up to five co-authors, show on every collaborator's profile, stay hidden until accepted and can be added after posting. *(practitioner-consensus: [Sked Social](https://skedsocial.com/blog/instagram-collaboration-feature), [Sprout Social](https://sproutsocial.com/insights/instagram-collab-post/); not checked against Meta help. Review: Sked wording confirmed.)* *Review caveat:* sharepics are a supporting asset only. Bertelsmann found that infographics lower reach and selfie-mode videos raise it (see section 8, point 2), so the main ask is the creator talking in their own format.
10. **Smaller creators answer DMs; bigger ones expect the channel named in their bio.** Practitioner guidance is consistent: use the contact preference in the bio first. DMs work for nano/micro creators, and from roughly 50k followers email or management is the norm. *[Review: removed vendor reply-rate percentages for DM vs email. They were unverified, measured paid brand deals, and would anchor expectations wrongly.]* *(practitioner-consensus: [Brandwatch](https://www.brandwatch.com/blog/influencer-outreach/), [influencerfee.com](https://influencerfee.com/blog/influencer-outreach-strategy/).)* Note that writing to a published "Kooperationsanfragen" email is still email under UWG § 7 (section 6).

## 2. Sub-persona playbooks

Shared for all six:

- **Lead:** a dated commitment. Examples: a story or Reel on a named day, a Collab post, a pinned link, or a campaign page they will use. "Klingt spannend" is not a lead.
- **Kill factors:**
  - a link-first or copy-paste message
  - "Kooperation?" with no angle
  - asking them to script for you
  - a vague size of ask
  - anything that smells like a party campaign or an AI letter flood
  - hidden strings (perks)

**(a) Politics/explainer creators on Instagram/TikTok (independent, 10k–300k)**

- **Flooded with:** brand deals, PR mail from parties and NGOs, hate DMs.
- **What they value:** fresh, verifiable material for a video they already plan; audience usefulness; not looking bought.
- **Reply trigger:** a ready, concrete viewer action that fits a topic they just covered ("deine Zuschauer fragen in den Kommentaren 'und was kann ich tun?'"). Quote the actual comment if visible.
- **Channel:**
  - Under ~50k: story reply or comment → one DM.
  - Above ~50k: the bio email or management.
- **Structure:** observed signal (their video plus their audience's question) → one sentence of BnB → ready angle → ask → no-strings note.
- **Smallest ask:** "Darf ich dir ein 15-Sekunden-Video und ein Beispiel zu deinem Thema schicken?" Permission to send the kit comes before any share request.

**(b) YouTube explainer formats (funk/ARD/ZDF, independent explainers)**

- **Flooded with:** topic suggestions and PR.
- **What they value:**
  - a story with a case or data
  - accuracy
  - editorial independence

  Public-broadcast formats cannot plug a third-party offer.
- **Channel:** editorial email or contact form, or the "Themenvorschlag" route in the description or imprint. For independents, the business email under "Kanalinfo".
- **Structure:** follow the editorial pitch (subject = possible headline; one news angle; one verifiable fact).
- **Smallest ask:** "Ist das ein Thema für euch?" or "Darf ich Zahlen/Beispiele schicken?"
- **Lead:** research call, data request, or a mention as a "what you can do" resource in the description. Never ask for a "share".

**(c) LinkedIn creators on politics, society or social impact**

- **Flooded with:** "pick your brain" asks, sales DMs, engagement-pod invites.
- **What they value:** a post-worthy observation or data point they can comment on in their own voice; being early on a civic story; reciprocity in comments.
- **Channel:** comment substantively on 1–2 posts → connect without a note or with a one-line reason → DM. Use the existing Thomas LinkedIn voice samples.
- **Structure:** their post → Thomas's concrete observation from BnB → offer of material → ask.
- **Smallest ask:** "Interessiert dich der Datenpunkt für einen Post?" or a 15-minute exchange.
- **Lead:** a post or comment thread naming BnB, an intro, or a repost of Thomas's post with their take.

**(d) Local and micro creators (Bremen city, neighbourhood, food or culture accounts, 2k–50k)**

- **Flooded with:** gastro and event comps.
- **What they value:**
  - local relevance (Beirat, Bürgerschaft, Schlaglöcher, ÖPNV, Kita)
  - meeting the founder in person
  - a local story ("Bremer baut Tool…")
- **Reply rates:** the most likely to reply and to meet. Small reach, but a local audience that trusts them.
- **Channel:** comment and story reply, then DM; or meet at a local event.
- **Structure:** a local issue they posted about → offer an example letter for exactly that issue → ask.
- **Smallest ask:** "Soll ich dir zeigen, wie ein Brief zu [ihr Thema] aussehen würde?" A coffee in Bremen is a fine second step.
- **Lead:** a story with link sticker, or a joint Reel ("Wir schreiben dem Beirat wegen…"). [PRUEFEN: whether Bremen Land/Kommune routing is live before promising local recipients.]

**(e) Satire and comedy creators**

- **Flooded with:** brand deals that want to be funny on command.
- **What they value:** material, absurdity, freedom to make fun of you too.
- **Reply trigger:** a genuinely absurd but true detail (e.g. how hard it is to find who is responsible for one bus stop), offered as raw material and not as a joke to perform.
- **Channel:**
  - Small: DM.
  - Big TV-linked satire: editorial pitch.
- **Smallest ask:** "Falls du mal was zu [Thema] machst: hier ist ein absurdes Detail, frei zur Verwendung."
- **Kill factor:** sending them a script or asking them to "make BnB go viral".
- **Lead:** a sketch or skit that names BnB as "the thing that actually exists".

**(f) Activist creators (climate, social justice, housing)**

- **Flooded with:** campaign asks and petitions.
- **What they value:** effective action their community can take, not another petition; credibility; avoiding astroturf and AI-spam accusations.
- **Risk to BnB:** looking like one camp's tool. **Risk to them:** being seen pushing an "AI letter generator".
- **Rules:**
  - Frame BnB as personal letters: AI drafts, the person edits, handwrites and sends.
  - Stress no mass mailing.
  - Say openly that you offer the same tool to everyone.
  - Let them choose the issue.
  - Do not co-brand BnB with their campaign slogan.
- **Best fit:** the campaign page flow (prefilled issue page) if live. [PRUEFEN: campaign flow live and creator-usable.]
- **Smallest ask:** "Wäre ein vorbefüllter Briefentwurf zu [ihr Thema] für deine Community nützlich?"
- **Lead:** they use a campaign page in a dated post or action week.

**The creator kit (send only after a yes to "darf ich schicken")**

*Review: trimmed to three core items. Thomas is solo and has said he gets too much output, so a six-part kit per creator would not get built. Optional items only on request.*

Core (always):

1. **One example letter on the creator's own topic**, made with BnB. This is the proof, and it is the thing the first DM offered.
2. **A 15-second vertical screen recording**: issue → postcode → draft. Captions on, no music that needs a licence. The creator can use it as B-roll behind their own talking head.
3. **One link**: `?utm_source=instagram&utm_medium=creator&utm_campaign=<handle>` or their own campaign page [PRUEFEN: whether BnB analytics can actually show UTM results per creator; campaign flow live and creator-usable].

In the same message, two lines:

- Facts: free, non-partisan, solo project supported by WE AID gGmbH [PRUEFEN: exact wording]. You write and send the letter yourself; nothing goes out automatically. [PRUEFEN: current counter; brief-nach-berlin.de showed "3.023 Briefe geschrieben" on 2026-10-04 while the founder-reported figure was ~1,800 on 2026-08-21; clarify drafts vs mailed letters before quoting any number.]
- The no-strings sentence (see the kit message draft E in section 3).

Optional on request: three niche issue examples, a 1080×1920 story frame, a privacy one-liner. No caption to copy.

## 4. Follow-up cadence

- **Touch 0 (warm-up, not outreach):** 1–2 substantive comments or one story reply over 7–14 days, no link and no pitch (`warm-contact-comment`).
- **Touch 1:** the first message via the creator's stated channel (bio email/management beats DM above ~50k).
- **Touch 2 (the single unanswered nudge, 5–8 days later), only with new value:**
  - a finished example letter for the issue in their latest post
  - a topical hook (debate, vote or local decision this week)
  - a ready story frame
- **Where touch 2 goes on Instagram:**
  - If the IG request is unaccepted, you cannot DM again. Use the bio business email, if listed for inquiries, as the one nudge and reference the DM in one line.
  - Otherwise leave one more genuine comment and stop.
  - Do not send both. Moving channels to get around a clear "no" is never OK. Moving to the creator's own stated inquiry channel once, because the DM sits unread in Requests, is the proposed exception for the lead to accept or reject. [ENTSCHEIDUNG: Thomas/lead. Review recommendation: allow it only if the bio explicitly names the email for inquiries, and never for nano creators who list no email.]
- **Stop** after touch 2. Re-open only on a genuinely new trigger, such as a new video on a topic BnB covers, at most once per quarter, and treat it as a new first touch.
- **After a yes:**
  - Within 24 h: send the kit.
  - Ask when their next video on the topic is planned. Only if they have no plan, offer one topical date ("Am [Datum] wird im Bundestag/Beirat über [Thema] entschieden, wäre das ein Anlass?"). *Review: pushing two date options onto someone doing an unpaid favour reads like a sales close.*
  - Default: their own format, in their words. Offer a Collab post only if they suggest it. *Review: a Collab post that Thomas scripts or cuts moves the post away from "aus eigener Motivation" (Leitfaden 1.2) toward a cooperation, and shows the creator on BnB's profile, which could look like reciprocal promotion. [PRUEFEN: legal view before using Collab posts with creators.]*
  - 2 days before: send one line asking whether anything is missing.
  - On post day: reshare, answer questions if invited, don't flood their comments.
  - 3–7 days after: thank them and share the result (UTM clicks or drafts started, only if measurable [PRUEFEN]). This is the follow-up that earns a second post. *(inference, no source)*

## 6. Legal, platform and ethics risks (risk guidance, not legal advice)

- **Werbekennzeichnung (MStV § 22, DDG § 6, UWG § 5a Abs. 4):**
  - Genuinely unpaid and unconditioned sharing normally needs no label (Leitfaden row C). Avoid "unbezahlte Werbung" labels.
  - Any consideration (money, tickets, travel, merch, reciprocal promo) or agreed conditions turns a post into labelled advertising: "Werbung"/"Anzeige" first word, on every story slide. Paid political advertising in video is prohibited (row L).
  - The presumption of consideration under § 5a Abs. 4 S. 3 UWG makes a written no-strings statement useful to the creator.
- **Thomas's own Reels:** own products are fine without a label when the founder relationship is clear (row D). For the faceless format, say in the bio and caption that it is Thomas's project.
- **TikTok:** check the content-disclosure toggle rules in `knowledge/channels/tiktok.md` for Thomas's own account. Creators sharing without consideration are, by inference, not branded content.
- **EU 2024/900 and Meta:**
  - No paid creator posts on issues tied to legislation or votes, and no boosting.
  - Meta blocks social-issue ads in the EU since October 2025.
  - Personal-capacity opinions are outside the regulation.
- **UWG § 7 (email):** a published business email is not express consent to advertising. Keep the email an individual, non-commercial inquiry with one ask and one nudge maximum. Never put creators on a newsletter (`knowledge/channels/email.md`).
- **Pressekodex Ziffer 7 and public broadcast:** funk/ARD/ZDF hosts must keep editorial separation. Never offer anything, and never ask for a "share".
- **DSGVO:**
  - Keep creator notes minimal in `workspace/`.
  - No scraping or bulk enrichment.
  - UTM links carry no personal data; do not add per-person tracking IDs beyond the creator handle.
  - Be ready to answer Art. 14/21 requests.
- **Platform rules:** Instagram prohibits repetitive comments and repeated commercial contact without consent. Write every comment and DM individually and post them manually.
- **Ethics:**
  - non-partisan framing
  - no invented quotes, numbers or impact
  - no pressure on activists to brand BnB as theirs

## 8. Added in review: three points that should move replies and leads

1. **Make the creator's call to action an offline act ("schreib's auf Papier"), never "teilt das" or "kommentiert".** In the Bertelsmann content analysis, 26 % of political short videos ask for online actions such as commenting or sharing, and these have a negative effect on reach. Calls to offline action have a positive effect: demo calls +17 %, election calls +8 %. A handwritten letter is an offline act, so BnB fits the pattern that travels. *(verified-source: Bertelsmann 2025, executive summary, "Calls to Action: lieber offline als online". This is a correlation across ~31,000 videos by politicians and influencers, not an experiment; applying it to BnB creator posts is inference.)* Drafting rule: the angle in the kit and in Thomas's own Reels ends with "Schreib's auf. Ab in den Briefkasten." and never with "Teilt das, damit es viele sehen". When pitching, this is also a reason that serves the creator: it gives their audience a concrete step without the reach penalty of a "share this" ask.
2. **Ask creators to talk in their usual selfie format and use the BnB screen recording as B-roll. Do not lead with sharepics or infographics.** Bertelsmann found that selfie-mode videos have a positive effect on views. Political influencers record almost half their videos that way, politicians only 13 %. Videos with infographics reach fewer people on average and made no impression in the experiment. Young people want videos without technical faults and in simple, honest language; only 17 % want filters or effects. *(verified-source: Bertelsmann 2025, executive summary, "Selfies performen reichweitenstark, Tanzen und Infografiken nicht" and "Technisch ohne Fehler und in einfacher, ehrlicher Sprache".)* This changes the kit (core = screen recording + example letter, sharepic optional) and supports Thomas's own on-camera test variant. For the faceless variant, use the screen recording plus a spoken voice-over rather than text-on-infographic slides (inference).
3. **Pitch "an address instead of an enemy image": BnB gives angry audiences a constructive next step without asking the creator to change tone.** LfM NRW found clear enemy images in 28 % of high-reach political influencer posts, and in 68 % of those cases the targets were institutions and political actors. *(verified-source: [LfM NRW, 2025-10-20](https://www.medienanstalt-nrw.de/presse/pressemitteilungen/pressemitteilungen-2025/september/political-influencer-zwischen-journalismus-und-algorithmus.html).)* Bertelsmann's surveys show young people view verbal attacks critically and reject "über andere herziehen"; in the experiment, attacks had no significant effect on their choice, although attack videos get about 40 % more views across all videos. *(verified-source: Bertelsmann 2025.)* Inference for the first touch: the strongest relevance hook is the creator's own comment section, where people ask "und jetzt?", and the offer is the concrete responsible body for exactly that complaint. It respects the creator's anger and makes BnB the "what you can do" part of their next video. It also shrinks the reputational risk in finding 8, because the post becomes "write to whoever is responsible", not "fight group X".
