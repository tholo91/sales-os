# Findings

1. Optimize for useful replies and next steps relevant to the project, not reply count. Vendor findings can differ because one analysis measures meetings and another measures replies; a refusal also counts as a reply in some datasets.
2. The original studies below concern sales email, in-deal sales communication, or recruiter InMail. None establishes an effective cadence for a civic project's LinkedIn connections, influencers, scientists, or journalists, or a German-versus-American response-rate advantage.
3. The local follow-up skill required a decision first while `core/steps/continue.md` required a draft first. Aligning them prevents an agent from assuming the next action is another message. Existing silence and manual-action safeguards remain.

# Working rules adopted

These are deliberate project guardrails and testable communication choices, not claimed research optima:

- Reuse recipient context through first touch, reply, and follow-up. Separate observed role/relationship from hypothesized benefit and hesitation; do not invent motives or stereotype a nationality.
- Distinguish promised delivery, explicitly invited deferred contact, and a fresh relevant reason. Deliver requested material without another ask; vague busyness or a due task does not establish invitation.
- Stop persuasion after a clear refusal or objection to contact. Silence is an unknown state. Connection acceptance, opens, and clicks establish neither interest nor permission. Never switch channels to get around these boundaries.
- Use the actual reply to choose the next ask. An example request is not creator endorsement, a factual answer is not campaign support, and a journalistic question is not coverage. Buyer politeness does not establish purchase fit.
- Preserve existing maximum-one-unanswered-nudge policy as a relationship guardrail. Read the matching market and channel rules; a country label alone cannot decide legal basis, formality, or follow-up timing.

# Proof and learning boundary

The edit checks can confirm routing/reference consistency and behavioral cases, not higher reply rates. For a founder-authorized manual pilot, choose one project outcome before sending (for example a substantive answer, invited example review, or qualified customer conversation), preserve role, relationship, market, channel, and actual ask, and compare only messages with the same observation window. Record clear refusals, no replies, and useful replies separately. Small non-random samples support qualitative learning, not causal uplift claims or universal DE/US benchmarks. No outreach was sent during this research.
