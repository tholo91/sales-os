# Press pitch benchmarks for founder-led editorial outreach

Research for a civic-tech founder pitching German political, digital, and broadcast desks. Most survey numbers come from vendor studies (US-heavy) read through secondary summaries; the originals were not accessible. Numerical claims are quarantined in `knowledge/evidence/claims-register.md`.

## Findings

1. **Length.** Muck Rack State of Journalism 2026 (via summaries): most journalists want pitches under 200 words and sent one-to-one by email. Propel/EPR/5W response-rate study 2026 (Propel's own 2024 data): pitches under 150 words got about four times the reply rate of pitches over 500 words. Sources: https://umarcomm.umn.edu/blog/2026/04/13/pitching-journalists-muck-racks-state-journalism-2026, https://theprnet.com/news/43828, https://everything-pr.com/epr-research-and-5w-release-the-pr-pitch-response-rate-study-2026. Confidence: low-medium.
2. **Timing.** Muck Rack 2026 summary: most journalists prefer pitches before noon. No reliable source for best weekday. Confidence: low.
3. **Relevance beats flattery.** Muck Rack 2026 and Cision 2025 summaries: the top rejection reason is a pitch outside the journalist's beat. Personalization works when it explains the fit ("Sie schrieben X, dazu gibt es Y"), not as a compliment. Wrong names and too-short notice are documented annoyances (Muck Rack 2025 panel: https://wnanews.com/2025/07/07/muck-rack-panel-discussion-highlights-state-of-journalism-2025-report/). Confidence: medium.
4. **What helps.** Original data, credible sources, a clear reason why now, visuals. Link a press page; do not attach files. Confidence: medium (survey summaries plus inference).
5. **Contact route.** Email is the preferred first channel. No source found on scheduling links; offering a phone number is low-risk (German Medien-Trendmonitor 2017: only 19 % are bothered by phone follow-up; https://www.presseportal.de/pm/6344/3715941). Inference: phone number in the body, scheduling link optional.
6. **Follow-up.** One timely follow-up (Muck Rack 2026 summary). The exact day count is practice, not evidence. German Medien-Trendmonitor 2025 (via secondary source): a majority experiences the volume of irrelevant PR mail as a burden. Confidence: low-medium.
7. **Broadcast and podcasts.** No primary source found for ARD/ZDF/DLF planning desks. Practice guidance: a one-sentence conversation premise, a demonstrable example or affected person, clear availability, and proof of prior interviews. Confidence: low (inference).
8. **AI framing.** Muck Rack 2026: 82 % of journalists use AI themselves (https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/3/19/3259178/0/en/Muck-Rack-s-2026-State-of-Journalism-Report-Finds-82-of-Journalists-Use-AI.html). AI is not a turn-off in itself; say exactly what the AI does and what the person does.
9. **Self-reported numbers.** No editorial guideline found. Practice: state how a number was collected (tool count vs. survey), the period, n, and that it is self-report and not representative.

## Hook accuracy lesson (2026-10-10)

The Bundestag-AI hook was being sharpened in drafts ("digitale", "KI-generierte" Massenpost). The primary source (heise, 23.09.2026) only says "automatisierte Massenpost", and that is one use case, not the main motive. Secondary outlets sharpened it further. Rule: quote the primary source's wording and scope; a journalist will check it.
