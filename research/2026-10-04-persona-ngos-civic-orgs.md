# 1. Findings that change drafting (ranked)

1. **Sell quality per action, not volume. This is the campaigner's own logic.** A US congressional-staff survey reported by Public Citizen found that 90% of staffers called handwritten letters influential and 88% said the same of personalized emails, against 51% for form emails. BnB's NGO page already says "Eine Petition zeigt Breite. Eine Briefkampagne bringt das Anliegen direkt in Wahlkreisbüros." Open with the gap between a Musterbrief or one-click mail action and real constituent letters, not with "AI tool". Evidence: verified-source for the percentages, but US-only and dated. The Public Citizen article (2012) does not state the survey year. "2011" is the researcher's attribution [PRUEFEN]. Never cite it as German evidence. [CMF via Public Citizen](https://www.citizen.org/news/personalized-emails-are-key-to-boosting-grassroots-influence/)
2. **Pre-empt the "KI-Briefflut" fear in the first message or the first reply.** In a field experiment with 32,398 emails to 7,132 US state legislators (2020, GPT-3), offices answered AI-written emails only about 2 percentage points less often than human ones. That is a response-rate result, not a detection test. It suggests AI text does not stand out, but it does not prove offices "cannot tell". It was emails, not handwritten letters. The credibility risk for an NGO is "our supporters sent AI spam". BnB's answer is built into the product: no auto-send, the person edits it, and handwriting plus postage is friction by design. Say this plainly in one sentence. Evidence: plausible, not re-verified (the SAGE page returned 403 on 2026-10-04; figures match the published abstract as remembered by the reviewer). US-only. Do not quote numbers in outreach. [SAGE](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/abs/10.1177/14614448231160526)
3. **Offer a test, not a partnership. Big campaign platforms already decide this way.** Campact's WeAct partner programme first tests a petition in a newsletter and scales only if results are promising. Applicants are judged on a clear goal, a change strategy, community support and timeliness. WeAct publishes its own partner inbox for this, which makes it an expected route, not a cold one. Mirror that: offer one campaign, one channel test (one newsletter segment, one story or one post), a 2-4 week window, a pre-agreed signal, and a stop or continue decision. Evidence: verified-source for WeAct's process. Transferring it to BnB is inference. [WeAct Partnerprogramm](https://weact.campact.de/s/weact-partnerprogramm)
4. **Personal advocacy actions are scarce in NGO programmes, and a ready-made one is a real gift.** M+R Benchmarks 2026 (2025 data, US nonprofits): an average of 50 emails per subscriber per year, of which 31 were fundraising appeals and only 2 were advocacy asks. Advocacy emails got 2.3% clicks and a 1.4% response rate, against 0.59% clicks and 0.05% response for fundraising emails. ECDA's European takeaway: rebalance toward non-money asks. The pitch for comms and campaign staff is "eine Mitmach-Aktion, die ihr nicht selbst bauen müsst". Evidence: verified-source (US benchmark) plus inference for Germany. [M+R email](https://mrbenchmarks.com/email-messaging/), [ECDA](https://www.centerfordigitalaction.eu/post/takeaways-from-m-r-s-benchmarks-study-2026)
5. **Non-partisanship goes in the first lines, because 2025-2026 is a high-anxiety period for gemeinnuetzige political work.** Examples: the CDU/CSU "551 Fragen" Kleine Anfrage (Feb 2025, anniversary statement Feb 2026), a draft annual tax law without the promised clarification on political activity (Allianz statement dated 2026-08-04; the researcher wrote Aug 2025 [PRUEFEN]), and a Berlin Finanzamt challenge to an organisation's status (2026-09-01). The Allianz Rechtssicherheit warns of self-censorship. Write "überparteilich", "jede Person schreibt ihre eigene Sicht" and "an die zuständigen Abgeordneten, unabhängig von der Partei". Never suggest targeting only one party's MPs. Evidence: verified-source on the climate. The effect on replies is inference. [Allianz Rechtssicherheit](https://www.zivilgesellschaft-ist-gemeinnuetzig.de/aktuelles/), [beck-aktuell](https://www.beck-aktuell.de/heute-im-recht/rechtspolitik-gesetzgebung/ngos-gemeinnuetzigkeit-union-551-fragen-2025-05-15)
6. **Have the Datenschutz and AI answers ready before anyone asks.** In a 1,235-person survey (DSEE/ZiviZ with WHU and VMI, fieldwork Apr-Nov 2025), 73% of people engaged in the sector use generative AI. Only 12% of organisations have a central AI initiative and 9% binding guidelines. Comms and PR is the top use area [PRUEFEN: "77%" not confirmed on the DSEE page]. Haus des Stiftens reportedly lists data protection (35%) and quality (27%) among reasons for not using AI [PRUEFEN: not re-checked, no URL in source table]. Expect the request "Unsere Datenschutzbeauftragte muss das sehen". Answer in one line and send the link to /datenschutz and /ki-transparenz. Evidence: verified-source. [DSEE/ZiviZ](https://www.deutsche-stiftung-engagement-und-ehrenamt.de/studienergebnisse/ki-im-gemeinnutzigen-sektor/), [Aktive Bürgerschaft summary](https://www.aktive-buergerschaft.de/befragung-ki-nutzung-in-gemeinnuetzigen-organisationen-meist-individuell/)
7. **Name the data-ownership trade-off honestly. It is the number-one advocacy-tool criterion.** Vendor buyer guides put "the supporter relationship belongs to the organisation" and CRM sync first. BnB gives the NGO no supporter contact data. That is a plus for privacy-minded orgs and a minus for list-building campaigners. Do not hide it. Offer the counterweight: the NGO keeps its own landing page and opt-in, and the campaign page shows the NGO as the visible sender. Whether creators see aggregate counts: [PRUEFEN: Kampagnen-Statistik für Ersteller live?]. Evidence: practitioner-consensus (vendor guides, commercial bias). [Soapbox Engage](https://www.soapboxengage.com/blog/2918-nonprofit-advocacy-software-salesforce-what-to-look-for), [CallHub](https://callhub.io/blog/advocacy/advocacy-software-for-nonprofits/)
8. **For small Vereine and Initiativen, the founder does the work.** Organisations are mostly volunteer-run and report problems recruiting volunteers and board members (ZiviZ-Survey 2023, 12,792 organisations). Time and open-ended commitment are classic barriers [PRUEFEN: NCVO claim has no source URL and is UK data]. The ask must be finite: "Ich setze die Kampagnenseite auf, du prüfst 10 Minuten und gibst frei." BnB's campaign flow already supports this: moderated before publishing, no account, no payment, and a transfer of ownership to the organisation. Evidence: verified-source on capacity. The concierge offer is inference. [ZiviZ-Survey 2023](https://www.ziviz.de/publikationen/ziviz-survey-2023-hauptbericht)
9. **Political-education actors need education framing, not campaign framing.** Under the Beutelsbacher Konsens (no overwhelming students, present controversial issues as controversial, start from the learner's interests), a bpb, Landeszentrale, VHS or teacher contact cannot promote a cause. They can teach "how do I address the responsible level". bpb's HanisauLand (children's platform, roughly 8-14 years) publishes worksheets titled "Schreib deiner/-m Abgeordneten!" (file dated 2026-02, title checked). That is a bridge for school contacts, not for VHS adult education. Evidence: verified-source. [bpb Beutelsbacher Konsens](https://www.bpb.de/die-bpb/ueber-uns/auftrag/51310/beutelsbacher-konsens/), [HanisauLand worksheet](https://www.hanisauland.de/system/files/2026-02/arbeitsblaetter-brief-abgeordnete-gs.pdf)
10. **Cold email to an organisation is a legal grey zone. Default to other routes, but do not treat every email as forbidden.** Corrected after review: BGH "FC Troschenreuth" (I ZR 197/05, 2008) concerned a *commercial* online-game provider emailing a small football club to ask about a paid banner ad on the club's website. The BGH called that commercial inquiry "Werbung". In the same press release it also held that a publicly listed address implies consent to inquiries that fit the recipient's *normal activity*. Unresolved for BnB: whether a free, non-commercial civic tool counts as "Werbung" at all, and whether a campaign offer to an organisation that itself runs letter actions fits "normal activity". Working rule: no info@ blasts and no sequences. One single, purpose-fitting email to a route the org publishes for this (partner inbox, Kooperationen, newsletter submission, Presse for a story) is a judgment call Thomas can make. LinkedIn or Instagram to the named person with a concrete reason stays the default. Evidence: verified-source for the case facts; transfer to BnB is inference, not legal advice. [BGH press release](https://www.bundesgerichtshof.de/SharedDocs/Pressemitteilungen/DE/2008/2008136.html), [UWG § 7](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/uwg_2004/__7.html)
11. **Anchor every touch to a dated campaign moment.** Campaign guides plan around goal, strategy, measures and evaluation, and involve fundraising, press and policy staff early. A tool pitch with no moment gets parked. Use the org's next Anhörung, Haushaltsberatung, Gesetzentwurf or local Ratssitzung (Bremen: Beiräte and Deputationen), or a regional election. Bürgerschaftswahl Bremen is on 2027-05-30 and Landtagswahl Niedersachsen on 2027-09-26 [PRUEFEN: Termine vor Nutzung]. Evidence: practitioner-consensus plus inference. [Wegweiser Bürgergesellschaft](https://www.buergergesellschaft.de/praxishilfen/kampagnen-und-aktionen), [buten un binnen](https://www.butenunbinnen.de/nachrichten/landtagswahl-niedersachsen-terminiert-100.html)
12. **Know the incumbent workaround and name it.** Organisations already run "Schreib deinem Abgeordneten" actions with Musterbrief PDFs (for example Mehr Demokratie BW, VGSD), letter-building kits (Content 4 Future, Jan 2025), and WeAct's contact-your-representative feature. BnB is not "the first". It is the version with postcode routing, a personal draft and a handwritten send. Ask "Wie habt ihr das beim letzten Mal gemacht?" before showing anything. Evidence: verified-source on existence. The positioning is inference. [Mehr Demokratie BW](https://www.mitentscheiden.de/news/jetzt-druck-machen-schreiben-sie-ihren-landtagsabgeordneten), [WfF/Content4Future](https://worforfuture.de/stark-fur-die-demokratie-dein-brief-an-politikerinnen-in-wenigen-klicks/)

## Added in review: three missing levers

13. **Partnerschaften für Demokratie (PfD) are the best-fitting local multiplier and are missing above.** Under the federal programme "Demokratie leben!", each PfD has a Koordinierungs- und Fachstelle run by a civil-society organisation (coordination, advice, Öffentlichkeitsarbeit), a local Bündnis, and a Jugendforum with its own Jugendfonds. Their job is to find participation formats for their Kommune, so "a free, non-partisan letter action for your Jugendforum or next Demokratiekonferenz" fits their mandate. One coordinator reaches many local Vereine. Start with Bremen [PRUEFEN: Bremer PfD-Koordinierungsstelle und Ansprechperson], then use the result as a reference for other PfDs. Smallest ask: "Wäre das was für euer Jugendforum oder eure nächste Demokratiekonferenz?" Evidence: verified-source for the structure; number of PfDs and 2025+ funding status [PRUEFEN]; fit is inference. [Demokratie leben! - Partnerschaften für Demokratie](https://www.demokratie-leben.de/das-programm/ueber-demokratie-leben/partnerschaften-fuer-demokratie)
14. **Make the first message forwardable, because the person you reach rarely decides alone.** Campaign planning involves several departments (Wegweiser Bürgergesellschaft), and small Vereine decide in a Vorstand that is short of people (ZiviZ). Keep the core explanation to two sentences that work unchanged in a Vorstand WhatsApp group or internal Slack. After a positive reply, send a 3-line "zum Weiterleiten" text plus one screenshot, not a deck. This turns "klingt spannend, ich frag mal intern" into a decision instead of silence. Evidence: inference from verified sector structure. [Wegweiser Bürgergesellschaft](https://www.buergergesellschaft.de/praxishilfen/kampagnen-und-aktionen), [ZiviZ-Survey 2023](https://www.ziviz.de/publikationen/ziviz-survey-2023-hauptbericht)
15. **Offer something reportable after the action, without claiming impact.** NGOs must report to members, Vorstand and funders. BnB already asks letter writers later whether their MdB replied (local skill `send-lastcall-batch`, Brief nach Berlin). For a pilot, offer anonymised, opt-in stories ("Abgeordnete:r hat geantwortet") as the NGO's follow-up content, labelled as output, not impact (PHINEO's output/outcome/impact distinction). This gives comms leads a second post and gives campaigners a reason to say yes. Do not promise replies from politicians. Evidence: local product source plus PHINEO terminology; value for reply rates is inference [PRUEFEN: per campaign filterable? consent for sharing stories?]. [PHINEO Kursbuch Wirkung](https://www.phineo.org/uploads/Downloads/PHINEO_Kursbuch_Wirkung_CH.pdf)

# 2. Sub-persona playbooks

Shared defaults for all sub-personas. A "lead" is a dated next commitment, not a friendly reply. Mention impact numbers only with a date and the label "eigene Zählung" [PRUEFEN: aktuelle Zahl]. Use "ich", never "wir". Supported by WE AID gGmbH.

## (a) Campaigners at large NGOs and campaign platforms (type: Campact, Mehr Demokratie, BUND, foodwatch)

- **Flooded with:** tool vendors, agency pitches, petition-platform offers, cooperation requests from small projects, press-list spam.
- **They value:** leverage on a decision-maker, timing, measurable mobilisation, low risk to brand and Gemeinnützigkeit, keeping the supporter relationship, and tests before scale.
- **What makes them reply:** a reference to their *current* campaign and its target, one concrete mechanism ("Briefe aus echten Wahlkreisen an die zuständigen MdB"), and a bounded test they could run without procurement.
- **What kills it:** "KI-Tool für NGOs", feature lists, "Partnerschaft" with no scope, a list-building trade-off they discover later, any whiff of mass mail, generic praise.
- **Channel and entry:** first, a warm intro if one exists (check Thomas's own LinkedIn connections and past BnB contacts before anything else; events such as Spinn.Bar or Deutscher EngagementTag only if free or cheap and reachable, re:publica costs a ticket [PRUEFEN: Kosten]). Second, LinkedIn to the named campaigner on this issue, with a person-specific reason. Third, the organisation's published partner route (for example WeAct's partner inbox) for a genuine partnership proposal. No info@ blasts.
- **First-touch structure:** their campaign moment → the one gap (form messages vs constituent letters) → what BnB does in one line → bounded test offer or question → easy forward.
- **Smallest ask:** "Passt das für [Kampagne] überhaupt, oder wer bei euch entscheidet über Mitmach-Tools?" Do not ask for a call in the first message unless the recipient's own post invites contact.
- **Lead:** agreement to test one campaign in one channel segment by a date, or a named internal owner plus a 20-minute call.
- **Reality check:** large NGOs have procurement, brand and legal review. Expect weeks, not days. For a solo founder, one large-NGO test per quarter is plenty; small and local orgs ((c), PfD in finding 13) give faster first references.

## (b) Communications and social-media leads

- **Flooded with:** tool pitches, influencer and agency offers, partner content requests, content-calendar overload.
- **They value:** ready-to-use content, a strong CTA beyond "teilen/unterschreiben", brand safety, little approval friction, something that works in Stories and Reels.
- **What makes them reply:** a finished asset idea tied to their next post ("Story-Sequenz: Problem → PLZ eingeben → eigener Brief"), visual proof (one screenshot or a campaign page), and an explicit statement that it is free and not advertising.
- **What kills it:** asking them to "spread the word", long text, unclear sender, AI hype.
- **Channel:** LinkedIn or Instagram DM to the person (Instagram is natural for social leads), or an intro from a campaigner in (a).
- **Smallest ask:** "Wäre so eine Mitmach-Aktion etwas für euren Kanal, oder lieber nicht?" plus an offer to send the draft asset.
- **Lead:** agreement to see a draft campaign page or asset, or a slot in their content plan.

## (c) Small Vereine and Bürgerinitiativen (volunteers, local issues)

- **Flooded with:** less vendor noise, but very little time. Volunteer work happens evenings and weekends.
- **They value:** visible local wins, a Ratsbeschluss or Landtag decision, members actually doing something, things that need no IT knowledge, people who show up in person.
- **What makes them reply:** genuine local knowledge of their issue ([SIGNAL: Ratssitzung/Bebauungsplan/Schließung]), Bremen proximity, and an offer to do the setup.
- **What kills it:** professional sales tone, anything that sounds like they must manage a tool, national framing for a local issue.
- **Channel and entry:** best in person (open meeting, Infostand, Freiwilligen-Agentur Bremen events). Otherwise the public social-media DM of the BI, or LinkedIn if a spokesperson is active there. Use the email or contact form only when the group invites contact on that topic [PRUEFEN: UWG-Risiko].
- **Smallest ask:** "Soll ich euch eine Testseite für [Thema] bauen, die ihr euch einfach anschaut?" Setup is done by Thomas; they approve.
- **Lead:** approval of a test campaign page and a date when they will share it with members (one Rundmail or WhatsApp group). No formal pilot, success metric or evaluation call needed; ask afterwards in one message how it went.

## (d) Umbrella networks (BBE, Paritätischer, VENRO, Landesnetzwerke, Freiwilligenagenturen)

- **Flooded with:** members' requests, policy work, event logistics. Tools are not their remit, but member service and newsletters are.
- **They value:** neutrality between members, usefulness for many members, no commercial interest, a fit with their themes (Demokratiestärkung, Engagement, Digitalisierung).
- **What makes them reply:** a submission that fits an existing format (newsletter item, workshop at an event, a Praxisbeispiel), already written and short.
- **What kills it:** asking for endorsement or "Partnerschaft", or asking them to push the tool to members.
- **Channel:** expected submission routes (for example BBE newsletter submissions per BBE site) and event calls for sessions. For the local layer, the Freiwilligen-Agentur Bremen and the Paritätischer Bremen engagement desk in person.
- **Smallest ask:** "Passt das als kurze Info in euren Newsletter / als Praxisbeispiel?" with a 5-line ready text attached.
- **Lead:** publication of the item, a session slot, or an intro to one member organisation.

## (e) Political-education actors (bpb, Landeszentralen, VHS, teachers as adjacent)

- **Flooded with:** project and material offers, often commercial or partisan. They are bound by Beutelsbacher Konsens and neutrality.
- **They value:** didactic fit, competence-building ("Wer ist zuständig: Bund, Land, Kommune?"), controversy-neutral design, privacy for minors, free access.
- **What makes them reply:** framing as a learning format (a 90-minute VHS evening or a school unit). For school contacts, reference the existing HanisauLand worksheet; for VHS, reference their own Politik programme.
- **What kills it:** campaign language, a pre-set political position, "Kampagne starten", data collection from students.
- **Channel:** the Fachbereich Politik at a VHS or a named Referent:in at a Landeszentrale via LinkedIn or a warm intro. Teachers via Fachkonferenz contacts, not cold.
- **Smallest ask:** "Wäre so ein Format für euer Programm denkbar, und wer plant das bei euch?"
- **Lead:** a planning conversation for a course date or unit, or a referral to the program planner.

# 4. Follow-up cadence

- **Default:** first touch plus one value-bearing follow-up after 7-10 working days. Then stop. This matches the repo rule of at most one unanswered nudge.
- **The follow-up must add something new:** a sketched campaign page for their issue (as a screenshot, not a link that requires work), a relevant date ("Am [Datum] wird im Ausschuss beraten"), the answer to the most likely objection (DSGVO, AI or non-partisanship in one line), or a one-line real example with a date [PRUEFEN].
- **Re-entry rule:** one later message is legitimate only when a *new campaign moment* tied to their issue occurs, for example a Gesetzentwurf, a Haushalt or an election. Not "checking in". If there is still silence, close the record as no-reply.
- **Channel switch:** only to a route the recipient or organisation offers for this purpose (a partner inbox, an event conversation, an intro). Never from LinkedIn silence to a cold info@ email (see section 6).
- **Timing hypotheses (inference):** avoid peak weeks right before their own campaign launch, the summer holidays and late December. Autumn (Sep-Nov) is annual-planning season for the next year. For political-education actors, semester and program planning runs months ahead [PRUEFEN: VHS-Programmschluss].
- **After a yes:** reply the same day with the draft campaign page or a date proposal. Responsiveness is the founder's advantage.

# 5. Call preparation

**They will ask:**

- Who is behind it and how is it funded? (solo project, free, supported by WE AID gGmbH, donations and grants)
- What happens to supporters' data? Do we get it?
- Isn't this AI spam? Will offices discount it?
- Is it non-partisan? Can we do this without a Gemeinnützigkeit risk?
- Can we brand or moderate it? Which levels are covered (Bund/Land/Kommune)? [PRUEFEN: Abdeckung live]
- What numbers will we see?
- What if the project stops?

**Thomas asks (past behaviour first):**

1. "Wann habt ihr zuletzt Leute an Abgeordnete schreiben lassen? Wie lief das, und woran habt ihr gemerkt, ob es gewirkt hat?"
2. "Was ist euer nächster Kampagnenmoment in den nächsten 8 Wochen, und wer ist die Zielperson?"
3. "Wer muss bei euch zustimmen: Kampagne, Kommunikation, Datenschutz, Vorstand?"
4. "Was müsste nach einem Test passiert sein, damit ihr sagt: Das machen wir wieder?"
5. "Was wäre ein No-Go für euch: Branding, Daten, Tonalität?"

**Have ready:**

- A live demo campaign on *their* topic, tested end to end.
- Links to /ki-transparenz, /datenschutz and /ngo-briefkampagne.
- A dated, sourced numbers line [PRUEFEN].
- Plain statements of what BnB cannot do (no supporter export, no auto-send, no guaranteed effect).
- A one-paragraph pilot proposal.

**Pilot offer (no cost; for large NGOs and platforms, persona a. For small Vereine use the light version in persona c):**

- One campaign, one channel test, 3-4 weeks.
- Thomas sets up the page and the organisation approves.

# 6. Legal, platform and ethics risks (risk guidance, not legal advice)

- **UWG § 7 Abs. 2 Nr. 2:** email "Werbung" requires prior express consent, organisations included, and a single unsolicited advertising email can suffice (case law per omsels). Corrected in review: FC Troschenreuth was a commercial banner-ad inquiry, and the BGH said a published address implies consent for inquiries within the recipient's normal activity. Omsels does not discuss non-profit senders at all; "no non-profit carve-out" is not stated there. Whether a free civic tool's offer is "Werbung" is open. Treat it as a risk, not a settled ban. Prefer LinkedIn or Instagram with a person-specific reason, warm intros, events, and published partner or submission routes. Even on social DMs, the ePrivacy definition of electronic mail is broad (see germany-eu.md). No sequences, no bulk.
- **DSGVO:** keep personal notes on contacts minimal and use public professional data only. Honour Art. 21 objections immediately. If a pilot involves BnB processing supporter data on the NGO's behalf, clarify the roles (independent controller vs Auftragsverarbeitung) before launch [PRUEFEN: mit WE AID / Datenschutzbeauftragten]. Never put supporter data in a recap email.
- **Gemeinnützigkeit and non-partisanship:** never frame BnB as helping an organisation "Druck auf Partei X" to make. Let the org define the Anliegen within its Satzungszweck. Letters go to the responsible MPs regardless of party, and BnB stays überparteilich in every touch.
- **Werbekennzeichnung:** when an NGO or creator shares BnB unpaid, it is not paid advertising. If any money, gift or reciprocal benefit enters the arrangement, check the Medienanstalten guidance (see recipient-context.md).
- **Political education:** Beutelsbacher Konsens means no preset positions in school or VHS material, and no data collection from minors.
- **Pressekodex:** applies if an organisation pitch turns into a media story. Never promise coverage (see editorial-pitch.md).
- **Integrity:** no invented partner logos, numbers, "bereits genutzt von", or implied endorsement by WE AID beyond "unterstützt von".
