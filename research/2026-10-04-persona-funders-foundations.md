# 1. Findings that change drafting (ranked)

1. **Check the Traeger fit before you write to any funder.** Every institutional funder below wants a gemeinnuetziger Traeger whose Satzungszweck covers the project (Freistellungsbescheid), or, for Prototype Fund, a natural person. WE AID gGmbH was founded by PHINEO in 2022, originally for short-term crisis aid. It says hosted initiatives can "apply for funding and conclude contracts" and today hosts education, school, culture and climate projects. Its exact Satzungszwecke are not on the pages checked. *Review correction:* an earlier version said WE AID's purposes are limited to aid for persecuted people and refugees. That came from a search summary and is unconfirmed. The range of hosted projects suggests broader purposes. It is still unknown whether "Foerderung des demokratischen Staatswesens" (AO §52 Abs. 2 Nr. 24) or Bildung (Nr. 7) is covered. The Traeger's management usually signs the application (Initiativkreis guide), so WE AID must agree before its name appears in any sketch. Ask WE AID first (Draft 0). `verified-source` (WE AID FAQ and about page, PHINEO press release 2022) + `inference`; Satzung [PRUEFEN].
2. **Most big names are closed doors for an inbound pitch. Route to the few open ones.** The Robert Bosch Stiftung says it "cannot process or respond to unsolicited funding requests" and works only through calls. The Bertelsmann Stiftung funds only projects it develops itself ("we do not make grants ... for third-party projects"). The Koerber-Stiftung runs its own projects; whether it takes inquiries is [PRUEFEN]. Schoepflin selects its own partners (secondary source). The open doors are:
   - **Stiftung Mercator:** accepts a sketch at any time by informal email to the responsible project manager on the team page. It should be 1,000-3,000 characters with title, description, criteria fit and requested amount. Review takes up to 6 weeks. Focus areas include "Teilhabe und Zusammenhalt" and "Digitalisierte Gesellschaft".
   - **ZEIT STIFTUNG BUCERIUS:** online application form with no deadlines stated. It has a "Politik & Gesellschaft" area and puts weight on Hamburg/Norddeutschland (Bremen fits). It funds only gemeinnuetzige Einrichtungen, and private persons only through scholarships.
   - **Buergerstiftung Bremen:** the board decides on current applications. Its Foerderschwerpunkt "Buergerverantwortung und Buergerbeteiligung" fits. It pays "grundsaetzlich keine Personalkosten und/oder Overheadkosten", and projects must be "im Wesentlichen ehrenamtlich organisiert und realisiert".

   `verified-source` (all re-checked 2026-10-04, except Koerber's inquiry policy and Schoepflin).
3. **Prototype Fund Class 03 is open until 30 Nov 2026, and it is the only route here that pays Thomas's own time.** It offers up to EUR 47,500 for 6 months. Applicants are individual developers or teams who are adult, self-employed or freelance, and resident in Germany. The gGmbH cannot apply. The two focus areas are data security and software infrastructure, and infrastructure projects "richten sich ... in der Regel nicht an Endanwenderinnen sondern an Programmierer". So BnB as an end-user app is a weak fit. An open postcode-to-responsible-level routing library extracted from BnB could fit. Ask in the consultation hours before writing (Draft D). `verified-source` (DSEE database, re-checked) + `inference`. Exact track rules and Thomas's freelance status [PRUEFEN].
4. **The best public-money window for 2027 has already closed.** The "Demokratie leben!" Interessenbekundung for projects from 2027 ran 3 Aug to 4 Sep 2026. Its levels are Kommune, Land, Bund (new), Digitaler Raum (new) and Sondervorhaben. DSEE currently lists no open programme. Foerderfonds Demokratie (8 foundations, max EUR 5,000) has been paused since January 2026. Stiftung Mitarbeit Starthilfe is reported as suspended (search summary) [PRUEFEN]. *Review correction:* NLnet is **open again**, with the Restack and CodeSupply funds (EUR 5,000-50,000, deadline 3 Nov 2026 12:00 CET). Both target internet infrastructure and open-source building blocks, not end-user apps, so the fit is as weak as Prototype Fund's. The claim that Mozilla's "Democracy x AI" cohort closed in March 2026 has no source in this file [PRUEFEN]. For big public money, the realistic route now is relationship-building for the next round. `verified-source` (except where marked).
5. **Talk before you submit, unless the funder publishes a sketch route.** German fundraising practice describes a "Sandwich" sequence: personal inquiry → background call → informal draft → formal application. Keep the first spoken pitch under 3 minutes. Do your own research first, and on the phone ask single targeted questions, not a whole catalogue. *Review addition:* where a funder publishes an open sketch route (Mercator), the sketch **is** the first touch. Asking "Darf ich Ihnen eine Skizze schicken?" adds a round trip and gives the officer nothing to judge (Draft A). `practitioner-consensus` (SKala-Campus, undated; Initiativkreis Hamburger Stiftungen guide; Wegweiser Buergergesellschaft).
6. **Most inquiries fail on fit, so never send a Serienbrief.** The Initiativkreis Hamburger Stiftungen guide *estimates* ("nach Schaetzungen") that up to 90% of inquiries are sorted out at once for thematic or structural mismatch or weak preparation. It also says mass mailings "fallen in der Regel sofort auf". Send one tailored text per funder, addressed to a named person, with the amount requested. Frame BnB funding as a time-limited project with a clear output, not open-ended running costs. `practitioner-estimate` (undated guide by Carolin Vogel; the 90% figure is an estimate, not a measured rate).
7. **Funders fund a Wirkungslogik, not a counter.** Use the PHINEO/Bertelsmann Wirkungstreppe: Input → Output → Outcome → Impact. A letter count is an output. *Review update:* the public homepage counter showed "3.023 Briefe geschrieben" on 2026-10-04 (evidence register). The older ~1,800 figure is outdated. It is unclear whether the counter counts generated drafts or mailed letters. Funders will ask whether letters were sent, whether offices replied and who is reached. BnB already asks writers in its last-call mail whether the office replied. Turn that into a stated measurement plan, and use the open question as the reason for the project (point 11). `verified-source` (Kursbuch Wirkung) + `observed` (counter) + `inference`.
8. **Non-partisanship is a funding condition, not just a brand value.** Under AO §52 Nr. 24 and the AEAO, democracy promotion must address democratic principles objectively and neutrally. Political activity must stay a means to the statutory purpose. Show the safeguards in one sentence in every pitch: no party content, the person decides and sends, no mass mailing. `verified-source` (Bundestag WD 2021; AO). Not re-checked in review.
9. **AI drafting needs its own sentence.** In Candid's 2024 survey of 527 US foundations, 23% said they *would not accept* AI-generated applications, 10% would accept them, and 67% were undecided. Write the sketch in your own plain words. Explain BnB's AI safeguards proactively: suggestion only, the person reviews and handwrites, routing is checkable. `verified-source` (US sample; transfer to DE is `inference`).
10. **Doors marked "closed" still open through people.** Candid (2025) reports two figures. In US tax data for 2023, 71% of private foundations ticked "no unsolicited requests". In a separate survey of 522 foundations, 55% said they would consider them. The samples differ, so the figures are not a direct contradiction. The routes in are past grantees, board members, the funder's public events and a short informational conversation. In DE, Thomas's possible warm routes are all **conditional or untested**: a warm network intro (details in private workspace) and PHINEO via WE AID (inference). `verified-source` (US data) + `inference` for DE.

Thomas's options are donations collected via WE AID [PRUEFEN: possible and earmarkable?], a small grant from one funder as co-funding for another, and asking in the Vorgespraech whether volunteer hours count [PRUEFEN per funder]. # 2. Sub-persona playbooks

## (a) Programme officers at private foundations (Mercator, ZEIT STIFTUNG, Bosch, Koerber, Buergerstiftungen)
- **Flooded with:** generic mass inquiries that don't fit the foundation's purpose, sketches without an amount, "we need core funding", requests to operative foundations.
- **Values:** fit to a named programme or focus area, a clear target group, a plausible Wirkungslogik, the applicant's competence, own contribution and co-funding, a perspective after the funding ends, and a short readable text.
- **Replies when:** the first lines name their programme or a comparable project they funded [SIGNAL], the text follows their published route, and it ends with one narrow, answerable question ("Passt das grundsaetzlich in Ihren Bereich?").
- **Kills it:** an attachment dump, superlatives, unverified numbers, calling for an "Austausch" without a question, LinkedIn pitches to people at closed-door foundations, phoning a funder that asks for no contact.
- **Channel / entry path:** a warm intro, then their public event or webinar. For open-route funders, email the responsible project manager on the team page **with the sketch inline** (Mercator) or use the online form (ZEIT). If you can't find the right person, phone the Zentrale ("Wer ist fuer Teilhabe zustaendig?"). Use LinkedIn only for a light touch around a real signal (a post, a talk), never for the funding pitch.
- **First-touch structure (open-route funder):** Traeger + project lead → BnB in plain words → one time-limited project → honest output fact [PRUEFEN] → fit to *their* area via one comparable grant [SIGNAL] → amount and co-funding [PRUEFEN] → one question (fit). Add a forward request in case the person isn't responsible.
- **Smallest ask:** "Passt das grundsaetzlich in Ihren Bereich?" or "Wer bei Ihnen ist fuer X zustaendig?"
- **Lead =** a named responsible person + a scheduled Vorgespraech, OR an explicit invitation to submit an application, OR a referral to a better-fitting funder.

## (b) Public programmes (Demokratie leben!, bpb, Laender, Prototype Fund)
- **Flooded with:** formally incomplete IBs; the procedure itself filters (late or incomplete submissions are excluded).
- **Values:** an exact fit to the published criteria, an eligible legal entity, Eigenanteil, capacity for a Verwendungsnachweis, an evaluation plan. bpb model calls (e.g. Demokratie im Netz 2.0, 2025) say "Natuerliche Personen sind nicht antragsberechtigt" and require an organisation with its own legal capacity. Some bpb streams need "anerkannte Traeger" [PRUEFEN].
- **Replies when:** you ask a concrete eligibility question during the official consultation hours or info event.
- **Kills it:** pitching staff outside the procedure, asking them to "take a look" at your product.
- **Channel / entry path:** official info sessions, Sprechstunden, programme FAQ. Locally: "Demokratie leben!" has a Kommune level (local Partnerschaften fuer Demokratie). Whether Bremen has one with a small Aktions- or Initiativfonds is [PRUEFEN]. Same for the Landeszentrale fuer politische Bildung Bremen as a funding route for freie Traeger [PRUEFEN].
- **Smallest ask:** "Ist ein Projekt mit Traeger WE AID gGmbH / als Einzelperson in Ihrer Foerderlinie grundsaetzlich antragsberechtigt?"
- **Lead =** a written eligibility confirmation + the next deadline in the calendar + a partner organisation (e.g. a recognised Bildungstraeger) willing to co-apply.

## (c) Corporate foundations / CSR (low priority for now)
- **Why low priority:** a political-adjacent free tool is a reputational risk for companies, and cold CSR pitches cost a solo founder a lot of time for little return. Pursue this only when a warm intro exists.
- **Flooded with:** sponsoring requests and logo deals.
- **Values:** reputational safety (strict non-partisanship!), employee engagement, regional ties, measurable and communicable outputs.
- **Replies when:** the ask is concrete and in-kind: cloud/LLM credits, pro-bono UX/legal review, employee volunteering, a small sponsorship for a defined module.
- **Kills it:** anything that reads as party-political, or a vague "partnership".
- **Channel / entry path:** only a personal intro (a warm network intro (details in private workspace), conditional) or the company's official form. No cold LinkedIn pitches to CSR managers.
- **Smallest ask:** one in-kind resource with a number ("API-Credits fuer ca. [PRUEFEN: N] Briefentwuerfe/Monat").
- **Lead =** a call with the person who owns the CSR budget, or an in-kind commitment.
- **Disclosure:** make any sponsor visible on the website and in posts ("Unterstuetzt von ...").

## (d) Civic-tech and open-source funders (OKF DE / Prototype Fund, NLnet, Mozilla, Wikimedia)
- **Values:** an open-source licence, reusability, public-interest infrastructure, documentation, community.
- **Status (2026-10-04):**
  - Prototype Fund: open until 30 Nov 2026; fit doubtful (finding 3).
  - NLnet: Restack and CodeSupply are open until 3 Nov 2026; infrastructure only, so the fit is doubtful.
  - Mozilla: status [PRUEFEN].
  - Wikimedia DE: funds Wikimedia-project volunteers, so it is not a fit.
- **Entry path:** Prototype Fund consultation hours or the projektbetreuung email; the OKF community and civic-tech meetups. Show the repo, not slides.
- **Smallest ask:** "Wuerde eine offene Routing-Bibliothek (PLZ → zustaendige Ebene) in den Schwerpunkt Software-Infrastruktur fallen?"
- **Lead =** a clear yes or no on fit + a submitted application, or a mentor or community contact.

## (e) Small donors / crowdfunding (betterplace, Steady, own Spendenseite)
- **Values:** a tangible result, trust, low effort.
- **Best moment:** in the product, right after a person has written their letter, plus milestone posts. Not cold DMs.
- **Mechanics:** betterplace is free to set up and takes a small transaction share [PRUEFEN: currently cited as 2.8%]. Donation receipts depend on the gemeinnuetzige Traeger (WE AID). Steady takes a platform share [PRUEFEN: about 10%] and suits a "Freundeskreis" of monthly supporters, not tax receipts. Donations also double as Eigenanteil (point 13).
- **Smallest ask:** "Wenn dir das geholfen hat: 3 EUR im Monat halten das Tool kostenlos" + a transparent cost line [PRUEFEN: real monthly costs].
- **Lead =** a recurring donor, or a person who shares the donation page.

# 4. Follow-up cadence

- **Rhythm:** slower than sales. Funders run on board cycles (SKala: proposals for the next year go to boards "spaetestens im November"; plan about 12 months ahead for large foundations). Practical consequence for October 2026: a sketch sent now aims at a 2027 decision. Don't promise quick money and don't chase.
- **Touch 1:** first contact (warm intro, sketch email or question email).
- **Touch 2:**
  - *For a question email* (Buergerstiftung, Prototype Fund): after ~10 working days without a reply, reply in the same thread with one new element and the same question.
  - *For a submitted sketch* (Mercator): wait the stated review time (up to 6 weeks), then send one status question:
  > Guten Tag [NAME], kurze Nachfrage zu meiner Skizze vom [DATUM]. Seitdem [PRUEFEN: ein neuer Fakt, z. B. erste Zahl aus der Nachbefragung]. Ist die Skizze bei Ihnen richtig gelandet, oder gehört sie zu Kolleg:innen? Viele Grüße aus Bremen, Thomas Lorenz
- **Touch 3, ~2 weeks later:** change the route, not the pressure. Call the Zentrale ("Wer ist zustaendig fuer ...?") or ask the warm contact for an intro. Then stop with that person.
- **After a rejection:** thank them and ask one question: "Gibt es einen Grund, den ich fuer andere Antraege lernen kann, oder eine Stiftung, die besser passt?" Ask whether a short update twice a year is welcome, and add them only if they say yes.
- **Channel switch:** email → phone (for funders that invite calls) → LinkedIn only for a real signal (their post or event), never as a parallel "chaser".
- **Stop:** after 2 unanswered touches per person, on a clear "no", or if the funder says it accepts no inquiries.

# 6. Legal, platform and ethics risks (risk guidance, not legal advice)

- **UWG §7 / email:** a grant inquiry from a nonprofit project to a foundation's published applicant contact is an expected route (Mercator invites sketches by email) and is not product advertising (`inference`, not legal advice). Corporate CSR managers and private individuals (donation asks) are different: use warm intros, the official form or your own opt-in channels. Do not send cold donation emails to private people. A connection on LinkedIn is not consent.
- **DSGVO:** take contacts only from team pages or the person's own publications. Use no enrichment tools, record the source in the contact file, and honour objections at once (Art. 21; consider Art. 14 information duties).
- **Gemeinnuetzigkeit:** BnB's political neutrality must be visible (AO §52 Nr. 24), and WE AID's Satzung must cover the purpose. Funds may be used only as agreed; any change needs the funder's consent. Breaching the agreement can lead to repayment.

- **Honesty:** never imply that a funder or partner already supports BnB. The connector contact's offers are conditional. Label reach figures as founder-reported or as "Zaehler auf der Website", say what the counter counts, and do not use AI-generated text that misstates facts in applications.
