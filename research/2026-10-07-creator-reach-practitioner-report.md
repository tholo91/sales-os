# Creator reach without budget: one creator's practitioner report

**Source:** Bob Blume, "INSIDE #1: 7 Fehler, wenn andere eure Reichweite teilen sollen", newsletter *Stimme für Bildungswandel* (Substack), 2026-10-01, https://netzlehrer.substack.com/p/inside-1-reichweite-ohne-budget. Supplied by the founder as the newsletter email. The author is an education creator and author who works without a team and reports about 240,000 followers himself. He calls the text subjective and based on his own experience. Paraphrased below; no outcome data, no reply rates.

## Findings (paraphrased)

1. **Reach is work.** From the creator's side, reach is the result of years of posting. An unpaid ask must acknowledge that effort, not treat reach as a free resource.
2. **Staircase.** Ask people at roughly your own level, not accounts many times larger. Many micro creators beat one large account. Without own reach you need a different argument. Offering a free login to a very large creator in return for showing the product closes doors.
3. **Generic or AI-written messages** visibly sent to many get ignored and annoy the recipient. Most creators handle their inbox alone.
4. **No relationship.** Repeated mentions or tags without a personal line, praise for unnamed content, and asking while not following the creator all read as disrespect.
5. **Mixing roles.** Asking for trial (time), feedback (expertise) and a share (reach) in one request is three paid services in one.
6. **Reciprocity.** The author is positively surprised when an ask acknowledges that support is not one-way, and less willing to help people who never gave anything back.
7. **Imprecision.** "May I send you something?" that hides a share request is evasive. Say what you want; the creator may still say no or want to try it first.

**How it works instead:** the runway grows with the creator's size: follow, comment, ask questions and share their work genuinely for months before asking a large creator. Stay persistent without taking offence after a no. Very large creators are often reachable only via management, which usually means a paid request; even then, show you know their work. Events and conferences help, but without an unprompted pitch. Interest must be real, not played.

## Fit with existing guidance

- **Confirms:** personal messages tied to one piece of their work, no generic praise, warm-up before the ask, bio email or management above ~50k, one clear ask.
- **Changes:** warm-up length scales with creator size; the first ask names the real goal; target size relative to the founder's own reach (Treppe); additions to the "Never" list.
- **Conflict 1, reciprocity vs. consideration:** the author values reciprocity, but a promised return favour can count as consideration and turn a civic share into labelled or prohibited political advertising (§ 5a Abs. 4 UWG; see the 2026-10-04 creator research). Resolution: acknowledgment and earlier genuine shares of their work, never a promised return for the share. Risk guidance, not legal advice.
- **Conflict 2, permission ask vs. precision:** the existing ladder opened with a permission question only. Resolution: keep the low-burden permission step but state the goal in half a sentence before it, without a "no pressure" ending (`workspace/voice.md` rejects those).
- **Not adopted:** the author's own effort figures and follower numbers as benchmarks; the staircase as a fixed size ratio. Tracked BnB outcomes by size band decide.

## Changes made

- `knowledge/personas/creators-influencers.md`: evidence line, three bullets in "What earns a reply", Treppe and scaled warm-up in "Channel and entry path", ask ladder step 1 and message skeleton name the goal, touch plan warm-up, "Never" list and reciprocity note in "Risks".
- `knowledge/evidence/claims-register.md`: staircase heuristic quarantined.
