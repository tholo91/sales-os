## Per persona: felt problem and what a first message should lead with

1. **Independent podcast hosts and journalists.** Felt problem: a full inbox of off-beat, promotional mail and little time (claims 1, 3, 5). Lead with the one sentence that says who in their own audience or region is affected, then one definable fact (claims 1, 2). For podcast hosts a listener-side angle is an assumption (claims 6, 7); a host-specific source in German was not found.
2. **NGO campaigners and civic initiatives.** Felt problem: little spare time and volunteers who step back for time reasons (claim 8). Lead with one finite, small thing tied to their current campaign moment. Whether they want an outside tool is an assumption (claim 9 shows coordination apps are normal, not that campaign tools are wanted).
3. **Political and educational creators.** Felt problem: load of requests and hostility (claim 10, brand creators only). Lead with a specific piece of their work and the benefit to their young audience (claim 11); why they accept or refuse unpaid collaboration is assumption beyond the creator notes already in the repo.
4. **Funders and foundation staff.** Felt problem: applications that do not fit, and applicants who cannot reach a person first (claims 12, 13). Lead with the named programme fit; use a contact person's pre-application question where the programme offers one (claim 12, assumption on transfer).
5. **Founders of small B2B SaaS teams.** Felt problem: silent trial users whose reasons they do not know. Marked assumption: no sourced claim measures this; claim 16 only shows that in-app prompts reach present users. Lead with their trial-drop-off moment, not the product.
6. **WG flatmates and students.** Assumption: the felt problem is "who bought what and who owes whom" at the shared shop (claim 15, vendor, not WG-specific). Lead with that moment, not the app.
7. **Remote workers choosing a place.** Felt problem candidate: no reason to leave the flat (claim 14, 2022 data, assumption on transfer). Lead with one concrete named place and what works there.
