# Bottom line

1. The brief's premise is outdated: Meta reversed the 2024 political-content limit in January 2025. Political content can now be recommended to non-followers at a "standard" default, and users can choose less or more. BnB will probably still be classified as political (inference). That makes reach less predictable, but it does not block it.
2. The ranking levers Thomas can control are the first 3 seconds (skip rate), watch time, and sends per reach. A Reel someone forwards to the friend who always complains is worth more than a liked one.
3. "Comment BRIEF and I'll DM you the link" is engagement bait under Instagram's recommendation rules. Use it only where non-follower reach doesn't matter. Default to bio link plus Story link sticker.
4. Paid boosting of social-issue content is unavailable in the EU since Oct 2025. Growth must come from organic Reels plus Collab posts with NGOs and creators. Collabs are also the cleanest "lead" format.
5. Faceless Reels must carry a voice or real audio and must not be mostly text, or they fall into Instagram's own "not recommended" patterns.
6. (Review) A cold Instagram DM probably lands as a text-only message request. DM 1 must work on its own without a link or clip. Ask for permission to send the link instead (see finding 15).
7. (Review) Build Reels from material only Thomas has: Bremen, his Bundestag internship, his mother's question, real (consented) letters, building it alone. Generic civic explainers are interchangeable (finding 16).

# 1. Findings that change drafting (ranked)

1. **Political content is recommendable again, but BnB is likely "political".** In Jan 2025 Meta said it would treat civic content more like other content (Meta, 2025-01-07). Mosseri set the Instagram/Threads control to less / standard (default) / more, rolling out in the US first and then worldwide (BusinessToday, 2025-01-09). Meta's Transparency Center defines political content as likely to mention governments, elections or social topics. It says political content may be recommended in Reels, Explore and in-feed recommendations. — *verified-source (Meta) + reporting*. Inference: a Reel about writing to your MdB/Landtag/Rathaus mentions government and will often be classified political. Viewers who chose "less" will not get it recommended. (Historical context only: under the 2024 limit, five large US political accounts lost about 65% of reach over 10 weeks, per Accountable Tech, an advocacy group. n=5, US, a different policy regime. Do not transfer the figure to BnB.) **Drafting rule:** lead with an everyday, concrete, local problem and the viewer's action ("Der Bus kommt nie. Wem schreibst du?"), not with parties, culture-war topics or outrage. Never disguise the civic nature or avoid honest words to dodge classification. Track non-follower reach share per Reel to see whether classification hurts. [PRUEFEN: current in-app control wording in DE; help pages render only in-app]
2. **Top three signals: watch time, likes per reach, sends per reach.** Mosseri reportedly said this in January 2025, adding that likes weigh slightly more for followers and sends slightly more for non-followers. — *Review downgrade: secondary-source*. The cited Dataslayer article attributes the quote to Buffer/Later compilations and links no primary Mosseri post. Instagram's own ranking explainer (2023, primary) independently names the same core predictions: reshare, watch to the end, like, go to the audio page. The rule holds; the exact weighting is not verified. The "sends = 3-5x likes" figure comes from the same Dataslayer article and is practitioner lore. Never quote it. **Rule:** every Reel needs one "sendable" element: a fact people forward ("Für Kitas ist nicht Berlin zuständig"), a scene that fits a friend ("der eine Onkel, der nur meckert"), or a 20-second demo someone can use right away.
3. **Explicit engagement asks lose recommendation eligibility.** Instagram stated (late May 2024, in a video it later deleted, quoted by Social Media Today on 2024-06-02) that it won't recommend content that explicitly asks for shares, comments or tags. Its example: asking people to comment a specific word, number or emoji. Open questions and normal CTAs are "fair game". Review note: the original video is gone, so treat this as a reported platform statement that stays current through the Recommendation Guidelines, not as a quotable live document. Buffer (2026) still cites this as part of the Recommendation Guidelines. — *verified-source (Instagram via Social Media Today) + practitioner*. **Rule:** no "Kommentiere BRIEF", no "Teil das", no "Schick das an...". Make the content worth sending instead. Use an open question only when real answers would help ("Bei welchem Thema in deiner Stadt wüsstest du nicht, wer zuständig ist?").
4. **The first 3 seconds are measured directly.** Since Aug 2025, Reels Insights show **skip rate** (share of viewers who leave within 3 s) and a **retention curve** (Social Media Today, 2025-08-24). Instagram's Creators FAQ says to make the first 3 seconds engaging. — *verified-source*. The thresholds "under ~40% is healthy, over 50% means a broken hook" are *unsourced practitioner rules of thumb*. The cited SMT article gives no benchmark. Compare BnB Reels with each other, not with these numbers. **Rule:** judge hooks by skip rate, the body by the retention curve, and the payoff by sends per reach.
5. **Length: ≤3 min is required for non-follower recommendation. 30-60 s is the practical band.** The Creators FAQ says Instagram recommends videos of 3 minutes or less to unconnected audiences. Socialinsider's 2025 data reportedly shows 30-60 s Reels with the highest reach rate (5.6% vs 5.2% under 30 s). — *FAQ verified. Socialinsider figure not verified in the review (the PDF could not be text-extracted). Vendor, correlational, and a 0.4-point gap that is too small to act on.* Length is not a lever. Make each Reel as long as its payoff needs. **BnB default:** demos 15-30 s, stories 35-60 s, nothing over 90 s.
6. **Originality and "not recommended" formats.** Instagram's ranking explainer (2023, still current in substance) limits low-resolution, watermarked, muted, bordered, majority-text and already-posted Reels. The Creators FAQ adds: drop third-party watermarks, post original content, stay under 3 min. [UNBELEGT, review: "Since April 2026, accounts that mostly repost others' content become non-recommendable" has no source in the table. Instagram's originality push dates back to at least 2024; do not cite a 2026 date.] — *verified-source (ranking explainer, FAQ: "Don't share unoriginal content")*. **Rule:** export without CapCut/TikTok watermark (Instagram's Edits app exports clean). Every faceless Reel needs voiceover or real sound and cannot be a text slideshow. Never repost a Reel that is already on the account as a new Reel.
7. **Raw beats polished in 2026.** Mosseri's year-end memo (2025-12-31) argues that authenticity is becoming reproducible with AI and that imperfect, explicitly unproduced content becomes a signal of being real. — *verified-source (Mosseri Threads post, re-checked in review)*. Caveat: this is a CEO opinion about a trend, not a ranking rule. It justifies cheap production. It does not prove that raw Reels get more reach. **Rule:** phone, daylight, one take with small stumbles is fine. No stock-footage montage, no AI avatar, no AI voice for the founder.
8. **Search runs on keywords, not hashtags.** Since Dec 2025 Instagram caps hashtags at 5 per post/Reel. Mosseri says hashtags help search little and don't drive reach (Digital Trends, Dec 2025; *review: the page returned empty on re-fetch, so this is not re-verified. Check the cap in-app when posting*). Practitioners report that captions, on-screen text, spoken words and alt text feed Instagram search. Public posts of professional accounts can appear in Google since July 2025 (*practitioner reports; verify*). **Rule:** the first caption line holds the searchable phrase people actually type ("Brief an Abgeordnete schreiben", "wer ist zuständig für Schlaglöcher"), then use 0-3 specific hashtags.
9. **Trial Reels need ≥1,000 followers.** They show a Reel only to non-followers first, with views, likes, comments and shares after ~24 h. They require a public professional account with 1,000+ followers (Social Media Today, 2025-07-13; practitioners report the same in 2026). — *verified-source (trade press)*. [PRUEFEN: BnB account follower count] If below 1,000, run the face/faceless test sequentially (Section "Test design"). If above, use Trial Reels to test two hooks for the same body without spamming followers.
10. **Cadence.** Instagram's Creators FAQ: creators with the strongest follower growth average 10+ Reels per month. Mosseri says there is no golden rule. Practitioner consensus is 3-5 Reels per week, consistently. — *verified-source + practitioner*. Review note: the FAQ figure is correlational (accounts that already grow post more). It does not prove that posting more causes growth. **Solo rule (review, adjusted for one person with limited energy):** 2 Reels per week from one 60-90 minute batch session as the floor you can sustain; go to 3 only if the batch habit holds for 4 weeks. Missing a week is better than dropping LinkedIn outreach, which currently produces the conversations. Reply to comments within the first days (the FAQ recommends liking/replying authentically within 7 days).
11. **Audio.** The FAQ says trending audio can affect distribution, but for BnB the voice is the content. Use trending audio only as low-volume background in faceless B-roll and only from Instagram's in-app library. Business accounts have a restricted music library. [PRUEFEN: account type Creator vs Business and which library is available]. — *verified-source + inference*.
12. **No paid fallback in the EU.** Meta stopped social-issue, electoral and political ads in the EU on 2025-10-06 because of the TTPA regulation. Organic posts are unaffected. — *verified-source (Meta notice, reporting)*. Inference: a "Beitrag bewerben" boost of a BnB Reel will likely be rejected. Collabs and creator/NGO reposting are the only multipliers.
13. **Collab posts are the partnership format.** One Reel can have up to 5 invited collaborators. It appears on every co-author profile, and likes and comments are merged (*Sked Social, 2025-12-22, re-checked in review*). Sked says both accounts must be business or creator profiles [PRUEFEN: in-app for the BnB account and the partner before proposing a Collab; if not possible, fall back to a Story with link sticker]. The Medienanstalten guideline (May 2025) says collab posts must be checked for advertising labelling on your own channel. **Rule:** the "smallest ask" to an NGO or creator is one Collab Reel or one Story with link sticker, not a "partnership".
14. **Face vs faceless has no reliable evidence.** One vendor study (The Content Labs) reports a 1.9x median-view advantage for on-camera. It covers 7,431 videos from only 96 creators, across TikTok, Instagram (3,485) and YouTube Shorts. It also reports that 84% of 100K+ hits were on-camera. Review: the sample is mostly creator-led entertainment, and creators self-select their format, so it says little about a civic tool demo. *Vendor, low confidence.* Treat it as a hypothesis and run the test below.
15. **(Review addition) Cold DMs arrive as text-only message requests.** Meta says it limits message requests to someone who doesn't follow or isn't connected to the sender to text-only messages (Meta newsroom, 2024-01-25, updated 2024-11-25). [PRUEFEN: the sentence sits in the teen-safety section; check in-app whether it applies to adult recipients, and whether read receipts ("Gesehen") show before a request is accepted.] — *verified-source (partial applicability)*. **Rule:** DM 1 to a creator or NGO is plain text that works on its own, with no link, clip or voice note. Ask "soll ich dir den Link schicken?" instead of sending it. Follow and genuinely comment first so the name is recognizable in the request folder. Don't stack follow-ups in an unaccepted request. If an NGO or creator lists a business/press email, that is usually the more reliable inbox.
16. **(Review addition) Make what only Thomas can make.** Mosseri's memo frames the bar as whether you can "make something that only you could create" (2025-12-31). — *verified-source (opinion, not a ranking rule)*. For BnB this is also the honest differentiator. Generic "So schreibst du deinem Abgeordneten" explainers exist everywhere. Material only Thomas has: his mother's question (already used in instagram-start.md), the Bundestag internship (in the repo's positioning; [PRUEFEN: exact facts before any claim]), building it alone in Bremen, real letters and replies (only with written consent), and live tool results for real local issues. **Rule:** every Reel contains at least one element another civic account couldn't post.
17. **(Review addition) Partners say yes to finished assets, not to ideas.** This is an inference from the Collab mechanics (finding 13) and from NGO and creator workload. Collab only works if the partner accepts the invite, so the lowest-friction ask is a finished draft: script or cut, caption, Story sticker text, and the topic page. # Five Reel script templates

**T1: On-camera, 15-30 s (problem → demo → step).**
0-2 s: hook line from table, face close, text overlay. 2-8 s: one sentence of tension ("Die meisten wissen nicht, ob Stadt, Land oder Bund"). 8-22 s: cut to screen. Issue typed or spoken, PLZ, recipient appears, draft scrolls (sped up 2x). 22-28 s: back to face: "Lesen, ändern, abschreiben, abschicken. Kostenlos, ohne Account." [PRUEFEN: "ohne Account" is a repo fact (evidence.md 2026-09-04), not a live check; the draft is sent by email, so don't imply that no data is needed] 28-30 s: "Link im Profil." Caption line 1 holds the keywords; no comment-word CTA.

**T2: On-camera story, 30-60 s (scene → tension → turn → payoff → handoff).**
0-3 s: story hook (#4/#6). 3-15 s: concrete scene with one detail (place, sentence, object). 15-30 s: tension ("Sie wusste nicht, wem. Und wie man aus Frust einen vernünftigen Brief macht."). 30-45 s: turn, with 5 s of B-roll of the tool. 45-55 s: payoff, one honest number with source [PRUEFEN: counter definition, generated vs mailed] or a user voice with consent. 55-60 s: open question or "Link im Profil". Loop the ending back into the first line if natural.

**T3: Faceless screen demo, 15-25 s.**
0-1 s: first frame already shows the typing ("Der Bus kommt nie."). Never a logo or intro. Voiceover by Thomas (his own voice, not AI). 1-15 s: real screen recording, zoomed to the active field, cut dead time, captions burned in. 15-22 s: hand writes the first line on paper, envelope, postbox. 22-25 s: on-screen "brief-nach-berlin.de · Link im Profil". Text covers no more than ~20% of the frame, so it is not a "majority-text" Reel.

**T4: Faceless text-on-B-roll, 10-20 s.**
B-roll Thomas films himself: street, bus stop, postbox, pen. Over it, 3 short text beats (each ≤7 words, 2-3 s) + voiceover or real ambient sound + optional low-volume library track. Example: "Du meckerst über den Bus." / "Die Stadt weiß davon nichts." / "Ein Brief sagt's der richtigen Stelle. 3 Minuten." (avoid "ändert das": impact promise). Ends on the postbox shot to loop.

**T5: Reply-to-comment, 20-45 s.**
Use the native "reply with Reel" so the comment sticker shows. 0-2 s: read the question aloud ("[Name] fragt: …"). 2-30 s: answer by showing, not telling (screen or paper). 30-40 s: thank the person by first name if public and harmless, then a pointer to the next question. Best source of hooks: real comments and DMs [SIGNAL: Kommentar].

# Filming checklist (solo founder, phone)

1. Batch: write 3 scripts (hook + 3 beats + end) in 20 min; film all in one 60-90 min session; edit in Instagram's Edits app (teleprompter, auto-captions, watermark-free export).
2. Frame: 9:16, 1080p+, phone at eye level on a stack of books or a cheap tripod, face in the upper third, keep the bottom ~20% and right edge free of key text (UI overlays).
3. Light: face a window; no backlight. Sound: quiet room, clip-on mic or phone close (≤50 cm); sound matters more than image.
4. First frame: already in motion (mid-sentence, typing, pen moving). Cut every pause >0.5 s in the first 5 s.
5. Captions: always on, burned in or native, proofread (names, PLZ, "MdB"). Many watch muted; captions also feed search.
6. Screen recordings: use a test issue; no real user data, emails or addresses on screen; zoom in; speed up waits.
7. Before posting: cover frame, keyword caption line, ≤3 hashtags, alt text, no watermark, no comment-word CTA, facts checked against evidence.md, Story with link sticker queued.
8. After posting: answer comments the same day; log skip rate, avg watch time, sends/reach, non-follower %, profile visits, link taps after 48 h and 7 days.

# Face vs faceless test design

- **Question:** which format gets more qualified attention per hour of Thomas's effort?
- **Unit:** matched pairs. Same topic, same hook wording (spoken vs on-screen), length within ±5 s, same caption template. Only the format differs: T1/T2 on-camera vs T3/T4 faceless.
- **Volume:** 6 pairs = 12 Reels over 6 weeks (2/week; review change from 3/week over 4 weeks to fit one person's capacity). Alternate which format goes first in each pair. Post at the same weekday/time slot. If ≥1,000 followers, post both as Trial Reels first.
- **Primary metric:** sends per reach. **Secondary:** skip rate, average watch time as % of length, non-follower reach share, profile visits → link taps, BnB starts from Instagram [PRUEFEN: whether BnB can log a `?ref=ig` source without new tracking/consent issues], DMs with a real issue or a partnership question. **Cost:** minutes to produce.
- **Decision rule:** a format wins if it beats the other on sends per reach **and** skip rate in ≥4 of 6 pairs without losing on link taps. If neither wins, keep both and default to the cheaper one, probably faceless. Any DM from an NGO or creator overrides reach metrics: note which format triggered it. With n=6, this is directional, not significant. Re-test after 3 months.
- **Do not change** hook type, topic and format in the same pair. Log every Reel in the experiments file (`templates/experiments.md` schema).

# Link paths and DM flows

- **Default:** bio link (BnB homepage or a current campaign page) + "Link im Profil" + a Story with link sticker on posting day. Story link stickers are available to all accounts. Clickable caption links are only in a limited Meta Verified test (2026, PetaPixel/Engadget) and should not be relied on.
- **Comment-keyword auto-DM: not recommended for BnB (review downgrade from "option").** Meta's API allows exactly one private reply to a user's comment within 7 days. Further messages need the user to reply (Meta developer docs, re-checked in review). This is a lead-funnel tactic from paid creator and SaaS marketing. For a free civic tool it costs reach (finding 3), adds DSGVO duties for a third-party processor, and reads as a funnel. Revisit only if bio-link taps are clearly the bottleneck, and then only on follower-facing Stories.
- **Manual DMs:** reply personally to people who DM a real issue. This is user-initiated, so a helpful answer with the link is fine.

# 6. Legal, platform and ethics risks (risk guidance, not legal advice)

- **Political classification:** reach to "less political" users will be reduced (finding 1). Never mislabel or hide the civic topic to evade it. During election periods, expect more scrutiny. Stay strictly non-partisan, which also matters for WE AID's gGmbH status [PRUEFEN: neutrality expectations with WE AID].
- **Engagement bait / automation:** comment-word CTAs and explicit share asks lose recommendation eligibility. Auto-DM tools act only on user-initiated comments, one reply within 7 days. No bulk DMs, no follow/unfollow, no scraping (Instagram Community Guidelines prohibit repetitive content and unsolicited commercial contact).
- **Werbekennzeichnung:** per the Medienanstalten guideline (May 2025), posts about your own project need no "Werbung" label when your own role is obvious. Say "mein Projekt" in caption or video. Collab posts and anything with payment, free goods or reciprocal deals must be checked for labelling on each account. Use Instagram's "Bezahlte Partnerschaft" tool when money is involved.
- **No ads:** Meta prohibits social-issue/political ads in the EU since 2025-10-06. Don't boost BnB Reels or pay creators to run ads about it.
- **DSGVO / KUG / consent:** no identifiable bystanders in B-roll without consent (KUG §22). No real letters, names, addresses or user messages on screen without written consent. Anonymise screenshots. Auto-DM tools need a data-processing contract and a privacy-notice mention.
- **Music and AI:** in-app licensed audio only. Business accounts may have a restricted library. No AI-generated faces or voice of Thomas. Label realistic AI visuals with Meta's AI tag.
- **UWG §7:** applies to email/phone follow-ups, not Instagram DMs as such. Use email only for published business/press addresses with a clear reason. For media contacts the Pressekodex separates editorial content from advertising, so never offer anything in exchange for coverage.
- **Claims:** no impact promises ("so änderst du Politik"), no unverified numbers or "Politiker antworten immer". Reported numbers carry date and source.
