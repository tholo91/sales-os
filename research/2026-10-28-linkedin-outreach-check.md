# LinkedIn outreach check: warm-up, micro-ask, salesy tells

Researched 2026-10-28 by sub-agents from web search snippets; most figures are vendor data, sample sizes and methods mostly unverified. Treat as working evidence, not proof.

## Findings

| Question | Finding | Source | Confidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Warm-up comment before DM | No controlled study isolates it. Vendors describe warm-touch sequences, but without a measured lift; Belkins figures contradict each other. | https://expandi.io/blog/linkedin-outreach-benchmarks-2026/ , https://belkins.io/blog/linkedin-outreach-study.md | low |
| Connection note | Acceptance about equal with or without a note (≈26% both); replies higher with a note (≈8–9% vs ≈5%). Waalaxy reports the opposite for acceptance. | https://expandi.io/blog/linkedin-connection-message-templates/ , https://lagrowthmachine.com/should-i-add-a-note-to-my-linkedin-connection-request/ | medium |
| Interest CTA vs. meeting ask | Gong (≈304k sales emails) favours an interest question over a time/day ask on cold first touch. Email only; transfer to LinkedIn is an assumption. | https://www.gong.io/blog/this-surprising-cold-email-cta-will-help-you-book-a-lot-more-meetings | medium (email), low (LinkedIn) |
| Follow-ups | Second follow-up adds the most replies, later ones little; 4–7 day spacing. | https://expandi.io/blog/linkedin-follow-up-message/ | medium |
| Length | Short first messages (≈25–75 words) correlate with more replies in Lavender email data (second-hand). | https://www.lavender.ai/blog/the-cold-email-benchmark-report | medium |
| Journalists | Creator-journalists prefer pitches under 200 words; 82% accept a follow-up in week one; relevant pitches are rare. | https://muckrack.com/stateofjournalism/ | medium |
| AI-sounding copy | No independent study; vendor claims conflict. Consistent theme: a real signal matters more than who drafted it. | https://www.autobound.ai/blog/ai-email-generators-what-actually-works | low |
| Platform limits | About 100 invites per week (vendor blogs, no official figure). Manual founder volume stays below. | https://www.surfe.com/blog/linkedin-limits-in-2025/ | medium |

## What this means for the Sales OS

- The existing rules (one observation, one easy ask, no call before relevance, optional warm-up, two follow-ups) match practitioner guidance.
- The salesy feel in drafts comes from generic first lines, several asks in one message, and large asks (guest article, share, call) before any reply.
- Thomas's confirmed own DMs use "Ich hab … gebaut" as a bridge after a concrete hook; that bridge is not the problem.
- Untested: whether a public comment before the DM lifts replies. Cheap test: 20 contacts with comment, 20 without.
