## Contradicts or weakens existing guidance

- `knowledge/markets/germany-eu.md`, "Rechtliche Kurzreferenz", Email bullet ("cold email only as an editorial pitch ... to an editorial address"): weakened. No ruling on editorial tips or press addresses was found, and claims 1-2, 12 show courts reading "Werbung" broadly and rejecting "public address" as consent. The editorial exception is the repo's own risk reading, not case law.
- Same file, "Risk boundary", ePrivacy paragraph and `research/2026-10-02-germany-us-outreach.md` Art. 2(h) row: weakened as to sourcing. The "broad electronic mail" case law (claim 5) concerns inbox ads; the DM position rests on OLG Hamm 18 U 154/22 (claim 4), which is unpublished-looking, disputed in date and form, and not clearly B2B.
- Same file, LinkedIn invitations bullet ("3 connection requests a month"): LinkedIn's own pages differ (claim 8).
- `knowledge/channels/linkedin.md` line 30 ("generally needs InMail"): InMail is Premium; free accounts have the group, coworker and connection-note routes (claim 9).
- Same file, Instagram bullet `[PRÜFEN: adult applicability]`: answered by claim 10 (news source, not Instagram's own page).
- Citation hygiene: the repo cites § 7 without paragraph numbers; the current statute puts electronic mail in § 7 Abs. 2 Nr. 2, while rulings from 2023 cite Nr. 3 a.F. (statute read 2026-11-11).
