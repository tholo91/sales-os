# Findings

Make role, actual relationship, recipient benefit, burden, and smallest appropriate ask a brief reusable check. Role is useful for investigating the person; it cannot establish their motives, interest, willingness, or permission. Select the purpose before drafting: voluntary civic participation, paid collaboration, and commercial customer acquisition have different actual requests and outcomes.

The sources below substantiate professional constraints and engagement principles. They do not prove that this check improves response rates, supply a universal DE/US tone, or justify any automatic contact. No psychological profiling or role-based reply forecast is supported.

# Repository audit and proposed integration

Inspected `knowledge/strategies/first-touch.md`, `editorial-pitch.md`, `visibility-vs-paid.md`, `.agents/skills/linkedin-multipliers/SKILL.md`, and the source policy and claims register. The existing rules already require individual evidence, prevent invented warmth, and recognize editorial pitches. The missing piece is consistent recipient benefit/burden reasoning across selection, first touch and follow-up.

1. Load `knowledge/strategies/recipient-context.md` from the shared first-touch, discovery and follow-up paths and relevant specialist skills. Preserve five fields in existing private contact/interaction notes; avoid another mandatory founder questionnaire or migration of private records.
2. In `linkedin-multipliers`, qualify current topical fit and actual capability before the lightweight reach score; treat audience size as capacity, not interest. Add the five-field note to evidence criteria and explicitly distinguish voluntary civic support from paid creator collaboration and commercial customers.
3. Resolve the drafting conflict: `linkedin-multipliers` prohibits every first-DM story request, while `first-touch.md` already routes expected editorial pitches separately. Allow the editorial exception only for verified beat and expected pitch route. An arbitrary connected journalist is not automatically an expected pitch route.
4. Apply recipient language and observed `du`/`Sie` or naming conventions while preserving founder voice. German default brand positioning need not force German onto an English-language recipient; country and LinkedIn connection status do not establish communication norms or consent.
5. Recompute the check from actual replies in follow-up; do not escalate because of role or silence. Judge useful replies and agreed next actions for the actual purpose, with denominators, dates and contact context. No fixed high response rate is promised.
