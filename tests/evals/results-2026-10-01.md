# LinkedIn public-post behavioral evaluation, 2026-10-01

## Method and limits

Five synthetic requests from `linkedin-public-post-cases.json` were executed by an independent agent using the current skills and required references. The agent received the prompts and minimal context, without the expected gates, prior audit, or proposed changes. It read no private workspace data and performed no external action. The lead reviewed the actual responses below against the case criteria.

This is one generated response per case with a qualitative review. It is neither a statistical model evaluation nor evidence of improved LinkedIn reach or conversions. The missing-material case prompted a clarification in the skill's output contract: the asset may be a focused input request while its material gate remains unsatisfied.
