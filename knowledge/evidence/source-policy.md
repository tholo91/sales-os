# Source policy

## Evidence levels

- `high`: primary official source or direct project evidence.
- `medium`: credible secondary source, first-hand report, or working framework.
- `low`: unsourced synthesis, anecdote, inference, or time-sensitive claim awaiting verification.

## Required metadata

Every curated knowledge file states source URL, publisher, retrieval and verification dates, jurisdiction, terms or license, confidence, and review date.

## Rules

- Preserve raw user-provided sources separately from curated guidance.
- Paraphrase rather than copy unless license and attribution are explicit.
- Keep numerical benchmarks, legal conclusions, platform limits, and cultural claims in the claims register until verified.
- Prefer current primary sources for technical, legal, and platform rules.
- Mark conflicts instead of silently selecting the more convenient claim.
