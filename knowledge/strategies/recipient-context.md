# Recipient context

Use this before selecting a person, drafting a first touch, or deciding a follow-up. Role guides what to investigate; the person's actual work and words decide the approach. These are working hypotheses, not personality profiles or guaranteed reply-rate improvements.

## Five-field check

Fill these from existing project and contact evidence. Keep it as a short internal note; do not make the founder answer five new questions for every message. Mark unknowns and hypotheses explicitly. An unknown benefit or burden is not itself a blocker: an honest, relevant no-ask first message may explore the fit without pretending to know it.

1. **Role and capability:** current function, relevant work, intended audience, and what this person can actually decide or contribute. Record the source and date. A title, follower count, or prestigious employer does not prove access, decision authority, political alignment, or willingness.
2. **Actual relationship:** no prior contact, connected only, public interaction, substantive exchange, trusted introduction, or ongoing work; include what was actually said and any contact preference. Connection acceptance is not a warm relationship, interest, or consent.
3. **Felt problem and benefit:** the moment or difficulty they or their people are in, in their own words where they wrote or said it (else the project's `icp.md` hypothesis, kept internal), and the one thing that changes for them, tied to one observed fact; plus one usable proof line from `## Gemeinsame Belegzeilen`, or none. This becomes line 2 of the draft. Distinguish benefit to them from the founder's desired distribution or revenue.
4. **Burden or risk hypothesis:** actual requested time and work; possible verification, reputation, editorial independence, public association, cost, implementation, or approval burden. Treat undisclosed motives as unknown. Do not infer sensitive characteristics or invent personal fears.
5. **Smallest appropriate ask:** one relevant next action, or no ask when none follows naturally. Include channel/language fit and known permission or expected-contact limits. A link review, free audit, introduction, public share, and meeting are real work even when phrased as a quick question. It also picks the approach and whether a what-it-is line belongs (`first-touch.md`, `**Approach by recipient**`).

If the fit or reason to contact cannot be stated honestly, research that one gap or choose another person. Do not pad drafts with generic praise. If capability is unclear but relevance is established, use a narrow question about responsibility rather than treating the person as a buyer or promoter.

## Choose the purpose first

| Purpose | What to make explicit | What counts as a suitable outcome |
|---|---|---|
| Voluntary civic support | The actual civic purpose, voluntary involvement, requested effort, and the person's freedom to decide. Preserve the active project's participation principles. | A substantive exchange, independently chosen use, relevant introduction, or voluntary sharing when agreed; no implied endorsement or owed promotion. |
| Paid creator or media collaboration | Commercial intent, real scope, compensation status, editorial/creative control, and rights if a concrete offer is being discussed. Do not pretend an unpaid favor is a paid opportunity. | Explicit scope or budget discussion with the appropriate contact; later delivery only under agreed terms. |
| Commercial customer discovery or sale | The real discovery or commercial purpose, observed workflow relevance, and current proof limits. Do not disguise a sales approach as independent research or recruit a distributor as if they were the user. | Useful problem evidence, a relevant decision-maker conversation, or an agreed evaluation/purchase step. |

## Role-specific working hypotheses

These suggestions identify questions to investigate. Individual evidence overrides them; several roles can apply to one person. Choose the role relevant to this request.

| Role | Evidence to inspect | Possible benefit and burden | Proportionate first step |
|---|---|---|---|
| Creator / influencer | Actual topics and audience, recent content, collaboration route, and any stated partnership preferences. | Relevant audience value may matter; content work, creative control and association with a cause or brand may matter too. | For voluntary civic contact, establish concrete topical fit without an immediate share request. For an expected paid-collaboration route, state the commercial purpose honestly. Keep independent opinion possible. |
| Researcher / scientist | Current research question, publication or engagement work, and institutional function. | A relevant case, dialogue or application may help; accuracy, time, research ethics and implied endorsement may create work. | One precise topic or relevant case. A request for analysis or review is consulting effort, not a free micro-ask; do not manufacture it as a sales funnel. |
| Journalist / editor / producer | Current beat, outlet audience, published work and expected pitch route. | A timely, verifiable story may be useful; promotional pressure and weak evidence undermine editorial usefulness. | When the route expects story ideas, use `editorial-pitch.md` directly: one news angle and a question about editorial interest. Otherwise use the ordinary first-touch rule. Never ask for guaranteed coverage or civic advocacy as editorial work. |
| NGO / community organizer | Current mission, specific activity, members served, person's remit and stated collaboration process. | A practical fit to existing work may help; coordination and an open-ended commitment may consume scarce capacity. | Connect to one existing activity; clarify whether a narrowly scoped example is relevant. Do not presume mission alignment means an obligation to distribute. |
| Commercial decision-maker / operator | Concrete workflow, remit, existing workaround, and who uses, approves or implements a change. | Work savings or better outcomes are hypotheses until evidenced; budget, change effort and shared stakeholder requirements may matter. | Check a specific workflow or business relevance; an invited buyer with a stated need can receive a direct next-step proposal. Avoid invented ROI and premature demos to the wrong role. |

## Follow-up updates the check

Start from the actual prior message, outcome and promised next step. Update benefit/burden hypotheses from new evidence, especially the person's own reply. Answer an expressed concern before asking for more; use the recipient's requested timing or channel. A substantive reply does not authorize every larger ask. If the person requests material, prepare that material without inventing another permission loop for them; sending remains the founder's manual action. Silence does not explain their motive or justify escalating a share, meeting or introduction request. Follow the existing follow-up stop rules.

For DE/US handling, use the market and channel references as an overlay. Actual language, self-presentation, location, contact route and purpose govern the draft; nationality alone does not establish tone, willingness or permission.
