# Visibility versus paid acquisition

Use direct conversations when the problem, ICP, message, or offer is still uncertain. Use visibility work when recurring insights can attract the same audience and support future conversations.

Do not use paid acquisition to compensate for an unvalidated problem or unclear conversion path. Consider ads only after the project can state who converts, why they act, what the next step is, and how success will be measured.

The cheapest default test is usually a real conversation, a useful public contribution, a warm introduction, or a small manually delivered pilot.
