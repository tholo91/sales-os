# Call-Spickzettel

A one-screen Markdown cheat sheet the founder glances at during a live call. It is derived from the call brief, never a replacement for preparation. It answers one question in under two seconds: what do I say or ask next?

## Rules

1. Keywords, not sentences; bold the one anchor word per line. Full sentences invite reading aloud and sound scripted. [1][2]
2. One block per call phase, in call order; the block change marks the transition. [2]
3. At most four lines per block; working memory holds about three to five chunks. [3]
4. Write out verbatim only three things: the opener, the starred key question, and the close. Everything else stays keywords. (Sales OS practice, unverified.)
5. Questions start with Was or Wie, never Warum, and ask about past behaviour per `discovery-call.md`. [4]
6. At most three proof lines, each with its Stand or n, taken only from approved project proof lines.
7. No talk ratio or question quota on the sheet; Gong's data does not support gaming one (see `discovery-call.md`). [5]
8. No open `[PRÜFEN: …]` or `[SIGNAL: …]` markers; nobody can verify mid-call. Leave unverified facts off the sheet and list them in the brief instead.
9. Place the window directly under the webcam, glance, then look back into the lens; rehearse once so the glance stays short. [6][7]
10. No live AI assistant that listens to the other side (e.g. Cluely) and no recording without everyone's prior consent; §201 StGB protects the non-public spoken word. Risk guidance, not legal advice. [8]

## Template

Fill only the blocks the call mode needs. Funder and media calls drop the offer block and keep the neutral demo line.

```markdown
## Spicker · [Name] ([Organisation]) · [Tag Uhrzeit] · [du/Sie]

**Ziel:** **[Zielcommitment]** mit **[Datum + Owner]**
**Okay-Nein:** „[akzeptables Nein]“

### 1 Einstieg (wörtlich)
„[Danke + Dauer bestätigen + ihre Agenda zuerst + am Ende nächster Schritt oder klares Nein]“

### 2 Wer ich bin
- **[Anker]** · [Stichworte]   (max. 4 Zeilen)

### 3 Belege
- **[Zahl oder Geschichte]** ([Stand/n])   (max. 3 Zeilen)

### 4 Fragen (★ = Pflicht)
- ★ „[Schlüsselfrage wörtlich]“
- ★ [Stichworte] · [Stichworte]   (max. 4 Zeilen)

### 5 Angebot
- **A · [Option]** · **B · [Option]**   (max. 4 Zeilen)

**Demo:** **[Beispiel/Thema]** · nicht zeigen: [Liste]

### 6 Wenn sie fragen
- **„[Einwand]“** → [Antwort in Stichworten]   (max. 4 Zeilen)

### 7 Abschluss (wörtlich)
„[Close-Frage]“ → „[Wen sollte ich noch fragen?]“ → Recap **[wann]**

### Nicht sagen
[Wort] · [Wort] · [Behauptung]
```

## Avoid

- A full script or paragraphs. [1][2]
- More than four lines per block or more than three numbers. [3]
- Warum questions. [4]
- Quoting 43:57 or "11–14 questions" as rules; they are only secondary citations of Gong. [5]
