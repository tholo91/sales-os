# Email

Email can fit expected professional inbound contexts, existing relationships, public editorial addresses, and properly permissioned outreach. The legal basis differs substantially by jurisdiction and situation.

Before writing, verify the relationship, recipient role, source of the address, sender and recipient jurisdictions, expected-contact or consent evidence, prior objection, purpose, and any applicable organizational policy. If that basis is unclear, do not solve it with softer wording; choose a safer route or seek qualified advice.

For Germany, promotional electronic mail generally requires prior express consent under UWG section 7, including for other market participants. The statutory existing-customer exception is narrow and requires every listed condition. GDPR legitimate interest concerns processing; it does not independently authorize an otherwise prohibited marketing message.

## Message discipline

- Explain why this recipient was selected.
- Use one CTA and no attachment in the first touch.
- Sell the next conversation, not the whole product.
- Provide a clear identity and honest reason for contact.
- Make the commercial purpose recognizable; do not disguise the sender or subject.
- Include an easy way to stop further contact and honor it immediately.
- Match `du`/`Sie`, greeting, and sign-off to the actual relationship or visible recipient evidence, not a country stereotype.

## Compliance boundary

For Germany/EU, consult current [UWG section 7](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/uwg_2004/__7.html), [DDG section 6](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/ddg/__6.html), [GDPR Articles 6 and 21](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2016/679/art_21/oj), and qualified legal advice before promotional cold email. Do not describe presumed consent as a loophole, infer consent from a page visit, or assume a social-network DM is legally outside electronic mail.

Gmail separately prohibits spam and unsolicited commercial mail. Authentication and recipient complaints affect delivery even at low volume; bulk-sender requirements add explicit SPF, DKIM, DMARC, spam-rate, and one-click-unsubscribe controls. Sources: [Gmail Program Policies](https://support.google.com/mail/answer/16734397?hl=en) and [sender guidelines](https://support.google.com/mail/answer/14229414?hl=en) (verified 2026-08-21).

For US outreach, consult the FTC's current [CAN-SPAM compliance guide](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/can-spam-act-compliance-guide-business). Compliance is broader than adding an unsubscribe link.
