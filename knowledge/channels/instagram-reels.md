# Instagram Reels

## Scope

Drafting Reel scripts only. The founder films, edits and posts. No scheduling, auto-DMs, sourcing or analytics automation. Creator and NGO DMs follow `knowledge/personas/creators-influencers.md`; public comments follow `knowledge/channels/instagram.md`. Gaps use the markers from `knowledge/foundations/human-writing.md` (section Draft-first and gap markers).

## Platform rules that change the script

- **Civic content:** Meta made civic content recommendable again in January 2025, but a Reel that mentions government or social topics is likely classified political, and viewers who chose "less" will not get it recommended. Lead with an everyday local problem and the viewer's step; no parties, no outrage. Never hide the civic nature to dodge classification. Glance at the non-follower share per Reel to see whether classification hurts.
- **Length:** at most 3 minutes for recommendation to unconnected viewers. Project defaults: demos 15–30 s, stories 35–60 s, nothing over 90 s. Length is not a lever; make the Reel as long as its payoff needs.
- **First 3 seconds:** Reels Insights show skip rate (viewers leaving within 3 s) and a retention curve. There is no external benchmark (the 40 %/50 % thresholds circulating online are unsourced); compare the project's Reels with each other.
- **Core levers:** watch time and sends per reach (Instagram's 2023 ranking explainer names reshare and full watch; the Mosseri "top three signals" quote is secondary-source). Never quote a "sends = 3–5x likes" figure.
- **No engagement-bait CTAs:** no "Kommentiere X", "Teil das", "Schick das an …" or tag requests; Instagram's recommendation guidelines exclude them. An open question that real answers would help is fine.
- **Originality:** no third-party watermark, never re-upload a Reel already on the account. Faceless Reels need voice or real sound and on-screen text under about 20 % of the frame; text slideshows and muted clips are not recommended.
- **Search:** keywords, not hashtags. Caption line 1 is the phrase people actually type; 0–3 specific hashtags. Check the current hashtag cap in-app.
- **Trial Reels** (shown to non-followers first) need a public professional account with 1,000 followers or more. `[PRÜFEN: Follower-Zahl]`
- **Audio:** in-app library only; business accounts may have a restricted library. `[PRÜFEN: Kontotyp]`
- **AI:** no AI voice or AI face of the founder. Label realistic AI visuals with Meta's AI tag.
- **No paid boost:** Meta runs no social-issue ads in the EU (see `knowledge/markets/germany-eu.md`, section `## Rechtliche Kurzreferenz für Outreach`). Growth comes from organic Reels and Collab posts.
- **Collab posts:** up to 5 collaborators; both sides need business or creator profiles. `[PRÜFEN: in-app für beide Konten]` Fallback: a Story with link sticker.

## Hooks

Three layers, all within the first 1–3 s:

1. **First frame** already in motion and human or concrete: a face mid-sentence, a hand writing, the real place of the problem. Never a logo, intro, or bare UI (cursor in a form gives no stakes); the screen is the payoff.
2. **Spoken line** of 8 words or fewer (fits 3 s) that names the viewer's problem or opens a question the Reel answers; never announces an operation ("Ich tippe …").
3. **On-screen text** of 3–7 words, visible for 2–3 s.

Never open with "Hallo, ich bin …". Hook types: Problem, Local, Story, Myth, Curiosity, Demonstration, Contrarian, Question, Reply, Behind the scenes. Project hooks live in `workspace/projects/<slug>/hook-library.md` when present.

Hook hygiene:

- The Reel delivers a step, never promises impact ("so schreibst du der richtigen Stelle", not "so änderst du Politik").
- Rotate hook types. Reuse a winning hook with a new topic, not a new hook with the same topic.
- Every Reel has one element only the founder could show (own scene, own build, own place, consented real material) and one sendable element (a fact people forward, a scene that fits a friend, a demo someone can use right away).
- Ending: `Link im Profil` plus a Story link sticker on posting day, or an offline act ("schreib's auf, ab in den Briefkasten").

## Script templates

Write spoken text verbatim in short main clauses, built on one concrete scene rather than a contrast slogan. Mark every live result, number and fact with `[PRÜFEN: …]` until checked; quote a number a site displays exactly as displayed.

- **`on-camera-demo`, 15–30 s (problem → demo → step).** 0–2 s hook, face close, text overlay. 2–8 s one sentence of tension. 8–22 s cut to screen: input typed or spoken, result appears, waits sped up. 22–28 s back to face: the viewer's step in one sentence. 28–30 s "Link im Profil."
- **`on-camera-story`, 30–60 s (scene → tension → turn → payoff → handoff).** 0–3 s story hook. 3–15 s concrete scene with one detail (place, sentence, object). 15–30 s tension. 30–45 s turn with about 5 s of product B-roll. 45–55 s payoff: one defined number with source, a consented user voice, or, when neither is verified, the viewer's concrete step. 55–60 s open question or "Link im Profil"; loop into the first line if natural.
- **`faceless-screen`, 15–25 s (problem → demo → step).** 0–2 s real-world B-roll of the problem (stop, pothole, hand writing) with an on-screen tension line; founder's own voiceover names the problem or question. 2–15 s payoff: real screen recording, zoomed to the active field, dead time cut, mechanical input silent, captions burned in. 15–22 s the real-world step (hand, paper, place). 22–25 s on-screen domain plus "Link im Profil". Text under 20 % of the frame.
- **`faceless-broll`, 10–20 s.** B-roll the founder films. Three text beats of 7 words or fewer, 2–3 s each, plus voiceover or real ambient sound and an optional low-volume library track. End on a shot that loops to the start.
- **`reply-to-comment`, 20–45 s.** Native "reply with Reel" so the comment sticker shows. 0–2 s read the question aloud. 2–30 s answer by showing. 30–40 s thank by first name only if public and harmless, then point to the next question. Source: real comments `[SIGNAL: Kommentar]`.

## Filming checklist

1. Batch: write 3 scripts in 20 min, film them in one 60–90 min session, edit in Instagram's Edits app (teleprompter, auto-captions, watermark-free export).
2. Frame: 9:16, 1080p or more, phone at eye level, face in the upper third, keep the bottom ~20 % and right edge free of key text.
3. Light and sound: face a window, no backlight; quiet room, phone or clip-on mic within 50 cm. Sound matters more than image.
4. First frame in motion; cut every pause over 0.5 s in the first 5 s.
5. Captions always on and proofread; they also feed search.
6. Screen recordings use a test issue: no real user data, emails or addresses on screen.
7. No identifiable bystanders without consent (KUG §22); no real letters, names or messages without written consent.
8. Before posting: cover frame, keyword caption line, 0–3 hashtags, alt text, no watermark, no keyword CTA, facts checked against evidence, Story with link sticker ready.

## Face vs faceless test

- **Unit:** matched pairs. Same topic, same hook wording (spoken vs on-screen), length within ±5 s, same caption template; only the format differs (on-camera vs faceless). Change one variable per pair.
- **Volume:** 4 pairs = 8 Reels at 2 a week; alternate which format goes first; same weekday slot. With 1,000+ followers, post both as Trial Reels first.
- **Track only:** skip rate (or 3-s hold), sends per reach, link taps or DMs with a real issue, plus production minutes.
- **Decision after 4 pairs:** a format wins if it is better on sends per reach and skip rate in at least 3 of 4 pairs without losing on link taps or DMs. On a tie, keep the format the founder actually keeps producing. A DM or Collab request from an NGO or creator outweighs reach numbers; note which format triggered it.
- n=4 is directional, not significant. Re-test after 3 months.

## Link paths and DMs

- Default: bio link (homepage or current campaign page) plus "Link im Profil" plus a Story link sticker on posting day. Clickable caption links are only a limited test; do not rely on them.
- Comment-keyword auto-DM tools are not used: the keyword CTA costs recommendation reach, the tool adds GDPR processor duties, and it reads as a funnel.
- Viewers who DM a real issue get a personal answer with the link; they asked.
- Cold DMs to non-followers likely arrive as text-only message requests: DM 1 works without link or clip (see the creators persona file).

## Metrics log

The project's `experiments.md` holds a `## Reel-Test` table, logged once 7 days after posting:

## Instagram-Basis

When the account has no base yet, the first content slot is the `Instagram-Basis` pack: bio (one line what, one line for whom, "Link im Profil"-ready, within the in-app character limit `[PRÜFEN: Zeichenlimit]`), 2 carousel texts to pin (why it exists; how it works in steps), and 1 demo Reel (`on-camera-demo` or `faceless-screen`). Reference `workspace/projects/<slug>/instagram-start.md` when present; reprint only a changed bio. Instagram DMs to creators wait for this base; LinkedIn and email outreach never waits for Instagram.

## Repurposing

- Share the source anchor, not the wording: a LinkedIn explainer becomes a Reel demo; a Reel story becomes a LinkedIn text post with a real photo.
- Never upload the same export twice on Instagram.
- A native Reel on LinkedIn only with LinkedIn-native text, labelled as a test.

## Legal and ethics

- Say "mein Projekt" in caption or video (own-product labelling). Collab posts and anything paid or reciprocal need a labelling check per account.
- Written consent for any real letter, message or face; anonymise screenshots.
- No impact claims; every number carries its definition, date and source or a `[PRÜFEN: …]`.
- Compact rules: `knowledge/markets/germany-eu.md` section `## Rechtliche Kurzreferenz für Outreach`. Risk guidance, not legal advice.
