# Phone

Use calls where a warm path, explicit invitation, existing relationship, or well-supported business relevance makes synchronous contact reasonable.

- Prepare one reason for calling and one question.
- Treat the first call as a relevance check, not a forced demo.
- Log permission, commitments, and requested follow-up.
- Do not assume a job posting, website visit, or broad business category creates legal permission.

German telephone outreach is context-sensitive under [UWG section 7](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/uwg_2004/__7.html). Treat this file as a risk flag, not legal advice.
