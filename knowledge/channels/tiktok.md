# TikTok research boundary

TikTok is a future Sales OS adapter. This file supports policy checks and content planning only; it does not imply a native sourcing, publishing, messaging, or analytics workflow.

## Platform-native content

- Start with one real founder observation, work scene, demonstration, or problem pattern.
- Use a vertical 9:16 frame, at least 720p, sound or dialogue, captions, and an intentionally human rather than overproduced presentation.
- State the proposition in the opening seconds, then use a hook, useful body, and one clear close.
- Prepare several honest hooks or treatments and learn from actual watch and response data; there is no perfect reusable script.
- Sales OS may draft the hook, spoken script, shot list, on-screen text, caption, disclosure checklist, and variants. The founder records, verifies, and publishes manually.

TikTok describes these as starting practices rather than guarantees. Source: [Creative best practices](https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article/creative-best-practices?lang=en) (verified 2026-08-21).

## Commercial and AI disclosure

- Promoting yourself, your own company, or your service requires TikTok's content disclosure setting; the post is labeled promotional content.
- Promotional content should use the Commercial Music Library unless the necessary music rights are otherwise secured.
- Realistic AI-generated or significantly edited people or scenes require a clear AI disclosure. Do not fabricate a client, endorsement, result, conversation, or founder appearance.

Sources: [commercial-content disclosure](https://support.tiktok.com/en/business-and-creator/creator-and-business-accounts/promoting-a-brand-product-or-service?invalid_lang=ms), [commercial music](https://support.tiktok.com/en/business-and-creator/creator-and-business-accounts/commercial-use-of-music-on-tiktok/), and [AI-generated content rules](https://www.tiktok.com/community-guidelines/en/integrity-authenticity/) (verified 2026-08-21).

## Guardrails

- No automated account operation, bulk commercial distribution, fake engagement, spam accounts, or engagement manipulation.
- No automatic publishing, commenting, direct messaging, following, or audience interaction.
- No unlicensed music, undisclosed commercial content, or undisclosed realistic AI media.
- Human review and action are mandatory.

Instagram remains a separate future adapter. Do not reuse TikTok assumptions as Instagram rules without current first-party verification.
