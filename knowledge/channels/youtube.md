# YouTube public-comment boundary

Use this file only for a verified, individual public-comment draft. It is not a general YouTube sourcing, publishing, messaging, or analytics adapter.

- Verify the relevant video segment or Short, description, target comment, and surrounding replies. Never imply Thomas watched the whole video if only a title, excerpt, or transcript fragment was available.
- Address the creator's actual point or reply directly to the intended commenter's point. A short, specific addition or sincere question is enough; do not use a stock reaction to get noticed.
- Avoid URLs, channel promotion, and requests to move to DM in contact-warming comments. Links may be held by channel moderation; URLs in Shorts comments are non-clickable.
- YouTube treats high-volume, repetitive, or deceptive traffic-driving comments as spam. Prepare one distinct contribution for manual review and posting.

Sources: [Spam Policy](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/2801973?hl=en), [comment settings](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9483359?hl=en), [link behavior](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/13748639?hl=en), verified 2026-09-22. Recheck current behavior and channel rules before use.
