# Signal-led outreach

A signal is a dated observation that may make a conversation relevant now. Record:

- Source and observation date.
- What was actually observed.
- Why it may matter to this person.
- Confidence and expiry/review date.
- Whether mentioning the signal would feel transparent or invasive.

Signals prioritize research; they do not prove purchase intent. Public posts, explicit questions, referrals, and direct product engagement are generally easier to reference honestly than inferred tracking or third-party surveillance data.

Never turn a signal into a fabricated personal claim. The target's current context remains a hypothesis until they confirm it.
