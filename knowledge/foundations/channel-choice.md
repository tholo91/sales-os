# Choose the conversation surface

Choose the smallest surface where the person already expects this kind of exchange. The channel is a testable choice, not a founder preference or a permanent strategy.

| Situation | Default surface | First human move |
|---|---|---|
| You know a credible mutual contact | Personal network | Ask for insight or a low-pressure introduction. |
| The person recently discussed the problem publicly | Their existing discussion | Add a useful, stand-alone response before asking for contact. |
| The person has visible professional context but no warm path | LinkedIn | Reference one real trigger and ask permission for one concrete example. |
| You have an expected, permissioned professional route | Email | State why them, one reason for contact, and one small CTA. |
| The person invited a synchronous conversation | Phone or call | Confirm relevance with one question; do not force a demo. |

Before choosing, verify four things: the person's actual behaviour, relationship temperature, market and platform risk, and whether the first move would still be useful without a product mention. If those point to different channels, prefer the lower-pressure route.

Do not use ads to decide this. Paid acquisition belongs only after the audience, message, conversion path, and success measure are already clear.
