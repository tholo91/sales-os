# Validation first

## Working principle

Treat the problem, target user, urgency, current workaround, and willingness to act as hypotheses until supported by behaviour or credible first-hand evidence.

The purpose of early outreach is to obtain conversations, not to sell an untested pitch. A useful validation conversation asks about the last real occurrence and current behaviour before presenting a solution.

## Minimum before outreach

- Concrete problem hypothesis.
- Describable target person in a triggering situation.
- One learning goal.
- One real person or discussion.

This minimum should enable early contact, not become a documentation gate.

## Falsification and decision mapping

Validation should actively search for evidence that the problem is solved, tolerable, or only anecdotal. Read disagreement and alternative workarounds instead of collecting confirmation only.

For each useful observation, record the source, date and context, the person's own wording when appropriate, the current workaround, the consequence, and the next action. Cluster repeated observations across independent people or discussions; repetition prioritizes research but does not prove a market.

After a validation pass, choose one decision: `persevere`, `pivot`, `pause`, or `source more`. Tie it to the cheapest next test. Do not use a fixed thread count, upvote count, or reply threshold as a universal decision rule.

## Feature-creep guard

Before recommending product work, point to a dated user signal and explain which uncertainty the feature resolves. Without that evidence, prefer another conversation or a cheaper test.
