# Persona: NGOs and civic organisations

Load for campaigners at large NGOs and campaign platforms, comms and social leads, small Vereine and Bürgerinitiativen, umbrella networks (incl. Freiwilligenagenturen), political-education actors (bpb, Landeszentralen, VHS; schools only via teachers' known contacts) and Partnerschaften für Demokratie (PfD) coordinators. Project facts come from the project's `positioning.md`; gap markers follow `knowledge/foundations/human-writing.md` `## Draft-first and gap markers`. A lead is a dated next commitment, not a friendly reply.

## Who and what they get flooded with

| Sub-persona | Flooded with | Replies to | Kills it |
|---|---|---|---|
| Campaigner, large NGO/platform | tool vendors, agency pitches, cooperation requests | their *current* campaign and target, one mechanism, a bounded test without procurement | "KI-Tool für NGOs", feature lists, "Partnerschaft" without scope |
| Comms/social lead | tool and influencer pitches, content overload | a finished asset idea for their next post, one screenshot, "kostenlos, keine Werbung" | "spread the word", long text, AI hype |
| Small Verein/BI | little vendor noise, almost no time (evenings, weekends) | real local knowledge, proximity, "ich baue, ihr gebt frei" | sales tone, a tool they must manage, national framing |
| Umbrella network | member requests, policy work, events | a short ready text that fits an existing format (newsletter, Praxisbeispiel, session) | asking for endorsement or member push |
| Political education | project and material offers | a learning format ("Wer ist zuständig: Bund, Land, Kommune?") | campaign language, preset positions (Beutelsbacher Konsens) |
| PfD coordinator | local Vereine, programme reporting | a free participation format for Jugendforum or Demokratiekonferenz | anything party-near |

Realism: large-NGO tests take weeks (brand, legal, procurement review); aim for one per quarter. Local organisations and PfDs give faster first references.

## What earns a reply

- Open with their dated campaign moment (Anhörung, Ratssitzung, Gesetzentwurf, Haushalt, Aktionstag, Wahl). A tool pitch without a moment gets parked.
- Non-partisanship in the first lines: "überparteilich", "jede Person schreibt ihre eigene Sicht", "an die Zuständigen, unabhängig von der Partei". Gemeinnützigkeit anxiety is high in 2025–2026; never suggest targeting one party.
- Sell quality per action, not volume: self-written personal messages vs form mails or Musterbrief PDFs. US survey numbers are background only, never in outreach.
- Pre-empt "KI-Briefflut" in one sentence: nothing is sent automatically; the person edits and sends.
- Respect the incumbent workaround and ask about it in the call (`## Call kit`), not in the first message. Do not claim to be first.
- Disclose trade-offs early (for example: no supporter data export [PRÜFEN: gilt für dieses Projekt?]).
- Make the core forwardable: two sentences that work unchanged in a Vorstand WhatsApp group or internal Slack.

## Channel and entry path

1. Warm intro or Thomas's own network first (`knowledge/channels/personal-network.md`); in person for local groups (open meetings, Infostände, Freiwilligenagentur events).
2. LinkedIn or Instagram DM to the named person with a person-specific reason (Instagram suits social leads and BIs).
3. Published partner or submission routes (a platform's partner inbox, a newsletter submission address, event calls).
- No info@ blasts, no sequences. One purpose-fitting email to a route the organisation publishes for this is Thomas's judgment call. Never switch from LinkedIn silence to a cold info@ email.

## Ask ladder

1. Permission: "Soll ich dir zeigen, wie so eine Aktion für [Kampagne] aussähe?" Referral fallback: "Falls das bei euch jemand anderes entscheidet, sag gern wer."
2. Fit, after they have seen it: "Wäre so eine Aktion vor [Datum] für euch sinnvoll, oder macht ihr das anders?"
3. Tiny free pilot: one campaign, one channel segment, 2–4 weeks, an agreed success signal, a decision date. Small Vereine get the light version: Thomas builds, they approve, one share date, one "wie lief's?" message afterwards.

Lead = a test date, a named owner plus a 20-minute call, or a page approved plus a share date.

## Give-first asset

- A prefilled, unlisted page or a screenshot sketch for their current campaign, without their name or logo until they agree [PRÜFEN: unlisted möglich?].
- After a yes: a 3-line "zum Weiterleiten" text plus one screenshot, not a deck (share kit in `knowledge/strategies/follow-up.md`).
- After a pilot: opt-in reply stories as reportable output, labelled output, not impact (PHINEO terms) [PRÜFEN: Einwilligung zum Teilen]. Never promise replies from politicians.

## Call kit

- **They ask:** Who is behind it, how is it funded? What happens to supporters' data, do we get it? Isn't this AI spam? Is it non-partisan, any Gemeinnützigkeit risk? Can we brand or moderate it; which levels (Bund/Land/Kommune) are covered [PRÜFEN: …]? What numbers will we see? What if the project stops?
- **Ask them:** "Wann habt ihr zuletzt Leute an Abgeordnete schreiben lassen? Wie lief das, woran habt ihr gemerkt, ob es gewirkt hat?" · "Was ist euer nächster Kampagnenmoment in den nächsten 8 Wochen, wer ist die Zielperson?" · "Wer muss zustimmen: Kampagne, Kommunikation, Datenschutz, Vorstand?" · "Was müsste nach einem Test passiert sein, damit ihr es wieder macht?" · "Was wäre ein No-Go: Branding, Daten, Tonalität?"
- **Have ready:** a live demo on their topic, tested end to end; privacy and AI-transparency links; one dated, sourced numbers line [PRÜFEN: …]; a plain cannot-do list (e.g. no export, no auto-send, no guaranteed effect); a one-paragraph pilot proposal.
- **Close:** "Sollen wir das für [Anlass] testen? Dann baue ich bis [Datum] die Seite, du gibst frei, und am [Datum] schauen wir 20 Minuten drauf." If not ready: one named owner and one date, or record no-fit. RC within 24 h restates owner, dates, success signal and the open question; never put supporter data in it.

## Risks

- Legal and platform rules (UWG §7 email routes, GDPR Art. 9, Werbekennzeichnung if any benefit flows): `knowledge/markets/germany-eu.md` `## Rechtliche Kurzreferenz für Outreach`. Clarify controller vs Auftragsverarbeitung before any pilot that touches supporter data [PRÜFEN: …].
- Never: "Druck auf Partei X", invented logos or "bereits genutzt von", implied endorsement beyond what a supporter has agreed, impact claims, data collection from minors, campaign framing with education actors.
- Time sink: a large-NGO test can eat weeks; cap it at one per quarter and run local references in parallel.

## Where to find them

- Thomas's own network and past contacts first; then current campaign pages with a dated moment.
- Published partner routes of campaign platforms; umbrella newsletter submission routes and event calls (e.g. BBE).
- PfD Koordinierungs- und Fachstellen via demokratie-leben.de, starting locally [PRÜFEN: Ansprechperson]; Freiwilligenagenturen.
- Local events in person; BIs via their public social accounts on the issue.
- Record the route plus the campaign moment and its date. No list scraping or bulk enrichment.
