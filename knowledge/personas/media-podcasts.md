# Persona: media and podcasts

Load for journalists and editors (national, local/regional, agency desks), podcast hosts and producers, newsletter makers, radio and public broadcasters incl. youth formats, and civic-tech reporters. Email pitches also follow `knowledge/strategies/editorial-pitch.md`; gap markers follow `knowledge/foundations/human-writing.md` `## Draft-first and gap markers`. Evidence: news aktuell Medien-Trendmonitor 2025 (DACH), Cision 2026, Muck Rack 2025/2026 (US-heavy), Presserat; details in the research file.

## Who and what they get flooded with

| Segment | Flooded with | Replies to | Kills it |
|---|---|---|---|
| National/politics desk | party and NGO statements, studies, lobby invites | a defined data point tied to a parliamentary moment | "Startup stellt sich vor", repeating an earlier angle |
| Local/regional desk, agency regional desk | municipal releases, club news | people and numbers from their area, a face for a photo | national copy with the city name swapped |
| Podcast host/producer | template guest pitches | an episode idea with title and 3 talking points tied to past episodes | "Ich würde gern mal in euren Podcast", no sign of listening |
| Newsletter maker | "please feature us" requests | something readers can do plus one paste-ready fact | feature request without having read the issue |
| Radio/public broadcaster | institutional PR, NGO campaigns | a listener problem, a voice for O-Ton, timing near elections | anything that sounds party-near or like a single-product ad |
| Civic-tech/digital reporter | AI launches, "democracy tech" hype | design decisions, data protection, failure modes | marketing language, unverifiable privacy claims |

## What earns a reply

- **Second-day story rule:** once a product has been covered, pitch only a defined data point, a consenting protagonist, a local pattern, a verified calendar hook, a counter-thesis or an honest failure. "This tool exists" is not news twice.
- First sentence names their audience or region; the second gives the fact. 82% reject pitches that miss their audience or beat (Cision 2026).
- Facts over storytelling: DACH journalists want facts, data, exclusives and fast answers; storytelling ranks low (26%).
- Every number carries definition, date and n. Drafts or sign-ups are not outcomes.
- Subject line = honest headline; 120–180 words; kit link instead of attachments (one small photo is fine); one sentence only the founder could write, since 53% oppose AI-generated pitches (Cision 2026).
- National desks get the lowest priority until a defensible data cut exists; local desks, agency regional desks and podcasts with a loyal audience come first.

## Channel and entry path

- **Default:** 1:1 email to a published editorial or author address (89% of DACH journalists prefer email, 8% social).
- **Warm-up:** a substantive comment on their piece on LinkedIn or Bluesky before the email; they use social mostly to watch and research.
- LinkedIn DM pitch only when they invite pitches there or are creator journalists/newsletter makers working on LinkedIn; respect "keine PR per DM".
- Podcasts: the show's contact or guest address or the producer, not the host's private DM. A show that already covered you gets an update note, not a guest pitch.
- State or regional hook: pitch the agency regional desk once instead of many papers. A clean aggregated dataset can go to a local-journalism network (e.g. CORRECTIV.Lokal) via a named contact, not the tip address.
- A warm intro beats every cold route. Sie by default; du only where their own channel uses it. Phone only for radio interviews, existing relationships or when offered.

## Ask ladder

1. Fit: "Wäre das was für [Ressort/Ausgabe]?" · Podcast: "Passt das grundsätzlich zu euch? Dann schicke ich drei mögliche Fragen." · Newsletter: "Wäre das was für deine Mitmach-Ecke? Dann schick ich dir zwei Sätze zum Übernehmen."
2. Material: offer only what already exists, e.g. "Soll ich Ihnen die Auswertung mit Methodik [PRÜFEN: Methodik dokumentiert?] schicken?" or the contact to a consenting protagonist.
3. Commitment: background talk, phone O-Ton slot or episode booking with a date.

Lead = material requested, interview or episode booked with a date, or saved as a source for a planned piece.

## Give-first asset

- A checked data point with definition, date and n; aggregated, opt-in only, minimum cell size [PRÜFEN: Schwelle, z. B. n≥10]. Political topics are special-category data.
- A consenting protagonist, with documented consent before any name is shared.
- A downloadable kit link in the signature: one-page fact sheet with number definitions, photos with rights notice ("frei für redaktionelle Nutzung"), prior coverage, methodology. 76% want a download link, not "auf Anfrage".
- An exclusive window for one outlet at a time ("bis [Tag] exklusiv für Sie, danach biete ich es anderen an"); embargoes are voluntary in Germany.

## Message skeleton

> Betreff: [Ort]: [Zahl] [Einheit] seit [Monat], die meisten zu [Thema]
>
> Guten Tag [Frau/Herr Nachname],
>
> in [Ort] haben seit [Monat] [Zahl] Menschen [was sie mit dem Projekt getan haben] [PRÜFEN: Zahl, Definition, Mindestfallzahl, Stand-Datum]. Ich schreibe Ihnen, weil Sie [SIGNAL: Artikel und Datum] über genau dieses Thema berichtet haben.
>
> [Projekt in einem Satz] [PRÜFEN: keine Wirkungsbehauptung]. [SIGNAL: ein Satz, den nur ich schreiben kann]
>
> Eine Nutzerin aus [Ort] würde mit Ihnen sprechen [PRÜFEN: schriftliche Einwilligung liegt vor]. Wäre das was für [Ressort/Ausgabe]?
>
> Viele Grüße, [Vorname Nachname]<br>
> [Telefon] · Zahlen mit Definition und Fotos: [Link Pressekit]

Variants: podcast = subject "Folgenidee: [Frage als Titel]", open on [SIGNAL: Folge und Punkt], du if the show uses it. Newsletter = LinkedIn DM under 60 words, only after a public comment. No city cut = lead with the protagonist. Without a verified local data point or a consenting protagonist, use a verified calendar hook (Sitzungswoche, Ratssitzung, local MdB) or recommend not pitching yet; "X Entwürfe seit Mai" is not news. If the draft works unchanged for another outlet, it is not ready.

## Touch plan

Uses the `Media email pitch` and `Podcast or newsletter` rows in `knowledge/strategies/follow-up.md`. Override: never a phone chase; after coverage, thank them the same day, share it publicly, ask once for an online link, and send an impact note only with real numbers.

## Call kit

- **They ask:** What exactly does the number count? Who funds this, are you partisan? Does it work (documented, consented cases only, no causal claims)? Isn't this AI flooding offices? What data is stored where (answer from the processing register, not memory)? Can I talk to a user or try it live?
- **Ask them:** format and length, deadline, angle, what they need (data, photos, protagonist), other voices they speak to, whether quotes go out for Autorisierung (ask, don't demand).
- **Have ready:** fact sheet with number definitions and dates, photos with rights notice, a fictional sample labelled as such, consenting protagonists, prior coverage, methodology, one independent voice [PRÜFEN: wer, mit Zustimmung]. Demo with a neutral prepared example from their beat or region, never their personal political concern.
- **Close:** "Was brauchen Sie bis wann von mir?", then repeat deadline and deliverables. RC within 24 h: "Wie besprochen: (1) [Zahl + Definition + Stand], (2) Pressekit: [Link], (3) Kontakt zu [Protagonist:in] nach Zustimmung bis [Datum]."

## Risks

- Legal and platform rules (UWG §7 email routes, Pressekodex separation, GDPR Art. 9): `knowledge/markets/germany-eu.md` `## Rechtliche Kurzreferenz für Outreach`. Keep pitches purely editorial, individually sent, to published addresses; honour "keine Mails" at once.
- Never: impact claims, undefined numbers, partisan or campaign framing (public broadcasters must stay balanced), asking to approve the article (quotes only), gifts or counter-favours, a donation link in a pitch, attachment dumps, mass-mail feel, user material without explicit consent, paid placement mixed with an editorial pitch.
- Wasted hours: log each pitch (outlet, segment, angle, reply, publication date, effect in the 4 days after); after about 10 pitches keep the segment with the best result per hour.

## Where to find them

- Bylines on recent pieces about the issue; desk and author addresses from Impressum or Redaktion pages.
- Agency regional desks for state hooks [PRÜFEN: aktuelle Adresse des Landesbüros]; local-journalism networks only with an aggregated opt-in dataset.
- Podcasts: contact or guest pages plus one or two recent episodes actually heard.
- Newsletter makers on LinkedIn, Substack and Steady; radio via the programme's editorial address (morning show, Landespolitik, Netz/Digital), regional first.
- Record the route plus two notes on what they cover. No list scraping or bulk enrichment.
