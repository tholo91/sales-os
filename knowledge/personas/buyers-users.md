# Persona: buyers and users

Load for a buyer or end user of a commercial project (HeySpeak, NomadSpots, Spießer, services) who has a real trigger. This file is generic. The concrete trigger, wording, asset and proof come from the project's `icp.md` (`## Triggering situation`, `## Current workaround`, `## Where they already talk and learn`), `positioning.md` (`## Problem in the buyer's language`, `## Buying triggers`, `## Recorded proof that may be used`, `## Message boundaries`) and, once one exists, `offer.md`. Gap markers follow `knowledge/foundations/human-writing.md` `## Draft-first and gap markers`. Evidence: Gong, Lavender, Instantly and Belkins (observational vendor data from B2B email); applying it to a solo founder's DMs is inference.

## Who and what they get flooded with

- **Buyer:** decides and pays (founder, team lead, freelancer, small-business owner). Gets automated sequences, a pitch right after accepting a connection, "quick call?" asks, calendar links and AI flattery.
- **End user:** uses the product (app user, remote worker, member of a community). Gets app promos, referral spam and "please download" DMs from strangers.
- Both ignore product biographies, "wir" from a solo founder, feature lists, demo asks before the message has shown relevance, and messages with several asks.
- A friendly reply does not show purchase intent (`knowledge/strategies/follow-up.md` `## Continuing by role`).

## What earns a reply

- Line 1 is their trigger: one dated thing they wrote, asked or did that matches the ICP trigger. If the sentence would work for someone else, rewrite it.
- Line 2 says what changes for them, in their words from `positioning.md`, not how the product works. Gong found that pitching cuts replies by up to 57%.
- 40–90 words. Gong's 28M emails: 3–4 sentences and 100 words or fewer had the best reply rates.
- End with one easy question. For buyers, a question about their situation ("Wie löst du das im Moment?") often works better than permission to send something, because the answer is also discovery evidence.
- Write within a few days of the signal. Use real proof only. "Ich baue das gerade allein" is honest credibility, not an apology.

## Channel and entry path

- **Warm first (Tier 1):** existing users and feedback-givers who opted in to contact, people who replied before, 1st-degree contacts with a fitting signal, and referrals with the referrer's permission (Intro kit in `knowledge/channels/personal-network.md`).
- **LinkedIn:** a substantive public comment 1–7 days before; a connection request without a note; save the 3 monthly notes for top targets; a DM within a day of acceptance that leads with the signal, never a pitch.
- **Communities** (from `icp.md`): reply publicly first and DM only where invited. Never send an uninvited Reddit DM.
- **Email:** no cold promotional email without consent. Use only a published inquiry route that fits the purpose, or reply after they asked.
- **Consumer end users** (apps): reach them in the product, in the project's own community and in public threads. Never DM strangers asking them to download or review.
- du/Sie: mirror their channel. Without a signal, use du for startups, freelancers and creators and Sie for established businesses.

## Ask ladder

1. **Interest or situation (default):** "Wie löst du das im Moment?" · "Ist [Problem] bei dir gerade Thema?" · "Soll ich dir [Asset] für deinen Fall schicken?"
2. **Asset after a yes:** send it within 24 h with one question about fit ("Passt das zu deinem Ablauf?").
3. **Short call (15–20 min):** only after a reply. Run it with `knowledge/strategies/discovery-call.md`. Name the price in the first substantive conversation (`knowledge/strategies/pricing.md`).
4. **Commitment:** a dated test or pilot with a decision date (`knowledge/strategies/demo-and-pilot.md`), a paid pilot, use by a named date, or an intro to the person who decides.

Lead = one of the dated commitments in step 4, or a call booked for a date. "Klingt spannend" is not a lead.

## Give-first asset

- Take it from the project's `positioning.md` or `offer.md`. If it has not been built yet, mark it `[PRÜFEN: Asset gebaut?]`.
- Options: a sample result for their case, a 45–60 s screen recording of the product doing their task, test access or an invite, or one checked data point from the project evidence.
- Build custom assets only after interest, or for at most 3 top targets a week.
- Never: free consulting to get a foot in the door, their data without consent, or fake "normally costs X" discounts.

## Message skeleton

> Moin [Vorname],
> du hast [SIGNAL: am [Datum] geschrieben, dass [Problem in ihren Worten]].
> Ich baue gerade allein [Projekt in einem Satz aus positioning.md], damit [PRÜFEN: Nutzen in ihren Worten, keine Wirkungsbehauptung].
> Wie löst du das im Moment? *(oder: Soll ich dir [Asset aus positioning.md] für deinen Fall schicken?)*
> Grüße aus Bremen, Thomas

Pick exactly one question. For an organisation, `Falls das bei euch jemand anderes entscheidet, sag gern wer.` may follow it. The skeleton is about 50–70 words. If it reads the same after removing the product name, line 1 is too weak.

## Touch plan

Override: the default LinkedIn DM row in `knowledge/strategies/follow-up.md` applies (F1 carries the asset or one new fact). Stricter: consumer end users get at most F1 and no F2 by DM, and emails sent through an inquiry route get no F2. After a call, RC within 24 h, then follow the default table's after-a-call row.

## Call kit

- **They ask:** What does it cost? Who are you, and will this still exist in six months? What happens to my data? How is it different from [Alternative aus positioning.md]? How much setup work is it? Who else uses it? (Name only recorded proof.)
- **Ask them** (pick 3; `discovery-call.md`): When did it last happen? What is the current workaround, and what does it cost? Who else is involved or decides? What have you already paid for or tried? What would need to be true to test or pay?
- **Have ready:** the offer scope with what is included and what is not, one price stated plainly, a demo on their case (only after you understand their workflow), one honest limitation, a one-line privacy answer, and a pilot paragraph with a decision date.
- **Close:** "Wollen wir [Umfang] bis [Datum] testen und am [Datum] entscheiden, ob es sich lohnt?" RC within 24 h: their goal in their words, next steps with an owner and date each, and the link.
- **Price pushback:** `knowledge/strategies/pricing.md` `## When the buyer says the price is too high`; other pushback: `knowledge/strategies/objection-handling.md`.

## Risks

- Legal and platform rules (UWG §7 email routes, no engagement automation, LinkedIn note limit): `knowledge/markets/germany-eu.md` `## Rechtliche Kurzreferenz für Outreach`.
- Never: invented customers, logos, discounts, urgency or scarcity; identical texts or sequences; treating friends' politeness as validation.
- Use testimonials and user stories only with documented consent.
- Building features for one prospect: check the feature-creep guard in `knowledge/foundations/validation-first.md` first.
- Wasted hours: if a batch of 10–15 produces no substantive replies, change the angle, not the volume.

## Where to find them

- Start with `icp.md` `## Where they already talk and learn`, then Tier 1: existing users, feedback-givers who opted in, people who replied, warm intros.
- Public signals: LinkedIn posts or comments that describe the trigger, and community threads (Reddit, Slack, forums) where they ask for a workaround or alternative.
- Events and meetups where the ICP actually shows up. Mention a shared event only if it really happened.
- Record the route, the trigger signal and its date. No list scraping or bulk enrichment.
