# Persona: funders and foundations

Load for programme officers at private foundations and Bürgerstiftungen, public programmes, civic-tech and open-source funders, and (low priority, warm intro only) corporate foundations and CSR. Small donors are reached in the product and in milestone posts, never by DM or cold email. Gap markers follow `knowledge/foundations/human-writing.md` `## Draft-first and gap markers`. Programme facts below are route examples dated 2026-10-04; project-specific funders, amounts and fiscal-host details live in the project's workspace files.

## Who and what they get flooded with

| Segment | Flooded with | Replies to | Kills it |
|---|---|---|---|
| Private foundation officer | mass inquiries off-purpose, sketches without an amount, "core funding" asks | a time-limited project fitting a named programme, with amount and co-funding | Serienbrief, attachment dump, pitching an operative foundation |
| Bürgerstiftung | sponsoring and personnel-cost requests | a concrete local, volunteer-run action with material costs only | salaries, overhead, vague "kleine Aktion" |
| Public programme | formally incomplete applications | an exact eligibility question in official Sprechstunden or info sessions | pitching staff outside the procedure |
| Civic-tech / open-source funder | end-user apps dressed as infrastructure | a reusable open component, repo over slides | product pitch, no licence |
| CSR (warm intro only) | sponsoring and logo deals | one concrete in-kind ask with a number | anything party-near, vague "Partnerschaft" |

## What earns a reply

- **A fundable project, not a tool:** one dated project with an amount and a Wirkungslogik (Input → Output → Outcome → Impact, PHINEO Kursbuch Wirkung). A counter is an output.
- **One honest number with its definition** (what it counts, what it does not, date) [PRÜFEN: …]; say openly what is not yet measured and make that gap the reason for the project.
- **Fit via one comparable past grant** [SIGNAL: …] from their project database or annual report, named in your own words.
- **Non-partisanship as a funding condition** (AO §52 Abs. 2 Nr. 24): one safeguards sentence in every touch (no party content, the person decides and sends, no mass mailing).
- **AI drafting gets its own sentence.** Sales OS drafts a scaffold; Thomas rewrites `Bezug zu Ihnen` and the project sentence in his own words before sending: 23% of 527 US foundations would not accept AI-generated applications, 67% are undecided (Candid 2024).
- A co-funding line, even "bisher keine"; one narrow question; a forward request if the person is not responsible.

## Channel and entry path

1. **Träger first:** before naming any fiscal host in a sketch, ask it in writing whether its Satzungszweck covers the project, who signs, fees and whether donations can count as Eigenanteil (project-specific Träger: see the project's workspace persona notes).
2. **Published route first, exactly as described.** Where a funder invites an informal sketch, the sketch is the first touch; asking "Darf ich eine Skizze schicken?" only adds a round trip. Example: Stiftung Mercator takes a 1,000–3,000-character sketch with amount by email to the responsible project manager, review up to 6 weeks [PRÜFEN: Stand 2026-10-04].
3. **No route published:** talk before submitting ("Sandwich": inquiry → short call → informal draft → application). Phone only the switchboard for "Wer ist für [Thema] zuständig?", one question at a time.
4. **Closed doors** (e.g. Robert Bosch Stiftung: calls only; Bertelsmann Stiftung: own projects only [PRÜFEN: Stand 2026-10-04]) open only via their public events, past grantees, board members or a warm intro. Never pitch them by DM or phone.
5. **LinkedIn:** react to a real post or talk with one observation and one question; never raise funding there first.

## Ask ladder

1. "Passt das grundsätzlich in Ihren Bereich?" or "Wer ist bei Ihnen für [Thema] zuständig?"
2. An eligibility question in the Sprechstunde, or the invited sketch by a stated date.
3. A Vorgespräch, informal feedback on a draft, or an invited application with a deadline.

Lead = named responsible person plus a dated Vorgespräch, an invited sketch or application with a deadline, or a referral to a better-fitting funder.

## Give-first asset

Nothing unsolicited: no attachments, no deck, no "take a look at our product". After interest, one page: project title, problem, target group, duration, budget with Eigenanteil, Wirkungslogik in 3 lines, how it is measured, what happens after funding, the number with its definition [PRÜFEN: …].

## Message skeleton

> Betreff: Projektskizze: [Projektname] – [Vorhaben in 5 Wörtern]
>
> Guten Tag [Frau/Herr Nachname],
>
> unten eine kurze Projektskizze, wie auf Ihrer Website beschrieben. Falls das zu Kolleg:innen gehört, freue ich mich über eine Weiterleitung. Träger: [Träger gGmbH] [PRÜFEN: schriftlich bestätigt, wer unterschreibt]. Projektleitung: [Vorname Nachname], [Ort].
>
> Was es gibt: [Projekt in zwei Sätzen, parteineutral; die Person entscheidet und sendet selbst, keine Massenaussendung]. [PRÜFEN: KI-Satz – Vorschlag, Person prüft, nachvollziehbar]
>
> Warum: [Problem und Zielgruppe in einem Satz].
>
> Stand: [Zahl] [Einheit] [PRÜFEN: Stand-Datum, was die Zahl zählt und was nicht]. Was ich noch nicht weiß: [offene Frage]. Genau da setzt das Vorhaben an ([PRÜFEN: Dauer]): 1. [Messung] 2. [Pilot mit Partnern] 3. [Ergebnisse offen veröffentlichen]
>
> Bezug zu Ihnen: [SIGNAL: ein vergleichbares gefördertes Projekt oder Ziel aus ihrem Programm, ein Satz]. Beantragt: [PRÜFEN: Betrag] Euro. Weitere Mittel: [PRÜFEN: Spenden / andere Förderer / bisher keine].
>
> Passt das grundsätzlich in Ihren Bereich?
>
> Viele Grüße aus [Ort]<br>
> [Vorname Nachname] · [Link]

Variants: Bürgerstiftung = one volunteer-run local action with material costs only; public programme = one eligibility question; LinkedIn = observation plus question, no funding. Without a recorded comparable grant the draft stays a skeleton and the next action is finding one grant. If the text works unchanged for another funder, it is not ready.

## Touch plan

Override of the default table in `knowledge/strategies/follow-up.md`: plan about 12 months ahead (boards decide in cycles); question email F1 after about 10 working days in the same thread with one new element; a submitted sketch gets one status question only after the stated review time; the next touch about 2 weeks later changes the route (switchboard, warm intro), never the pressure; stop after 2 unanswered touches per person.
After a rejection: thank them, ask one learning question or a better-fitting funder, and ask whether a twice-yearly update is welcome; add them only on yes.

## Call kit

- **They ask:** Who is the Träger and who signs? What exactly is the time-limited project, what does it cost, who else funds it, what is the Eigenanteil? Who is reached? What is actually sent or done, not just drafted, and how is it measured? Non-partisanship, misuse, privacy, AI errors? What happens after funding, bus factor as a solo founder? How is it different from similar tools?
- **Ask them** (max 3): "Welche Förderlinie passt am ehesten?" · "Woran scheitern Anträge bei Ihnen meistens?" · "Zählen Spenden oder ehrenamtliche Stunden als Eigenanteil?"
- **Have ready:** one page, no deck; a 2-minute pitch (problem → what it does → honest result with its caveat → the one project → the ask); cost line; safeguards sentence; Träger status in one honest sentence; user voices only with consent [PRÜFEN: …].
- **Close:** "Ich schicke Ihnen bis [Datum] die Skizze; Sie geben mir bis ca. [Datum] Rückmeldung zur Passung – passt das?" No fit: "Kennen Sie Förderer, die besser passen?" RC same day: "(1) [Förderlinie/Passung], (2) [Hinweis zum Antrag], (3) ich schicke bis [Datum] [Skizze/Entwurf]. Falls ich etwas falsch verstanden habe, korrigieren Sie mich gern."

## Risks

- Legal and platform rules (UWG §7 email routes, GDPR, Werbekennzeichnung for sponsors): `knowledge/markets/germany-eu.md` `## Rechtliche Kurzreferenz für Outreach`. No cold donation emails to private people; contacts only from team pages or their own publications, source recorded.
- Never: imply a funder or partner already supports the project, undefined or unverified numbers, superlatives, "Austausch" without a question, phoning funders who ask for no contact, submitting AI text unedited, quoting the "up to 90% sorted out" figure as data (it is a practitioner estimate).
- Funds are bound to the agreed purpose; changes need the funder's consent. Name sponsors and in-kind partners visibly.

## Where to find them

- A shortlist of 3–5 funders, each anchored to one comparable past grant from its project database or annual report; drop a funder without one. Ask that grantee how they got in.
- Funder pages "für Antragstellende", team pages for the responsible project manager, programme FAQs and calendars with deadlines [PRÜFEN: jede Frist mit Datum].
- Info sessions, Sprechstunden and the funder's public events; local Bürgerstiftungen and local "Demokratie leben!" Partnerschaften [PRÜFEN: Stand 2026-10-04, Runde ab 2027 geschlossen].
- Civic-tech and open-source funds (e.g. Prototype Fund, NLnet) only for a reusable open component [PRÜFEN: Fristen und Förderfähigkeit, Stand 2026-10-04].
- Record route, source and the comparable grant in the contact file. No list scraping or bulk enrichment.
