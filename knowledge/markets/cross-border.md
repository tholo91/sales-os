# Cross-border adaptation

Before adapting outreach across markets, check:

- Recipient language, role, and actual communication style.
- Channel expectations and legal basis.
- Directness, formality, proof expectations, and acceptable CTA.
- Local buyer process, budget owner, and procurement constraints.

The imported cultural comparisons are hypotheses only. Do not claim universal German or American behaviour.
