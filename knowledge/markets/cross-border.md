# Cross-border adaptation

Use country as context, not as a personality prediction. Before adapting first touch or follow-up, check:

- Sender establishment, recipient's relevant location, and exact channel; do not infer jurisdiction from name, nationality, or language.
- Actual purpose: commercial promotion, civic engagement, editorial story, genuine research, or delivery of an agreed reply.
- Recipient role, audience, language, visible writing, and relationship history.
- Permission and expectations for this channel, scope, and follow-up; public availability or connection acceptance is not general marketing consent.
- Proof needs, workload, decision authority, institutional restrictions, and the smallest useful next step.

Use the applicable German or US market file for channel duties and unresolved context; do not overwrite verified individual preferences with its writing defaults. A German person writing in English may still prefer an informal exchange; a US professor may require formal, careful academic context. These are possible situations, not country rules. Preserve the founder's real voice in either language.

Broad market guidance never overrides a person's current public wording, relationship history, community rules, or legal risk. Record the evidence for formality, directness, humor, greeting, and sign-off separately; leave any unsupported choice neutral.

## Sender duties and uncertain cases

[GDPR Article 3(1)](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2016/679/oj) applies to processing in the context of an EU establishment's activities even when processing occurs outside the EU. A US recipient does not switch those applicable duties off. Conversely, a US sender cannot apply CAN-SPAM alone to every recipient abroad. Determine the applicable national marketing rules for the actual case rather than asserting that UWG always or never applies extraterritorially.

Unknown jurisdiction or ambiguous purpose is a material blocker only when it changes suitability of the proposed channel. Resolve it through targeted research before marking that message ready. Continue work that does not depend on the answer; avoid a new approval step for every draft. A switch from email to DM does not itself resolve legal or platform uncertainty.

## Learning without country stereotypes

Record actual outcomes by project, role, relationship, purpose, channel, market, and ask. Count substantive replies, agreed next steps, negative responses, and stops separately; a polite reply or connection acceptance is not adoption. Show counts and small-sample limits before comparing rates. Country differences are hypotheses to test against comparable contacts, not a guarantee or a reason to intensify unanswered follow-ups.
