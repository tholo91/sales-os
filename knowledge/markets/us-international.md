# US and international

## Communication hypothesis

It can be appropriate to state the desired outcome earlier and use a direct, consultative CTA. Keep the message human and specific; do not convert cultural generalizations into rigid rules.

This file cannot determine familiarity, humor, greetings, sign-offs, or how direct one person wants the message. Current target and channel evidence comes first.

## Compliance boundary

US commercial email is not permissionless simply because the recipient is a business. Follow the current [FTC CAN-SPAM guide](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/can-spam-act-compliance-guide-business), the recipient's jurisdiction, data-source restrictions, and platform rules.
