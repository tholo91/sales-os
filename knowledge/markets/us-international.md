# US and international

## Communication hypothesis

When recipient evidence is thin, test an early explanation of relevance and outcome, plain English, and one direct but easy-to-decline next step. This is an unproven writing default, not evidence that Americans want optimism, informality, faster decisions, or more follow-ups. Do not add superlatives, first-name familiarity, or a meeting request just because the target is US-based.

This file cannot determine familiarity, humor, greetings, sign-offs, or how direct one person wants the message. Current target and channel evidence comes first.

## Compliance boundary

US commercial email is not permissionless simply because the recipient is a business. Follow the current [FTC CAN-SPAM guide](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/can-spam-act-compliance-guide-business), the recipient's jurisdiction, data-source restrictions, and platform rules.

For covered commercial email, CAN-SPAM generally uses an opt-out model rather than Germany's prior-consent rule. It covers individual and B2B email too. Use accurate sender information and subjects, recognizable advertising, a valid postal address, and an easy opt-out. Keep the opt-out operational for at least 30 days and honor requests within 10 business days; Sales OS stops further outreach immediately on an objection. Outsourcing does not remove sender responsibility.

The message's primary purpose determines commercial classification. Genuine civic, editorial, or research content is not automatically a commercial email, but those labels do not exempt a message whose substance promotes a commercial product or service. Social familiarity or LinkedIn connection is not a statutory transactional relationship.

This email guide does not establish how a particular social DM is classified. Read the relevant platform policy separately. US federal email rules also do not settle every state, sector, privacy, provider, or foreign-sender obligation. Do not apply this file to Canada, the UK, or another country just because the message is in English.

For a German/EU-established sender, retain applicable GDPR duties even for US contacts; see `knowledge/markets/cross-border.md`. Each follow-up needs the same applicable channel basis and an honest reason, with no country-based persistence rule.
