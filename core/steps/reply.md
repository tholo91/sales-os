# Reply handling step

1. Read the selected pending action, its `action_id` and `target_ref`, the exact inbound reply or transcript, the preceding message or ask, contact history, channel context, and active positioning and evidence.
2. Preserve the interaction before interpreting it. Separate what the person said from inferred intent and do not upgrade politeness into interest.
3. Classify the immediate conversational job as answer, clarify, deliver a promise, invite a call, prepare an offer, handle an objection, defer, refer, or close.
4. Answer the person's actual point first. Use recorded proof only, mirror their formality and level of detail, and keep one next ask proportional to the signal they gave.
5. For an objection, label the concern and ask or answer only what is needed to understand fit; do not counter every concern, introduce urgency, or discount before value and scope are clear.
6. For interest, earn the smallest concrete commitment: a relevant answer, artifact review, scheduled call, offer input, or decision date. For a decline or no-fit, close cleanly and preserve the stated reason as evidence.
7. Draft exactly one channel-native reply or decide not to reply. External delivery remains manual.
8. After founder confirmation, preserve the reply or no-reply decision as an interaction record and resolve only the selected action. Route a booked conversation to a contact-referenced scheduled-call action, an offer request to a proposal action, and substantive new evidence to an interaction-debrief action. Every new action must reference that interaction, state its concrete reason and due date, and create no generic cadence.
