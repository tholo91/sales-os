# Reply handling step

1. Read the selected pending action when one exists; for an explicit founder request without one, the reported inbound text is the target (no action id). Read the preceding message or ask, contact history, channel context, and active positioning and evidence. Load `knowledge/strategies/recipient-context.md` (`## Five-field check`), the matching channel reference, and market references (`knowledge/markets/germany-eu.md`, `us-international.md`, `cross-border.md`) only for email or cross-border. Load `knowledge/personas/<persona>.md` and the persona section of `workspace/projects/<slug>/personas.md` when present. `connector` and `other` have no persona file: `connector` uses `knowledge/channels/personal-network.md` (`## Intro kit`) and the `Connector after an intro` touch-plan row; `other` uses the `## Drafting card` in `knowledge/strategies/first-touch.md` and the default `LinkedIn DM` row. Use the Intro kit for any intro offer; for an intro to a media, NGO, or funder target, read only that persona's `## What earns a reply` for the blurb's angle. Use `knowledge/strategies/linkedin-public-post.md` (`## Comment-to-DM bridge`) for public comments. Reuse the recipient's recorded role, relationship, possible benefit and barrier, and ask burden; update them only from evidence.
2. Preserve the interaction before interpreting it. Separate what the person said from inferred intent and do not upgrade politeness into interest.
3. Classify the immediate conversational job as answer, clarify, deliver a promise, invite a call, prepare an offer, handle an objection, defer, refer, or close.
4. Answer the person's actual point first. Use recorded proof only, mirror their formality and level of detail, and keep at most one next ask proportional to the signal they gave. Deliver requested material without adding a meeting, share, or introduction ask. A creator's request to see an example is not agreement to endorse it; a researcher's factual reply is not campaign support; a journalist's question is not promised coverage. For buyers, a reply alone does not establish authority, need, or budget.
5. For an objection, label the concern and ask or answer only what is needed to understand fit; do not counter every concern, introduce urgency, or discount before value and scope are clear.
6. For interest, earn the smallest concrete commitment relevant to this role and their actual invitation: a relevant answer, artifact review, scheduled call, offer input, or decision date. For an invited delay, preserve its exact scope and timing; a vague “busy” is not an invitation to follow up. For a decline or no-fit, close cleanly and preserve the stated reason as evidence. An objection to contact or unsubscribe stops persuasion and promotional follow-ups. Recheck channel and purpose boundaries when moving beyond the request, especially across DE/US; a reply does not license unrelated promotions or a channel switch.
   - Intro offers and yeses are the top priority; draft them the same day. An intro offer: accept within 24 h; when later, one short apology clause, no explanation; the target may be the organisation the connector named (the connector picks the person); blurb per the Intro kit; no second ask; respect any condition the connector attached (for example only if direct outreach fails).
   - A yes or interest: the asset is the share kit (`knowledge/strategies/follow-up.md`, `## After a yes: share kit within 24 hours`) sent within 24 h, or one concrete dated next step; for creators include the written no-strings sentence.
7. Draft exactly one channel-native reply per `knowledge/foundations/human-writing.md` (`## Draft-first and gap markers`) or decide not to reply. External delivery remains manual.
8. After founder confirmation, preserve the reply or no-reply decision as an interaction record and resolve only the selected action. Route a booked conversation to a contact-referenced scheduled-call action, an offer request to a proposal action, and substantive new evidence to an interaction-debrief action. Every new action must reference that interaction, state its concrete reason and due date, and create no generic cadence. Create an `inbound_reply` action, or keep the selected one open, only if the confirmed reply stays unsent; it is due today so it stays visible. After a confirmed send: if the other side owes the next step, create `decision_follow_up` +5 working days; if Thomas owes something, create `follow_up` (`touch: commitment`) by the promised date. Update the pipeline board row and count a dated commitment as a lead.
