# Call preparation step

1. Read the active project evidence and the contact's interaction history; classify the call as validation, discovery, demo, or pilot.
2. State one call hypothesis and the evidence that would support or contradict it.
3. Prepare five behavior-first questions grounded in the person's known context.
4. Anticipate one likely resistance and prepare one plain response or calibrated question. For price or scope pressure, define what may change and what outcome boundary must hold.
5. Select one relevant artifact to show and explicitly list what not to demo.
6. Define one concrete close that fits the call type and one acceptable no-fit outcome.
7. List the exact evidence, wording, commitments, and language signals to capture afterward, then set `pending.interaction_debrief: true` after the call occurs.
