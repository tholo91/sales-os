# Call preparation step

1. Read the selected scheduled-call action and `target_ref`, active project evidence, and the contact's interaction history; classify the work as media, partner, funder, connector, validation, discovery, demo, pilot, pricing, or explicit practice. An unnamed or unrecorded counterpart gets `[PRÜFEN: Name, Programm/Ressort, Gesprächspartner:in, Termin]` slots instead of a stop; create the contact record only after the founder names them. For multiplier modes load `knowledge/personas/<persona>.md` (`## Call kit`) and the project's `## Call-Kit` in `workspace/projects/<slug>/personas.md` when present. Practice without a real contact has no pending customer action.
2. State one conversation objective and the target commitment that would resolve it: time, reputation, or money, with a date. Add one acceptable no-fit outcome. Compliments and "schick mal Infos" are not commitments.
3. Prepare only the mode-relevant plan:
   - Media: one-sentence Kernbotschaft, three proof lines each with source and date or `[PRÜFEN: …]`, what cannot be said, and questions on format, deadline, angle, needs, and Autorisierung; target = dated material delivery, interview slot, or routing to a named colleague or Rubrik.
   - Partner: 3–5 behaviour-first questions about their last supporter or audience action, the next dated campaign moment, the decider, and their must-haves; a cannot-do list (no supporter export, no auto-send, no guaranteed effect, no creator script); target = campaign or test date plus owner, or a creator's dated piece in their own format with disclosure agreed.
   - Funder: a pre-call research checklist (Förderzweck, programme, one comparable past grant, eligible applicant or Träger, deadline, Eigenanteil) with `[PRÜFEN: …]`, at most three phone questions, project lines plus Eigenanteil; target = invited Skizze by a date or a named better-fitting programme.
   - Connector: 1–3 named targets, intro mechanics (double opt-in or name-drop permission), the forwardable blurb per `knowledge/channels/personal-network.md` (`## Intro kit`), and a report-back promise; target = one named intro with agreed mechanics.
   - Validation: five behavior-first questions that test one problem, behavior, or willingness-to-change assumption without introducing a hidden pitch.
   - Discovery: five behavior-first questions about the last occurrence, workaround, consequences, decision process, and switching condition.
   - Demo: the buyer context to confirm, one proof moment to show, what not to show, and one decision question.
   - Pilot: use case, owners, duration, success and stop conditions, price, and decision date to agree.
   - Pricing: recorded value evidence, one price and payment shape, likely resistance, scope boundaries, and one decision ask.
   - Practice: one real upcoming moment or assumption-labeled scenario, one buyer turn at a time, concise feedback, and another round only when useful.
   Media, partner, and connector plans add the first-60-seconds contract, a 30-second and a 2-minute pitch (founder story only from recorded material, else `[PRÜFEN: …]`), at most five questions with two starred, and one intro ask by name; funder plans keep three questions in total and use the better-fitting-funder question as the close fallback.
4. Anticipate likely resistance only when supported by the context or the persona `## Call kit`. Prepare one plain answer and one Wie/Was question per objection; for price or scope pressure, define what may change and what outcome boundary must hold.
5. Select one demo moment or artifact when the mode needs proof and explicitly list what not to show. For journalists and funders use a neutral prepared example and never ask their political concern; for partners and connectors use their topic. Test it beforehand and keep a screenshot fallback.
6. Define one concrete close that fits the mode and one acceptable no-fit outcome. Ask the other person to state the next step as when and where in their own words, and use that sentence verbatim in the recap. Then, except in funder calls (the better-fitting-funder fallback replaces both), ask the own-words question ("Wie würdest du das in einem Satz erklären?") and "Wen sollte ich außerdem fragen?" for one named person. Do not force a call booking, offer, or sale when clarification or a clean no is the honest result.
7. List the exact evidence, wording, commitments, objections, and language signals to capture afterward. No recording or AI notetaker without prior informed consent. After the founder confirms a real completed or cancelled call, preserve the outcome as an interaction record and resolve only its scheduled-call action. When the call occurred, create an immediate contact-referenced `interaction_debrief` pending action with that interaction as `source_interaction_ref`, a concrete reason, and a due date; a rehearsal never changes customer state or evidence. Then create one action per commitment per `knowledge/strategies/follow-up.md` (`## Commitments become actions`) and a `follow_up` with `touch: RC` due the call date +1 when the recap was not yet sent; deliver promised assets in their format the same day (fact sheet, test link, prefilled page, blurb).
8. Return one next action, the ready-to-use brief with the RC recap draft or the current practice turn, one manual founder step, and the exact result to bring back.
