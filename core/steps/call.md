# Call preparation step

1. Classify the call as validation, discovery, demo, or pilot.
2. State the call hypothesis in one sentence.
3. Prepare five behaviour-first questions.
4. Anticipate one likely resistance and prepare one label, mirror, or calibrated question. For price or scope pressure, define what may change and preserve the outcome boundary.
5. Select one relevant artifact to show and explicitly list what not to demo.
6. Define one concrete close and the evidence to capture afterward.
