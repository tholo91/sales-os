# Call preparation step

1. Read the selected scheduled-call action and `target_ref`, active project evidence, and the contact's interaction history; classify the work as validation, discovery, demo, pilot, pricing, or explicit practice. Practice without a real contact has no pending customer action.
2. State one conversation objective and the evidence or commitment that would support, contradict, or resolve it.
3. Prepare only the mode-relevant plan:
   - Validation: five behavior-first questions that test one problem, behavior, or willingness-to-change assumption without introducing a hidden pitch.
   - Discovery: five behavior-first questions about the last occurrence, workaround, consequences, decision process, and switching condition.
   - Demo: the buyer context to confirm, one proof moment to show, what not to show, and one decision question.
   - Pilot: use case, owners, duration, success and stop conditions, price, and decision date to agree.
   - Pricing: recorded value evidence, one price and payment shape, likely resistance, scope boundaries, and one decision ask.
   - Practice: one real upcoming moment or assumption-labeled scenario, one buyer turn at a time, concise feedback, and another round only when useful.
4. Anticipate one likely resistance only when supported by the context. Prepare one plain response or calibrated question; for price or scope pressure, define what may change and what outcome boundary must hold.
5. Select one relevant artifact to show when the mode needs proof and explicitly list what not to show.
6. Define one concrete close that fits the mode and one acceptable no-fit outcome. Do not force a call booking, offer, or sale when clarification or a clean no is the honest result.
7. List the exact evidence, wording, commitments, objections, and language signals to capture afterward. After the founder confirms a real completed or cancelled call, preserve the outcome as an interaction record and resolve only its scheduled-call action. When the call occurred, create an immediate contact-referenced `interaction_debrief` pending action with that interaction as `source_interaction_ref`, a concrete reason, and a due date; a rehearsal never changes customer state or evidence.
8. Return one next action, the ready-to-use plan or current practice turn, one manual founder step, and the exact result to bring back.
