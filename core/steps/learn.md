# Learning step

1. Capture what the person actually said or did without polishing it into a success story.
2. Separate observed or carefully paraphrased facts, interpretations, decisions, and open questions.
3. Record language that earned a reply, caused confusion, or was rejected, with channel and context. Keep it as an anecdote unless repeated evidence supports a broader rule. Update the recipient-context check only from actual new evidence; do not turn one creator's concern into a rule about all creators or one US reply into a cultural fact.
4. Update only affected evidence, ICP, validation, experiment, and contact records. Preserve prior claims and dates.
5. Record commitments, a real follow-up date when one exists, and the next falsifiable test with its prediction and decision rule. For outreach review, group comparable confirmed manual attempts by project/purpose, role, relationship, channel, market, and ask. Report counts and a common observation window; separate substantive replies, declines/objections, and completed agreed next steps. Unknown delivery remains unknown. Treat small or mixed samples as directional learning, never promised uplift; change one material variable in the next test. Record persona, angle, ask-ladder step, give-first asset, touch (`T1`, `F1`, `F2`, `commitment`, `RC`), and outcome (keine Antwort, Absage, Rückfrage, Gespräch, Lead, Intro) in the interaction and the pipeline board row. A lead is a dated commitment (call booked, test or campaign date, share date, intro sent, material requested by a journalist, invited Skizze), never a reply.
6. Resolve only the selected `interaction_debrief` action. Turn every row of the interaction's `## Commitments` section into one contact-referenced action per `knowledge/strategies/follow-up.md` (`## Commitments become actions`), with that interaction as `source_interaction_ref`, a real reason, and a due date: Thomas's promises become `follow_up` (`touch: commitment`), the other side's offers or promises become `decision_follow_up`, an agreed call becomes `scheduled_call`, and a reply drafted but not confirmed as sent becomes `inbound_reply` due today. Permission to contact a named third person becomes a Tier-1 row on the pipeline board, not an action; create a minimal contact record from `templates/contact.md` only with facts from the interaction. Create proposal actions only when the interaction supports them.
7. Route through `core/steps/route.md` before recommending product work or more outreach.
