# Engagement step

1. Collect what exists of the learning goal, `target_ref`, `draft_mode`, the persona (`media-podcasts`, `creators-influencers`, `ngos-civic-orgs`, `funders-foundations`, `buyers-users`, `connector`, `other`), and the language-evidence packet (target wording, public context, rules, observed tone). `connector` and `other` have no persona file: `connector` uses the intro kit in `knowledge/channels/personal-network.md`; `other` uses the Drafting card in `knowledge/strategies/first-touch.md` alone. Route `draft_mode: reddit_dm` to the dedicated Reddit DM workflow; this general engagement step handles `draft_mode: outreach`.
2. For a named person or organisation, first take the bounded public-context look in `knowledge/foundations/human-writing.md` (Draft-first rule 2) when web or read-only browser tools are available; its strongest dated item is the opener and the why-now. If an evidence item is still missing, draft from verified role, outlet, or organisation facts and recorded project facts, and mark each gap per `knowledge/foundations/human-writing.md` (Draft-first and gap markers). A placeholder-only target gets a role-based skeleton. The missing check becomes the founder's manual step, never a reason to stop.
3. Load the references listed in `.agents/skills/draft-outreach/SKILL.md`. For `buyers-users`, take the ask ladder and give-first asset from the project's `icp.md` and `positioning.md`. For a journalist or editorial pitch, also read `knowledge/strategies/editorial-pitch.md`; do not generalize its headline test to unrelated sales emails.
4. Apply drafting evidence in this order: verified target context, the founder's matching channel voice, community or platform norms, the individual's formality, then broad market hypotheses.
5. Run the inbox filter: why this person, why now, which recorded proof is usable, and whether one easy reply invitation tests the learning goal. Use the five-field recipient-context check to choose the ask: actual role and capability, real relationship, possible recipient benefit, possible burden or risk, and smallest fitting next move. Keep inferred benefits and concerns internal and labeled; do not tell a recipient what they must want or fear. Distinguish civic support, editorial interest, research dialogue, customer discovery, and paid deliverables. Check purpose, sender and recipient jurisdictions, language, and the matching market/channel rules without treating US recipients as an exemption from sender obligations. Time the message to the recipient's action moment (a dated hearing, campaign day, vote, episode, or a Reel whose comments ask what to do); apply the Drafting card. A connection request carries no ask; a first DM ends with exactly one ask from the ask ladder in `knowledge/strategies/first-touch.md` and the persona's `## Ask ladder`.
6. Preserve the founder's explicit desired outcome when choosing the ask. With an established, recipient-specific reason, a requested conversation may be invited directly; do not substitute an abstract validation question. One invitation may offer an answer by message as an alternative, but do not stack requests for a call, testing, sharing, and introductions. Default ask = permission to send the persona's give-first asset or a short situation question; offer only an existing asset or mark `[PRÜFEN: Asset gebaut?]`; for organisations a one-line referral fallback may close the same ask. Run a conversation-state check: what did the person actually say or do, what one fact is missing, and whether the next move is acknowledge, clarify, offer value, request permission, or agree a next step.
7. Remove claims or context that cannot be traced to project or target evidence, or mark them `[PRÜFEN: …]`. Keep at most one ask; reduce it until it is easy to answer or decline. Do not disguise a request for a meeting, introduction, share, or substantial feedback as a micro-ask. After a substantive reply, address that reply before proposing a larger next step.
8. Draft in the channel-native shape. Do not reuse a greeting, sign-off, disclosure, or CTA pattern across channels. An email draft includes both a subject and body. For a journalist pitch, test whether the subject truthfully expresses the publishable news angle, then keep the body to recipient relevance, news and proof, founder relevance, and one CTA.
9. Remove generic compliments, unnecessary founder or product explanation, automatic “no pressure” endings, and campaign filler.
10. Check length and proof against `knowledge/strategies/first-touch.md` (`## Drafting card`); proof only from `## Gemeinsame Belegzeilen` in its exact wording.
11. Return one draft first, then one short rationale, then `Vor dem Senden prüfen:` (max three) when markers are open; `Risk:` only for a real factual, cultural, platform, legal, or relationship risk. For a LinkedIn first message, apply the first-message coaching in `knowledge/strategies/first-touch.md`: when correcting a premature ask, use that rationale for the single concrete nudge. Do not add another output block.
12. Create an alternative only when explicitly requested. Stop for human review. Set the lane to `awaiting_manual_send` only after the founder accepts the draft for manual action and only when this target is the current lane's `target_ref`; an explicit draft for another person leaves `current_outreach` unchanged.
