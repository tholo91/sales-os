# Engagement step

1. Confirm the learning goal, a real `target_ref`, the selected `draft_mode`, and a complete language-evidence packet: target wording, public context, rules, and observed tone. Route `draft_mode: reddit_dm` to the dedicated Reddit DM workflow; this general engagement step handles `draft_mode: outreach`.
2. If the target is a placeholder or a material evidence item is missing, stop and route to sourcing. Do not polish a guess.
3. Read the active project evidence, `workspace/voice.md` when present, `knowledge/foundations/human-writing.md`, `knowledge/foundations/conversation-principles.md`, and only the matching channel, strategy, and market references. For a journalist or editorial pitch, also read `knowledge/strategies/editorial-pitch.md`; do not generalize its headline test to unrelated sales emails.
4. Apply drafting evidence in this order: verified target context, the founder's matching channel voice, community or platform norms, the individual's formality, then broad market hypotheses.
5. Run the inbox filter: why this person, why now, which recorded proof is usable, and what single easy ask tests the learning goal.
6. Run a conversation-state check: what did the person actually say or do, what one fact is missing, and whether the next move is acknowledge, clarify, offer value, request permission, or agree a next step.
7. Remove claims or context that cannot be traced to project or target evidence. Reduce the ask until it is easy to answer or decline.
8. Draft in the channel-native shape. Do not reuse a greeting, sign-off, disclosure, or CTA pattern across channels. An email draft includes both a subject and body. For a journalist pitch, test whether the subject truthfully expresses the publishable news angle, then keep the body to recipient relevance, news and proof, founder relevance, and one CTA.
9. Remove generic compliments, unnecessary founder or product explanation, automatic “no pressure” endings, and campaign filler.
10. Return one draft first, followed by one short rationale. Add a risk note only for a real factual, cultural, platform, legal, or relationship risk.
11. Create an alternative only when explicitly requested. Stop for human review and set the lane to `awaiting_manual_send` only after the draft is accepted for manual action.
