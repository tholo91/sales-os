# Follow-up step

1. Read the contact and interaction history, including the register (du/Sie), greeting and exact ask of T1, the last ask, promises, prior refusal, language that earned a reply, and channel norms. Load `knowledge/strategies/recipient-context.md` (`## Five-field check` only), `knowledge/strategies/follow-up.md` (touch plan), `knowledge/personas/<persona>.md` (`## Touch plan`, `## Give-first asset`) plus `## Gemeinsame Belegzeilen` and `## <persona>` from `workspace/projects/<slug>/personas.md` when present (without a project section, the persona file alone), the matching channel file, and market files (`knowledge/markets/germany-eu.md`, `us-international.md`, `cross-border.md`) only for email or cross-border. Reuse the recorded role, relationship, possible benefit and barrier, and ask burden; update only from new evidence. Do not require a new questionnaire when the facts are present. Before drafting F1, take the bounded public-context look per `knowledge/foundations/human-writing.md` (Draft-first rule 2) when web or read-only browser tools are available.
2. Classify the trigger: a due touch-plan action (`F1`, `F2`, `commitment`, `RC`), promised delivery, invited later contact, new relevant context, or the founder asking about a named contact. A founder request may create the follow-up decision without a pre-existing pending action; use the last recorded interaction as the source. When the action reason says completion is unknown, open with one clause "Schon erledigt?" and still draft. Silence alone, connection acceptance, opens, and clicks do not establish interest, rejection, or permission. A clear refusal or objection to contact stops persuasion and promotional follow-ups; preserve it as evidence.
3. Choose exactly one outcome: F1 value nudge, F2 park message, deliver, thank, request a referral, public comment, defer to an invited date, or stop. Defaults: after T1 with no reply, F1 with one new recipient-relevant element (below); after F1 with no reply, F2; after F2, stop and engage only publicly. Apply stricter persona or channel overrides from the touch plan (one follow-up for journalists, no DM into an unaccepted Instagram request, Reddit 0–1). Overdue F1 (T1 older than the channel's F1 plus F2 window, about 21 working days on LinkedIn): choose F1 only with a real new reason (new post, date, fact), else park; weigh park explicitly. F1 must carry one new recipient-relevant element (their new post, date, or fact, from the look or recorded facts, or an existing asset tied to it); when none exists, recommend F2 park instead of a generic nudge; a custom asset counts against the weekly budget of at most 3 top targets (`core/steps/pipeline.md`), so other contacts without an asset get a new fact or park. Unaccepted Instagram request: `Kommentar` (one genuine public comment) or the bio's named inquiry email once, never a second DM. Unknown channel: the contact's `primary_channel`, else one check item. A missed commitment by the recipient may justify one gentle reminder; it is not a debt. Do not move channels to bypass silence or refusal.
4. If contacting, draft per `knowledge/foundations/human-writing.md` (`## Draft-first and gap markers`) with the existing relationship language. A missing asset becomes `[PRÜFEN: Asset gebaut?]`; only when no look was possible does a missing new reason become `[PRÜFEN: neuer Anlass, z. B. aktueller Post]`. Never restart the product pitch or combine delivery with a new meeting, share, or introduction request. Recheck DE/US/cross-border channel and purpose rules when context changes; nationality and a prior reply are not blanket permission.
5. Return the decision first per the `handle-follow-up` output contract (`Touch: F1 | F2 | Lieferung | Dank | Empfehlung | Kommentar | Später | Stopp — <one sentence>`), then the draft, then `Vor dem Senden prüfen:` when markers are open. The sales-copilot wrapper takes precedence when invoked through it.
6. After founder confirmation of the send or the defer/stop decision, resolve only the selected action; never mark a draft as sent. Record the touch as an interaction. Create the next action from the touch plan (F1 sent → `follow_up` with `touch: F2`, due +10 working days, unless the channel row or persona override has no F2; F2 sent → none) and any new commitment per `## Commitments become actions` in `knowledge/strategies/follow-up.md`, each referencing the interaction with a concrete reason and due date. `Kommentar` creates no private follow-up action; the board row records it. Update the pipeline board row (`Nächster Touch (Datum)`, `Ergebnis`).
7. Never create cadence beyond the touch plan. A stop decision closes the board row as `geparkt` with the reason.
