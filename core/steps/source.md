# Conversation sourcing step

1. Read the learning goal, ICP, evidence, `knowledge/foundations/audience-language.md`, `knowledge/strategies/recipient-context.md`, channel-choice guidance, the persona file's `## Where to find them` (`knowledge/personas/<persona>.md`), the board in `workspace/projects/<slug>/pipeline.md` (`core/steps/pipeline.md`), and only the relevant channel and market files. If the board already has an `offen` row without a first touch, select it before searching.
2. Translate the problem into phrases people use before they know the product. Keep observed wording separate from search hypotheses.
3. Select a channel from target behavior and existing relationship paths, not founder convenience.
4. Search current public sources or the founder's real network without scraping, bulk enrichment, or copying private material. Work in tier order and record the tier and signal date:
   - Tier 1, warm: open commitments, offered intros, people who replied or named the founder, permissions to name someone, and existing users or feedback-givers who opted in to contact (for BnB: letter writers or reviewers who left a contact route).
   - Tier 2, fresh public signal: people who commented or reposted the founder's posts in their own words (never likers only), or acted publicly on the topic this week (a call to contact politicians, coverage of a decision in their area, a campaign moment).
   - Tier 3, role-based cold: the persona file's search recipes, narrowed to one region, one topic and the last 14 days.
5. For every candidate, verify identity or permalink, recency, relevance, and whether the person can answer the learning question. Build the five-field recipient-context check from existing evidence: role and actual capability, relationship, possible benefit, possible burden, and smallest appropriate ask. Label benefit and burden as hypotheses unless supported by the person's words; reject unsupported access or authority. Record purpose, sender/recipient jurisdiction, and language separately from those fields. A known role or accepted connection is not interest or channel permission.
6. Build a language-evidence packet with current target wording, discussion context, community or platform rule check, and observed tone. Label missing parts. The packet is an internal checklist: each missing item becomes a `[SIGNAL: …]` or `[PRÜFEN: …]` note for the draft (`knowledge/foundations/human-writing.md`, `## Draft-first and gap markers`).
7. Reject only candidates whose identity or relevance cannot be verified, or who refused or opted out. Thin language evidence is not a reason to reject.
8. Return at most five opportunities, or in batch mode up to the open batch slots (max 10) in tier order, with one persona and one angle for the cold part. For each, include one observed language signal or a marked gap and one culture or rule check.
9. Recommend one opportunity and one manual next move. When selected, preserve the recipient context and its evidence in the private contact record, store a stable `target_ref`, set the lane to `needs_draft`, and set `draft_mode: reddit_dm` only when the verified Reddit context explicitly supports private contact; otherwise use `draft_mode: outreach`. Missing motive evidence may remain a labeled hypothesis; missing material identity or contact permission must be researched rather than guessed; missing language evidence becomes a marked gap for the draft. In batch mode, write the rows to the pipeline board (`Ergebnis: offen`) and, if the lane is `needs_target`, select the first row as above.
