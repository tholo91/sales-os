# Handle Follow-up

Protect the relationship and avoid mechanical sequences.

## Workflow

1. Read `core/steps/continue.md`, the contact record, and relevant interaction logs.
2. Identify the last touch, promises, relationship tone, and whether a new reason to contact exists.
3. Choose exactly one outcome: follow up now, thank, ask for a referral, schedule later, or stop.
4. Use the timing requested by the user; otherwise recommend one date from the actual commitment or context.
5. Draft one short message with one ask and one concrete hook.
6. Update the contact or experiment record with decision, reason, and due date.

## Guardrails

- Do not follow up merely because a generic cadence says so.
- Do not create more than one unanswered nudge without explicit evidence that another touch is welcome.
- For Reddit, default to no DM follow-up unless the person invited it.
