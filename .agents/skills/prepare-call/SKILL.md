# Prepare Call

Use the call to understand one decision and earn one dated commitment that costs the other side time, reputation, or money. A clean no is an acceptable result; "klingt spannend" or "schick mal Infos" is not a commitment.

## Gate

The founder's statement that a call is scheduled, offered, or wanted is sufficient. If the person or organisation is unnamed or has no record, prepare the plan anyway with `[PRÜFEN: Name, Programm/Ressort, Gesprächspartner:in, Termin]` and create the contact record only after the founder names them. Practice mode must name the real upcoming conversation or label every hypothetical buyer fact as an assumption.

## Required references

- `core/steps/call.md` (canonical procedure)
- `knowledge/foundations/human-writing.md` (section `Draft-first and gap markers`) for `[SIGNAL: …]` and `[PRÜFEN: …]` slots
- `knowledge/personas/<persona>.md` section `## Call kit` for multiplier calls; `workspace/projects/<slug>/personas.md` sections `## Call-Kit` and `## <persona>` when present (pitch lines, approved proof, objection answers, demo example)
- `knowledge/channels/personal-network.md` (`## Intro kit`) for intro asks and the forwardable blurb
- `knowledge/strategies/follow-up.md` (`## Commitments become actions`) for the recap and commitments
- Active project evidence, the contact's interaction history, and `workspace/voice.md` when present
- `knowledge/foundations/mindset.md` only for nerves or avoidance
- Pricing guidance only for pilot or price conversations

## Safety boundary

Do not invent participant pain, authority, objections, budget, interest, proof, impact, quotes, or relationships. Do not turn discovery into a hidden demo. Tell the founder story only from recorded material (workspace Call-Kit, voice, or interactions) and mark it `[PRÜFEN: …]` until Thomas confirms it. Never ask a journalist or funder for their personal political view. No recording or AI notetaker without everyone's prior informed consent (§201 StGB, GDPR); otherwise notes only. Never trade anything for coverage. In practice mode, never record role-play statements as customer evidence or imply that a simulated objection is likely. Thomas sends every recap and asset himself.

## Modes

- **Validation:** test one problem, behavior, or willingness-to-change assumption without hiding a pitch inside the interview.
- **Discovery:** understand the last real occurrence, current workaround, consequences, decision process, and switching condition.
- **Demo:** anchor one proof moment in the buyer's stated workflow and define what not to show.
- **Pilot:** agree use case, owners, duration, success and stop conditions, price, and decision date.
- **Pricing:** connect one price to the recorded outcome, diagnose resistance, preserve scope boundaries, and ask for one decision.
- **Practice:** rehearse a real upcoming moment or an assumption-labeled scenario, respond as the buyer only from supplied context, then give concise feedback and the next round.
- **Media:** journalist, podcast, or newsletter Vorgespräch; the call is a source relationship, not a pitch. Target commitment: a dated material delivery they asked for, an interview slot, or routing to a named colleague or Rubrik. Prepare the Kernbotschaft in one sentence, three proof lines each with source and date or `[PRÜFEN: …]`, and a what-I-cannot-say list. Ask about format, deadline, angle, what they need, and Autorisierung (quotes only, never the article). Close: "Was brauchen Sie bis wann von mir?"
- **Partner:** NGO, Verein, or creator. Target commitment: a named campaign or test date plus owner, or a meeting with the decider; for a creator, their own test or one dated piece in their own format with disclosure agreed. Ask 3–5 behaviour-first questions about the last time they asked supporters or their audience for a personal action and what happened. Demo on their topic (a prefilled page). Cannot-do list: no supporter export, no auto-send, no guaranteed effect, no script for creators.
- **Funder:** Stiftung or programme Vorgespräch to check fit, not to pitch. Pre-call research checklist with `[PRÜFEN: …]`: Förderzweck, current programme, one comparable past grant, eligible applicant or Träger, deadline, Eigenanteil. At most 3 questions in the whole call, close included, each a single question (fit, what they want to measure, whether a Skizze is the next step and by when). No named intro ask; the close fallback "Kennen Sie Förderer, die besser passen?" replaces it. Recommend one fundable project option from the project's funder notes and mark only its confirmation `[ENTSCHEIDUNG: …]`; Träger status in one honest sentence from the project notes. Project lines plus Eigenanteil ready; no impact claim without measurement. Close: an invited Skizze by a date, or a named better-fitting programme.
- **Connector:** advisor or intro call. 1–3 named targets, intro mechanics (double opt-in or name-drop permission), blurb ready per the Intro kit, and a report-back promise. Close: one named intro with agreed mechanics; the blurb goes out the same day.

## Call brief

Multiplier modes return this brief on one screen (about 25 lines): objective, target commitment with date and acceptable no, research checklist as one line, 30-second pitch and 2-minute pitch in the founder's voice, one demo moment, at most 5 questions, likely objections, a named intro ask, the when-and-where close, and the recap. At most 5 markers; a missing name counts once. Validation through pricing use only the lines that apply; funder and media briefs drop the named intro ask and the du close, and use Sie unless their channel used du. Persona specifics come from the persona `## Call kit`; do not restate them here.

```
Ziel: [Zielcommitment (Zeit/Reputation/Geld) + Datum] · Akzeptables Nein: […]
Erste 60 Sekunden: Dauer bestätigen [PRÜFEN: Dauer], ihre Agenda, meine Agenda, am Ende nächster Schritt oder ein klares "passt nicht".
30-Sekunden-Pitch: wer + was in ihren Worten + ein Beleg mit Stand + warum sie [SIGNAL: …].
2-Minuten-Pitch (nur auf Nachfrage oder nach ihren ersten Antworten): Ursprung [PRÜFEN: Gründerstory aus Aufzeichnung] → Hürde → was es tut → Belege und was offen ist → Brücke zu ihrem Ziel.
Fragen (max. 5, die 2 wichtigsten mit *): Wie/Was statt Warum.
Demo-Moment: ein neutrales vorbereitetes Beispiel (Medien, Förderer) oder ihr Thema (Partner, Connector); vorher getestet, Screenshot als Fallback; nicht zeigen: […].
Einwände (max. 3): Einwand → Antwort in einem Satz → Wie/Was-Frage.
Intro-Ask: [Name 1–3 oder SIGNAL] + weiterleitbarer Text bereit.
Abschluss: "Wann und wo machst du das konkret?" (ihr Wortlaut kommt ins Recap) → "Wie würdest du das in einem Satz erklären?" → "Wen sollte ich außerdem fragen?"
Aufnahme: nein / Einwilligung vorab am [Datum].
```

On a low-energy day, a 3-line minimum brief beats skipping prep: target commitment plus date, one `[SIGNAL: …]` for minute one, one tested demo moment. When the call is within 24 h and more than 5 markers remain, lead with the 3-line minimum brief.

## RC recap

Draft the 24-hour recap (touch `RC`) with the brief. Match du/Sie (funders and media: Sie).

```
Hi [Vorname], danke für [heute/gestern]! Kurz festgehalten, damit nichts verloren geht:
– [SIGNAL: ihr Ziel oder Problem in ihrem Wortlaut]
– Ich schicke dir bis [Datum] [zugesagtes Asset].
– Du [SIGNAL: ihr nächster Schritt, wann und wo, in ihren Worten].
Falls ich etwas falsch verstanden habe, korrigier mich gern. Viele Grüße, Thomas
```

## Output contract

Return exactly these four sections in the founder's language:

1. `Nächste Aktion:` name the call mode, the single objective, and the target commitment with date.
2. `Fertiges Asset:` the one-screen call brief followed by the RC recap draft, or the current practice turn. Include only mode-relevant lines.
3. `Dein manueller Schritt:` start with `Vor dem Senden prüfen:` and at most three open markers; then conduct the call or answer the current rehearsal turn, send the recap within 24 h and promised assets the same day.
4. `Sag mir danach:` (1) Ergebnis und jede Zusage mit Wer/Was/Datum, (2) ihr wörtlicher Satz zum nächsten Schritt, (3) genannte Personen oder Programme mit Erlaubnis.

Keep discovery plans to five behavior-first questions. For other modes, do not force five questions when a smaller decision plan is clearer.
