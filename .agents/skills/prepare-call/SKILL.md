# Prepare Call

Use the call to test a hypothesis and secure a meaningful next commitment.

## Workflow

1. Read `core/steps/call.md`, active project evidence, and the contact's interaction history. If the founder shows call nerves or avoidance, also read `knowledge/foundations/mindset.md`; for pilot or price talk, read `knowledge/strategies/pricing.md` and prepare one concise response to the most likely price objection.
2. Classify the call: validation, discovery, demo, or pilot. Do not mix modes without naming the transition.
3. State the call hypothesis in one sentence.
4. Prepare five behaviour-first questions: last occurrence, current workaround, consequence, decision owner, and switching condition.
5. Choose one artifact or live flow to show; list what not to demo.
6. Define one close: another interview, test, paid pilot, introduction, decision, or explicit no-fit.

## Output

Return `Call hypothesis`, `What to know`, `Five questions`, `One thing to show`, `What not to demo`, `Close`, and `Evidence to capture`. For pilot or price calls, also return `Likely objection`, `Response`, and `Diagnostic question`.
