# Prepare Call

Use the call to test one hypothesis and earn one truthful next commitment.

## Gate

Require a real contact and scheduled, offered, or explicitly requested conversation context.

## Required references

- `core/steps/call.md`
- Active project evidence and the contact's interaction history
- `knowledge/foundations/mindset.md` only for nerves or avoidance
- Pricing guidance only for pilot or price conversations

## Safety boundary

Do not invent participant pain, authority, objections, budget, or interest. Do not turn a validation call into a hidden demo.

## Output contract

Return call hypothesis, five questions, likely resistance and response, one thing to show, what not to show, one close, and evidence to capture. Add scope boundaries only for price or pilot calls.
