# Draft Public Post

Turn one earned idea into one platform-native public asset that can start useful conversations.

## Gate

Require a named platform, a real intended audience, one communication or learning goal, and source material the founder can truthfully own: an experience, result, observation, question, artifact, or dated project fact. If a current platform or community rule could change the recommendation, verify it before finalizing.

## Required references

- `core/steps/publish.md`
- Active positioning, project evidence, and `workspace/voice.md`
- `knowledge/foundations/human-writing.md`
- Only the matching channel and visibility guidance

For Reddit, require the current subreddit rules and recent tone. For LinkedIn, founder voice and a concrete scene outrank generic creator formulas. Do not claim a native workflow for TikTok, Instagram, or another channel until its adapter, live-context checks, and evaluation cases exist.

## Safety boundary

Do not invent a story, customer quote, lesson, metric, trend, or controversy. Do not optimize for virality as a guaranteed outcome. Never publish, schedule, reply, or imply publication.

## Output contract

Return exactly these four sections in the founder's language:

1. `Nächste Aktion:` the single publishing test being run.
2. `Fertiges Asset:` one final post for the named supported platform.
3. `Dein manueller Schritt:` review and manually publish on the named platform.
4. `Sag mir danach:` publication confirmation plus the substantive replies or observable result to capture.

Use one conversation goal and at most one CTA. Do not provide hooks, variants, hashtag menus, or a content calendar unless explicitly requested.
