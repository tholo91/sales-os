# Draft Public Post

Turn one earned idea into one platform-native public asset that can start useful conversations.

## Gate

Recover a named platform, a real intended audience, one communication or learning goal, and source material the founder can truthfully own: an experience, result, observation, question, artifact, or dated project fact. Thin material never blocks the post; draft from recorded facts and mark the gaps. Meaning before hook and the choice of a centre that has material follow `core/steps/publish.md` step 4 (feedback 2026-09-18). If a current platform or community rule could change the recommendation, verify it before finalizing.

For requested ideation, use available project facts and founder observations as starting material. A finished premise or personal story is not required to suggest angles. Label ideas needing confirmation; they are proposals, not evidence or publication-ready drafts.

## Required references

- `core/steps/publish.md`
- Active positioning, project evidence, and `workspace/voice.md`
- knowledge/foundations/human-writing.md (section Draft-first and gap markers)
- `knowledge/foundations/short-punchy-content.md`
- Only the matching channel and visibility guidance
- For LinkedIn posts, `knowledge/strategies/linkedin-public-post.md`
- `workspace/projects/<slug>/hook-library.md` when present (project sentence, premise fallbacks)
- `## Gemeinsame Belegzeilen` of `workspace/projects/<slug>/personas.md` (the only usable numbers, quoted as displayed); no persona pick
- The pipeline board's `## Content-Slots` for the next open post slot

For Reddit, require the current subreddit rules and recent tone. For LinkedIn, the founder's own wording, evidence, and perspective outrank creator formulas. Use an existing draft or notes before asking questions; ask the one focused question in the manual step, never instead of the post. Do not require a full self-written first draft or a personal scene for every post. Instagram Reels and short-video scripts route to draft-reel; other platforms without an adapter get a plain-text draft labelled as untested.

## Shape and split check

Default to a focused asset with one earned point and a clear benefit for the intended reader. Choose the writing approach from the material: experience, result or explanation, reasoned position, genuine open question, or announcement. These are options, not templates or a rotation schedule. Ground the post in one concrete scene, verified result, artifact, or observation; one concrete scene beats a contrast slogan. Every post contains one plain sentence saying what the product does for the reader, even when the product is not the topic. End with a relevant statement, question, or action; a CTA is optional. Cut preamble and product history before adding polish, keeping a short, punchy asset when that serves the idea.

For LinkedIn, pick the archetype and first-line checks from `knowledge/strategies/linkedin-public-post.md`.

When improving a draft, preserve its strongest original lines and make the smallest edits needed for clarity, evidence, and focus. Check that the body delivers the opening's promise and that the ending resolves the same point. Do not rewrite into a creator's persona or append an unrelated promotional PS.

If recent posts are available, check for repeated openings, story arcs, list layouts, and endings. Choose a different suitable treatment when it improves the new idea; do not vary only synonyms or force novelty at the expense of voice and relevance.

## Requested ideation

Return up to three meaningfully different, grounded premises when the founder asks for ideas or angles. Give each a concrete angle, source anchor or missing confirmation, intended reader benefit, and suitable treatment. Preserve the source's actual status; a verified artifact is not necessarily published or approved to share. Recommend one in a short sentence. Do not offer three rewritten hooks as three ideas or require the founder to choose a framework name. Develop the selected premise into one post; proposing ideas does not mark a draft ready.

Before drafting, check whether the source contains two independent stories, lessons, audiences, or desired actions. If yes, recommend splitting it into two focused posts. Each post needs its own opening, payoff, and ending; do not turn them into cosmetic variants. If the founder asks for one post, flag the split recommendation briefly before proceeding.

## Safety boundary

Do not invent a story, customer quote, lesson, metric, trend, or controversy. Do not optimize for virality as a guaranteed outcome. No keyword-comment CTAs ("Kommentiere X und ich schicke dir …"), "Teil das" prompts, or tags for reach. Never publish, schedule, reply, connect, or imply publication.

## Output contract

Return exactly these four sections in the founder's language:

1. `Nächste Aktion:` the single publishing test being run.
2. `Fertiges Asset:` one final post for the named supported platform.
3. `Dein manueller Schritt:` `Vor dem Senden prüfen:` first (open markers, at most three items), then, once they are closed, manually publish on the named platform when you can stay about 60 minutes, and reply publicly to substantive comments first.
4. `Sag mir danach:` publication confirmation plus the result relevant to the post's goal, such as substantive replies, intended audience reached, or link visits; use only available, dated evidence. Also ask for substantive commenters (name plus comment) for the DM bridge in `knowledge/strategies/linkedin-public-post.md`.

If source material is thin, still return one complete post from recorded facts; mark open facts inline, for example `[PRÜFEN: aktuelle Zahl und was sie zählt]`, and put the one question in `Dein manueller Schritt`. Markers fill details, never the core: a premise whose core material does not exist switches per `core/steps/publish.md` step 4. A post with open markers is not ready to publish; say so in plain words.

`Fertiges Asset` may end with exactly one line `Alternativer Einstieg:` built from a different centre that has material, with the body unchanged.

For requested ideation, `Fertiges Asset` is the compact set of grounded ideas with one recommendation; the manual step is choosing an angle or confirming its missing source detail. Keep the same four-section handoff without adding a publishing instruction or changing a draft-ready state.

Use one conversation goal and at most one CTA. Apart from that one line, do not provide hooks, variants, hashtag menus, or a content calendar unless explicitly requested. If two genuinely independent post ideas are present, return two clearly separate assets only after the split has been accepted or requested.
