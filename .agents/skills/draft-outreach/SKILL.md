# Draft Outreach

Write the message the channel and context call for, then stop.

## Gate

Draft immediately for any named person, organization, role at an outlet, or selected discussion. A real `target_ref` plus a complete language-evidence packet (current target wording, public discussion context, community or platform rules, observed tone) makes the draft send-ready; every missing part becomes a marker per `knowledge/foundations/human-writing.md` (section Draft-first and gap markers). When web or read-only browser tools are available, first take the bounded public-context look (same section, rule 2); markers cover only what it could not establish. Never write `dein Post` or imply a current trigger that was neither supplied nor found there with URL and date. Return no draft only in the stop cases listed there.

## Required references

- `core/steps/engage.md`
- `knowledge/foundations/human-writing.md` (section `Draft-first and gap markers`)
- `knowledge/strategies/recipient-context.md` (`## Five-field check` only)
- `knowledge/strategies/first-touch.md` (start with `## Drafting card`)
- `knowledge/personas/<persona>.md`, loaded by section (`## Ask ladder`, `## Give-first asset`, `## Message skeleton`)
- `workspace/projects/<slug>/personas.md` sections `## Gemeinsame Belegzeilen` and `## <persona>` when present
- `knowledge/channels/personal-network.md` (Intro kit) for connector or name-drop touches
- Active project evidence and `workspace/voice.md` when present
- Only the matching channel and strategy references; a market reference only for email or when sender and recipient jurisdictions differ
- `knowledge/foundations/audience-language.md` only for public-thread or community targets

For journalist or editorial outreach, load `knowledge/strategies/editorial-pitch.md`. Do not apply its headline framing to unrelated sales emails.

Apply this order: verified target context, the founder's matching channel voice, community or platform norms, individual formality, then broad market hypotheses.

Before writing, use the five-field recipient-context check to choose the purpose and size of the ask, not merely a personalized opener. Distinguish commercial customer discovery, voluntary civic support, editorial coverage, and a paid creator campaign. Check sender and recipient jurisdictions and actual language separately. Do not translate the same pitch mechanically or infer motives from nationality, reach, or role.

Pick the persona first (`media-podcasts`, `creators-influencers`, `ngos-civic-orgs`, `funders-foundations`, `buyers-users`, `connector`, `other`) and use its `## Ask ladder` and `## Give-first asset`; for `buyers-users` they come from the project's `icp.md` and `positioning.md`. `connector` and `other` have no persona file: `connector` uses the intro kit in `knowledge/channels/personal-network.md`; `other` uses the Drafting card in `knowledge/strategies/first-touch.md` alone. Mark a line-one signal that was neither supplied nor found in the look as `[SIGNAL: …]`; offer only an asset that exists, or mark `[PRÜFEN: Asset gebaut?]`.

Apply `knowledge/strategies/first-touch.md` (`## Drafting card`) for lengths, line order, the close (default: permission to give), and the referral fallback; a persona skeleton overrides it for its persona. For funders with a published sketch route, the sketch itself is the first touch.

Treat the exchange as a state check, not a script: identify what the person actually said, what one fact is still missing, and whether the next move is to acknowledge, clarify, offer relevant value, request permission, or agree a next step. Do not manufacture visible effort, urgency, scarcity, or familiarity.

For LinkedIn first messages, apply the first-message coaching in `knowledge/strategies/first-touch.md`. If the founder's requested ask is premature, provide one usable draft with the smallest fitting next move and use the single `Why:` sentence for a concrete nudge. When the ask fits, draft it without corrective coaching. Do not force a compliment or small-talk sequence.

## Safety boundary

Use only recorded facts and proof, plus dated public items from the look cited with URL. Never invent familiarity, pain, traction, permission, formality, or product fit; an unverified claim stays a `[PRÜFEN: …]` marker. Never send, post, submit, or imply delivery.

## Output contract

Return exactly one draft first. Follow it with `Why:` and one short sentence. Add `Risk:` only when a real risk exists. Use exactly one ask from the ask ladder in a cold first touch; no ask only for a pure thank-you. Preserve an explicitly requested conversation when concrete recipient context establishes relevance, following `core/steps/engage.md`; do not replace it with an abstract validation question. A direct editorial pitch may use its one relevant next step. Generate an alternative only when explicitly requested.

For email, the single draft includes both `Subject:` and the body. For a journalist pitch, the subject states the evidence-backed news angle rather than labeling the project, and the body follows the editorial-pitch reading budget.

When markers are open, add `Vor dem Senden prüfen:` (max three items) after `Why:` and do not call the draft send-ready. For a placeholder-only target return the role-based skeleton titled `Skelett – nicht sendefertig`. Name missing context in plain language; do not expose internal field names such as “language-evidence packet.”
