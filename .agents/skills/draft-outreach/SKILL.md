# Draft Outreach

Write the message the channel and context call for, then stop.

## Gate

Require a problem hypothesis, target-person hypothesis, learning goal, real `target_ref`, and a language-evidence packet containing current target wording, public discussion context, community or platform rules, and observed tone. A placeholder or missing material evidence routes to `$find-conversations`.

## Required references

- `core/steps/engage.md`
- `knowledge/foundations/audience-language.md`
- `knowledge/foundations/conversation-principles.md`
- `knowledge/foundations/human-writing.md`
- Active project evidence and `workspace/voice.md` when present
- Only the matching channel, strategy, and market references

For journalist or editorial outreach, load `knowledge/strategies/editorial-pitch.md`. Do not apply its headline framing to unrelated sales emails.

Apply this order: verified target context, the founder's matching channel voice, community or platform norms, individual formality, then broad market hypotheses.

Treat the exchange as a state check, not a script: identify what the person actually said, what one fact is still missing, and whether the next move is to acknowledge, clarify, offer relevant value, request permission, or agree a next step. Do not manufacture visible effort, urgency, scarcity, or familiarity.

## Safety boundary

Use only recorded facts and proof. Never invent familiarity, pain, traction, permission, formality, or product fit. Never send, post, submit, or imply delivery.

## Output contract

Return exactly one draft first. Follow it with `Why:` and one short sentence. Add `Risk:` only when a real risk exists. Use one ask. Generate an alternative only when explicitly requested.

For email, the single draft includes both `Subject:` and the body. For a journalist pitch, the subject states the evidence-backed news angle rather than labeling the project, and the body follows the editorial-pitch reading budget.

When the gate fails, return `No draft: <missing evidence>` first, then one concrete `$find-conversations` or `$validate-problem` action. Name missing context in plain language; do not expose internal field names such as “language-evidence packet.” Do not soften the refusal with a guessed template.
