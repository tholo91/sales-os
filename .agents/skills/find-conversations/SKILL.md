# Find Conversations

Find evidence-rich opportunities, not names to fill a funnel.

## Gate

Require a concrete problem hypothesis, target-person hypothesis, and learning goal. A target must be a real, verified person, organisation, or permalink. The language-evidence packet is an internal checklist: thin evidence becomes `[SIGNAL: …]` or `[PRÜFEN: …]` notes for the later draft, not a rejection.

## Required references

- `core/steps/source.md`
- `core/steps/pipeline.md` (board and weekly budget)
- `knowledge/personas/<persona>.md`, section `## Where to find them`
- `knowledge/foundations/audience-language.md`
- `knowledge/foundations/channel-choice.md`
- `knowledge/strategies/recipient-context.md`
- The active project's validation, ICP, and evidence artifacts
- Only the matching channel and Germany/EU, US, or cross-border market references

Qualify the person's actual role and ability to help, the real relationship, a recipient benefit hypothesis, a burden or risk hypothesis, and the smallest fitting next move. Keep observed facts separate from hypotheses; neither a job title nor a country establishes motivation. Recover this context from available evidence rather than asking the founder to fill out a questionnaire.

## Safety boundary

No scraping, bulk enrichment, exhaustive-search claims, invented links, automatic DMs, or copied private-community material. If live verification fails, label search paths unverified rather than presenting candidates as real. Contact existing users only through the route they opted in to.

## Output contract

Single mode: return at most five opportunities. For each: target, dated relevance, observed language signal or marked gap, culture or rule check, relationship path, risk, and one human move. Let the recipient-context check determine that move; show only the details that change the selection or ask. Recommend one opportunity first.

Batch mode (weekly start or explicit request): fill the open batch slots, max 10, in tier order (Tier 1 warm and opted-in users, Tier 2 fresh public signal, Tier 3 persona search recipes; definitions in `core/steps/source.md`). Name the one persona and angle for the cold rows once, then one compact table `Ziel | Persona | Tier | Signal (Datum) | Erster Schritt` (comment, connect, DM, email, or intro ask). Recommend the first row and write the rows to the project's `pipeline.md`.
