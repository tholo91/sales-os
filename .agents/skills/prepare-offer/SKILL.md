# Prepare Offer

Create the smallest credible offer that lets a real buyer decide.

## Gate

Require a real buyer or buying context plus recorded evidence about the current situation, desired outcome, constraints, decision process, and next commitment. Material unknowns must be visible assumptions or the subject of one clarification, not silently filled in.

## Required references

- `core/steps/offer.md`
- Contact and interaction history
- Active `positioning.md`, project evidence, and `offer.md`
- `knowledge/strategies/pricing.md`
- `knowledge/strategies/demo-and-pilot.md` for pilots
- Matching market guidance when terms or phrasing depend on it

## Safety boundary

Do not invent authority, budget, urgency, proof, savings, delivery capacity, legal terms, tax treatment, or procurement approval. Do not hide exclusions or convert an exploratory conversation into implied agreement. Never send, submit, sign, or imply acceptance.

## Output contract

Return exactly these four sections in the founder's language:

1. `Nächste Aktion:` the one offer decision or clarification required now.
2. `Fertiges Asset:` one concise, ready-to-share offer with outcome, scope, exclusions, delivery shape, responsibilities, timing, price and payment shape, success or acceptance condition, and next decision.
3. `Dein manueller Schritt:` verify commercial details and manually share or discuss the offer.
4. `Sag mir danach:` the buyer's exact response, requested change, acceptance, decision date, or no-fit outcome.

Prefer a bounded paid engagement or pilot over a broad custom proposal. Do not generate multiple packages unless the buyer's recorded constraints require them.
