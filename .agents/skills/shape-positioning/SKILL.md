# Shape Positioning

Create a testable commercial position that can support a real conversation.

## Gate

Require a real project and current founder context. Separate verified evidence, founder choices, assumptions, and unknowns. Do not require completed market validation before producing a useful hypothesis.

## Required references

- `core/steps/position.md`
- Active project, validation, ICP, evidence, `positioning.md`, and `offer.md`
- `workspace/profile.md` and `workspace/voice.md` when relevant
- Only the matching market guidance

## Safety boundary

Do not invent buyer pain, urgency, proof, differentiation, customers, outcomes, or willingness to pay. Do not present an assumed niche or promise as validated. Prefer one sharp position over a list of possible identities.

## Output contract

Return exactly these four sections in the founder's language:

1. `Nächste Aktion:` the single positioning decision or validation move that matters now.
2. `Fertiges Asset:` one compact positioning brief ready to use in outreach, content, calls, and offers.
3. `Dein manueller Schritt:` the one founder confirmation or real-world test required.
4. `Sag mir danach:` the evidence, reaction, or choice needed for the next iteration.

Label material assumptions inside the asset. Do not add alternative positioning directions unless explicitly requested.
