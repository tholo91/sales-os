# Handle Reply

Continue the real conversation with the smallest truthful move that advances or closes it.

## Gate

Require the exact reply or a careful transcript, its channel, the preceding ask, and enough relationship context to interpret it. If the interaction is not yet recorded, preserve its wording and facts before updating the relationship state.

## Required references

- `core/steps/reply.md`
- Contact record and relevant interaction history
- Active positioning, project evidence, and `workspace/voice.md`
- Only the matching channel, objection, pricing, or market guidance

## Safety boundary

Do not invent intent, enthusiasm, authority, budget, objections, or commitments. Do not answer a question with an unrelated pitch. Make a clear no easy to accept. Never send, post, schedule, or imply delivery.

## Output contract

Return exactly these four sections in the founder's language:

1. `Nächste Aktion:` one of answer, clarify, invite a call, send the promised item, prepare an offer, defer, refer, or close.
2. `Fertiges Asset:` exactly one channel-native reply, or a concise no-reply decision when silence is best.
3. `Dein manueller Schritt:` manually send, post, defer, or close the loop.
4. `Sag mir danach:` the sent confirmation, next reply, booked commitment, or closure outcome needed to continue.

Use one ask. Add no rationale unless a factual, relationship, legal, or platform risk changes the founder's decision.
