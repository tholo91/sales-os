# Handle Reply

Continue the real conversation with the smallest truthful move that advances or closes it.

## Gate

Require the reply text, or the founder's paraphrase marked `[PRÜFEN: genauer Wortlaut]`, its channel, and the preceding ask when recorded. An explicit founder request needs no queued action. If the interaction is not yet recorded, preserve its wording and facts before updating the relationship state.

## Required references

- `core/steps/reply.md`
- `knowledge/strategies/recipient-context.md` (`## Five-field check`)
- Contact record and relevant interaction history
- Active positioning, project evidence, and `workspace/voice.md`
- Only the matching channel, objection, or pricing guidance; market files (`germany-eu.md`, `us-international.md`, `cross-border.md`) only for email or cross-border
- `knowledge/channels/personal-network.md` (`## Intro kit`) for intro offers
- `knowledge/strategies/linkedin-public-post.md` (`## Comment-to-DM bridge`) for public comments

## Safety boundary

Do not invent intent, enthusiasm, authority, budget, objections, or commitments. Do not answer a question with an unrelated pitch. A reply does not justify a larger ask, public endorsement, or another channel; use the person's exact invitation. Make a clear no easy to accept and stop persuasion or promotional follow-ups after an objection to contact. Never send, post, schedule, or imply delivery.

## Output contract

Return exactly these four sections in the founder's language:

1. `Nächste Aktion:` one of answer, clarify, invite a call, send the promised item, prepare an offer, defer, refer, or close.
2. `Fertiges Asset:` exactly one channel-native reply, or a concise no-reply decision when silence is best.
3. `Dein manueller Schritt:` `Vor dem Senden prüfen:` (max three items) when markers are open; then manually send, post, defer, or close the loop.
4. `Sag mir danach:` the sent confirmation, next reply, booked commitment, or closure outcome needed to continue.

A public comment reply is 2–4 sentences, answer first, no question that guesses their motive; DM only after their next reply states a need or interest. Use at most one ask; delivery, thanks, and closure may need none. Add no rationale unless a factual, relationship, legal, or platform risk changes the founder's decision.
