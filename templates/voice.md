# Founder voice

Built from real messages the founder has sent, so drafts sound like the founder and not like a language model. Optional but strongly recommended before drafting outreach. Store the filled version at `workspace/voice.md`.

## Evidence labels

- `confirmed-native`: explicitly confirmed as naturally written by the founder.
- `sent-approved`: sent by the founder, but authorship or editing is not confirmed.
- `ai-assisted-or-unknown`: origin is unclear; do not use it to define default voice.
- `rejected`: explicitly disliked or recognized as unlike the founder.

## Recognition test

A draft passes when it looks like something the founder could type in that exact channel after reading that exact context. Target evidence outranks this file. When evidence is weak, research or write less.
