## Optional outreach review

Use for a real outreach test; record only confirmed manual attempts and dated outcomes. Define the intended useful response and observation window before comparing results. Group by project/purpose, role, actual relationship, channel, market, and ask; keep jurisdiction evidence separate from language. Leave unavailable delivery information unknown.

| Cohort / ask | Observed at / elapsed time | Confirmed manual attempts | Substantive replies | Declines or objections | Completed agreed steps | One next test |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|

Report raw counts before rates and state the denominator; never mix all attempts with known-delivered messages. Outcomes can overlap (a substantive reply can later complete a step). Connection acceptance, opens, and polite acknowledgments do not count as substantive replies. A refusal is useful evidence but not progress toward adoption; an agreed step is not a completed step. Small or mixed samples cannot prove a role, country, or copy effect. Choose one material change rather than increasing volume or persistence from silence.

## Optional public-post review

Use only for an active public-post test. Set the desired result before publishing; enter observations only after confirmed manual publication. Keep private links, wording, and replies in the project's workspace.

| Post reference / published at | Intended audience / main point | Desired result | Observed at / elapsed time | Organic, paid, or unusual amplification | Actual result / available diagnostics | One next change |

Use `not recorded` for missing data. Relevant conversations, resource use, or another project goal take priority over reach; a link visit alone is not proof of use or payment. Compare similar observation windows. A small sample gives a working hypothesis, not a causal performance claim.
