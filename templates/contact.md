## Recipient context for the next message

Recover from current evidence and interactions; keep unknowns visible. This is a compact internal check, not a questionnaire or psychological profile.

- Role and actual capability: what this person does and can decide, assess, publish, or introduce; source and date.
- Actual relationship: no prior exchange, connected only, introduced, substantive exchange, or existing collaboration; interaction reference.
- Possible recipient benefit: a hypothesis tied to their work; mark confirmed preferences separately.
- Possible burden or risk: effort, relevance, reputation, editorial independence, authority, or budget; hypothesis unless stated.
- Smallest fitting ask: one next move proportional to the relationship and purpose, or no ask/stop; reason.

Record the purpose (discovery, civic support, editorial, research, paid work), sender and recipient jurisdictions, recipient language and formality evidence, and channel permission separately. Never infer these from a name, nationality, employer prestige, or connection acceptance. Update only when new evidence changes the next move; historical interactions remain intact.
